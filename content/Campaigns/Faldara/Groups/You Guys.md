**You Guys** is the adventuring party at the center of the Faldara campaign. The group began when [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul Seragon]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin Draper]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] met in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]] while dealing with Paul's stolen mask.

The party has accumulated several names over time, including **SPP3 4eva**, **Socialist Pizza Party of 3**, and, for a while, **SPP3 4eva + Happy**.

## Current Members

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul Seragon]]: Tabaxi monk and Earl of Lakeville.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin Draper]]: Wood elf bard, dancer, and The Twinkler.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]]: High elf Circle of Stars druid and follower of Favaro.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]]: Half-elf Armorer artificer, merchant, and mask maker.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]]: Tortle barbarian rescued inside Tur'Farinil.

## Former Member

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Yhefff-Two Ua|Yhefff-Two Ua]], better known as Jeff, traveled with the group only briefly before dying inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

## Friends, Rivals, and Fellow Travelers

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] began as festival rivals and became recurring allies and fellow travelers. Their shared experiences in Vualio, on the road, and around Arbaroŝirm have tied the two groups together despite plenty of suspicion and irritation along the way.

## Current Situation

In Session 22 the party closed the rift, and in Session 23 Seren Kaŭro verified their victory. He awarded 100 gp to each member. The party returned to Arbaroŝirm, where Fineks agreed to accompany them temporarily on a journey toward Apeiron. The party's official name is **You Guys, You Can't Ask Me for Names**, usually shortened to **You Guys**.
