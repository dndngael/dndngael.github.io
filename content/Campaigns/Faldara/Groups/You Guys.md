# You Guys

The adventuring party at the center of the Faldara campaign. The group began when [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul Seragon]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin Draper]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] met in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]].

The party has accumulated several names over time, including **SPP3 4eva**, **Socialist Pizza Party of 3**, and **SPP3 4eva + Happy**.
