The Masked Ones are the official faith dedicated to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Vual|Vual]], god of masks. Their religious authority is deeply intertwined with the political and social structure of the Kingdom of Vualio.

The clergy guide citizens through religious practice and the system of masks that forms such an important part of Vualian identity.

## Known Hierarchy

- **Decidantoj:** The highest religious and governmental authorities, associated with interpreting Vual's will and assigning societal roles during the Maskovo.
- **Circle of Elders:** Twelve high-ranking clergy who oversee regions and major ceremonies.
- **Umbrals:** Local clergy responsible for temples, daily ritual, and direct spiritual guidance. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] is one such member.
- **Fellowship:** Members training for higher clergy ranks.
- **Devotees:** Temple servants and faithful who have not taken higher vows.

## Masks and Ceremony

The **Maskovo** is a major rite in which citizens move from childhood masks into masks representing social roles. The Masked Ones also play a major role in **La Festo de Maskoj**, the great festival honoring Vual.

Their symbol is a mask divided between light and darkness, reflecting Vual's association with secrets, hidden knowledge, and the boundary between what is seen and unseen.

## Questions Raised by the Campaign

The party has now encountered ancient places and beings that seem to remember a time before Vualio's present mask traditions. What that means for the faith's history remains an open question rather than an established answer.
