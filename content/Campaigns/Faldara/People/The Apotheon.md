# The Apotheon

The Apotheon is a mysterious masked figure who appeared to Paul, Alvin, and 27 in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Vision in the Lazuli Grotto]].

He appeared as a spectral humanoid figure made of dark red-and-black vapor, dressed in a black cloak and wearing a crimson mask with glittering golden markings. Purple eyes were visible behind the mask.

The Apotheon claimed to be imprisoned and lost in darkness. He begged the Dream Weaver to guide people capable of saving him to the place where he had first prayed, and said he had once prayed to **the Spinner** in the heart of a temple of evil.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] later found the name or title Apotheon connected to an ancient hero or warrior.

The party's search for him led from the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]] to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].
