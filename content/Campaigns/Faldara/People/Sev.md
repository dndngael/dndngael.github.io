**Sev** is a man rescued by [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] from a cabin being burned by five hostile spellcasters on the road to Stormwatch Harbor. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] pulled him from the burning building. He died in the ensuing *Fireball* but was brought back with *Revivify* and healing.

## A False Name

Sev first introduced himself as **Reginald Maxwell III**, affecting the manner of a minor noble. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] recognized that he was performing a role. Sev dropped the disguise and gave his real name.

## What Sev Told the Party

Sev claimed to be a private investigator who had worked in Stormwatch Harbor. He said a remarkably tall woman wearing a gray coat, whom he guessed might have Goliath ancestry, hired him to look into a conspiracy. He believes his work brought the spellcasters to his cabin.

He spoke of something called **the Faceless**, a symbol resembling a hooded or masked figure with a void-like face, and strange sayings about seeing without eyes, speaking without mouths, and why people wear masks. He also described the harbor's supposedly burning waters, inaccessible dry docks, and religious tensions.

**Those descriptions are Sev's testimony.** The party has yet to verify his interpretation, his employer's identity, or the reported state of the harbor.

## Assistance and Leads

After his rescue, Sev gave the group the combination to a safe beneath his bed. It contained **120 gp** and a **Potion of Invisibility**, which the party recovered. He also told them that his former office in the **Dry Docks District** holds investigation notes and that his landlady **Gladys** had been paid for the current rental period.

Sev said he did not want to return to Stormwatch Harbor. His whereabouts after leaving the roadside encounter are not established.
