Lurvius is a member and apparent leader of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]]. The party first met his group as rivals during the festival in Vualio City.

He is cautious about Vualio's mask customs, and he was notably uneasy about Paul having gone without his mask. His eye color changes with his emotions, something his companions regard as normal for him.

## Session 21

During the confrontation with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]], Lurvius appeared through a magical doorway alongside Ĵura and Astra while all three were under a strange influence. His eyes were silver, and he attacked the party despite visible hesitation.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] reached him by reminding him of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] and of the people and things that mattered to him. The influence broke, Lurvius's eyes changed from silver to blue, and he thanked Happy for reminding him what mattered.

He then turned against Faylen and helped the party finish the confrontation.

## The Velmian Connection

Inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], the party found an ancient tapestry depicting a grown man who looked strikingly like Lurvius. The inscription named the figure **Velmian** and called him a hero who had been "apotheosized" and was "lost yet unforgotten."

Earlier, the ghost-child [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] had taken one of Happy's glowing objects and brought it to Lurvius because it somehow felt like it belonged there.

What exactly connects the two remains unknown.
