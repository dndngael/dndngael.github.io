**Nedry** is a bespectacled gnome secretary working for Gladys, the landlady of Sev's former rented office.

## Session 28

Nedry helped You Guys gain an audience with Gladys and took an interest in their new **Lheani (and You Guys) Detective Agency**. He offered to send possible clients their way, watch for suspicious visitors, and perhaps meet them for a drink.

He gave directions to the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]] and the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Churning Chapel|Churning Chapel]], and warned that outsiders might be asked to undergo a trial of faith before receiving entry. His knowledge of the city's larger mysteries is not established.
