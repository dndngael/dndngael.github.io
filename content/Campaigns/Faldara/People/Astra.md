Astra is a drow mage and artist who travels with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]]. He has long braided white hair, pale gray-white eyes, and a taste for dangling gold jewelry.

## History with the Party

The party first met Astra during the pre-festival games in Vualio City, then competed against him and the rest of Dawn's Bane in the final challenge through the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]].

Astra has repeatedly helped the party while also making it very clear that he considers their grace, planning, and general competence questionable.

During the journey toward Arbaroŝirm, he saved the group from danger and remained one of the more direct bridges between Dawn's Bane and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]].
