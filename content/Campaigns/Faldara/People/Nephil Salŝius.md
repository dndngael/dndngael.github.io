# Nephil Salŝius

Nephil Salŝius is a blind scholar associated with the Nivela Institute who studies magical resonance and unusual frequencies around [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

Nephil uses a specialized staff to perceive resonance and can often detect things that sighted people cannot. He became the party's answer to the "blind one who sees the way" in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Riddle of Tur'Farinil|Farinia's riddle]].

## Resonance Research

Nephil describes each person's unique natural psychic frequency as a [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul song]]. His research helped connect [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] symptoms to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]].

He also possesses [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]], a material capable of absorbing or suppressing psychic energy.

## Farinia's Codex

Nephil agreed to give the party Silentstone if they helped him recover [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]], an ancient crystal archive believed to store information through psychic frequencies. The party eventually found the Codex inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|Tur'Farinil's hidden library]].
