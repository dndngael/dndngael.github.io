# Donneran

Donneran is a friendly firbolg who tends [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/The Dilapidated Temple|the Dilapidated Temple]] in Arbaroŝirm and follows Favaro.

The party has repeatedly turned to Donneran for spiritual guidance. With his help, they have communed with Favaro about mysteries including [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]], her Moon's Tear, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] worsening condition.

Donneran also grows a plant called Juwanna, which has played a less solemn role in several conversations with the group.
