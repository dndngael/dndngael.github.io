Donneran is a friendly firbolg herbalist who has made his home in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/The Dilapidated Temple|the Dilapidated Temple]] in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]]. He tends an extensive collection of plants in the old ruins and follows [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Favaro|Favaro]].

Donneran is remarkably relaxed about living in a city under regular attack from creatures emerging from Tur'Farinil. His preferred approach to most problems is to tend his garden, stay out of the worst danger, and offer visitors a place to sit down.

## Guidance from Favaro

The party has repeatedly turned to Donneran when they needed divine guidance. Through him they have communed with Favaro about mysteries including [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]], her so-called Moon's Tear, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] worsening condition.

Favaro's answers have often been precise without being particularly straightforward, leaving the party to decide how much meaning to read into the wording.

## Juwanna

Donneran also cultivates a smokable plant called **Juwanna**. It has played a considerably less solemn role in several of the party's visits to the temple.
