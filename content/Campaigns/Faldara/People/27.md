27 is a high elf druid of the Circle of Stars, a tutor, a follower of Favaro, and a firm believer that nature can be understood through mathematics.

## The Search for Zero

27's home island, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]], is in danger. The leader of his order, Archdruid 7, sent him away to seek a prophesied hero named **Zero**, who is believed to be important to saving it.

His nautilus shell and the Fibonacci sequence are personally meaningful, and the idea of "zero" has gained strange echoes elsewhere in the campaign.

## Notable Connections

27's faith in Favaro has repeatedly given the party access to divine guidance through [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]].

During the exploration of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], 27 recognized and investigated ancient imagery, including the tapestry connected to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Dyad War|the Dyad War]].

See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Find Zero|Find Zero]].

## Session 23: Toward Apeiron

When Nephil found a Codex passage about an island associated with Favaro's thumbprint and the sequence 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 27 recognized the pattern from the natural world. He believes [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] may be Zero and welcomed her decision to travel to Apeiron in search of answers for her suppressed soul song. The group plans to travel south to Stormwatch Harbor and sail onward. These theories are not yet confirmed.
