Paul Seragon is a tabaxi monk, noble, and Earl of Lakeville. He was one of the original three members of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]].

## Known History

Paul's family died from sickness, leaving him the only surviving member of his family.

His introduction to the campaign was unusually appropriate for a world obsessed with masks: shortly after meeting Alvin and 27 in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]], Paul's mask was stolen. The group recovered it after investigating during the festival.

During the pre-festival games, Paul won the Dragon Cake eating competition in spectacularly messy fashion and earned the title **The Hurlin' Earl**.

## Current Connections

Paul was the person who first touched the object that triggered the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Vision in the Lazuli Grotto]], and he became closely associated with the mysterious [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Mask Piece|mask fragment]] found there.

In the latest documented events in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], Paul grabbed the strange stone moving toward the rift. A silver thread appeared linking the rift and the stone through him.
