# Umbral Kaz

Umbral Kaz is a gnome associated with the Umbrals of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones|The Masked Ones]]. The party first met him while participating in the Riddles and Rhymes competition during the festival in Vualio City.

After the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Vision in the Lazuli Grotto]], Kaz researched the figure calling himself the Apotheon and had a strange dream or vision of his own.

Kaz identified the chamber in the grotto as an ancient prayer site connected to Narvono. He also identified **the Spinner** mentioned by the Apotheon as Farinia, deity of time and fate.

His research found the name or title **Apotheon** connected to an ancient hero or warrior. Kaz pointed the party toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] as the next place to investigate.
