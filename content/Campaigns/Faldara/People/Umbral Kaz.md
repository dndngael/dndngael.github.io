Umbral Kaz is an elderly gnome priest and member of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones|The Masked Ones]] in the Lowgates district of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]].

The party first met Kaz without realizing his religious rank, when he ran the **Riddles and Rhymes** competition during the pre-festival games. He later helped oversee the final competition in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]].

## The Apotheon Investigation

After the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Vision in the Lazuli Grotto]], Kaz researched the mysterious figure calling himself the [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|Apotheon]] and experienced a strange dream or vision of his own.

Kaz recognized the hidden grotto chamber as an ancient prayer site connected to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Narvono|Narvono]], the Dream Weaver. He also identified **the Spinner** named in the Apotheon's plea as [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]], deity of time and fate.

His research found the name or title **Apotheon** associated with an ancient hero or warrior. Kaz's best lead pointed the party toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]], setting the next major arc of the campaign in motion.
