# Seren Kaŭro

Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro is a leading authority in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] and one of the city's most visible defenders against the creatures emerging from [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

The party first met Seren in the middle of a monster attack, where he demonstrated the ability to create copies of himself while fighting.

## Relationship with the Party

Seren's opinion of the group suffered after a fight with a drunk soldier in the barracks. Paul later apologized and offered help from Lakeville, improving the relationship somewhat.

Seren knows a great deal about the temple and its dangers. As conditions worsened, he eventually declared Tur'Farinil off-limits, forcing the party to navigate the conflict between his authority and their need to continue investigating.
