# Yhefff-Two Ua

Yhefff-Two Ua, usually called **Jeff**, was a human wizard from Vualio introduced to the group by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil Salŝius]] shortly before their expedition into [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

He was the twin brother of Yhefff-Three Ua.

Jeff's adventuring career with the party was tragically brief. Soon after entering Tur'Farinil, he was killed by a chandelier trap. His remains then transformed into a hostile, goo-like creature that the party was forced to fight.

Some of Jeff's remains were collected in one of Alvin's bottles while the group considered whether anything could still be done for him.
