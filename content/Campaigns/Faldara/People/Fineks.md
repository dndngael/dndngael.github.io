Fineks is a tiefling druid and member of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]]. She has short fiery-orange hair, orange eyes, and is unusually short for a tiefling. Her companion Torĉ, pronounced "Torch," resembles a mixture between a cat and an owl.

## The Affliction

In Arbaroŝirm, Fineks became increasingly ill. She experienced paranoia, hallucinations, whispering or buzzing sounds, shadowy figures, and dark veins beneath her skin.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] connected her symptoms to the strange material [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]] and to the way it interfered with what he calls a person's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul song]].

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] eventually embedded [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] into Fineks's mask. The treatment stopped the whispers and shadowy manifestations, but Nephil found that it also muted her soul song almost completely.

Fineks is more stable, but whether the underlying problem has actually been cured remains uncertain.
