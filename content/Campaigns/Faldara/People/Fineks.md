Fineks is a tiefling druid and member of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]]. She has short fiery-orange hair, orange eyes, and is unusually short for a tiefling. Her companion Torĉ, pronounced "Torch," resembles a mixture between a cat and an owl.

## The Affliction

In Arbaroŝirm, Fineks became increasingly ill. She experienced paranoia, hallucinations, whispering or buzzing sounds, shadowy figures, and dark veins beneath her skin.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] connected her symptoms to the strange material [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]] and to the way it interfered with what he calls a person's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul song]].

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] eventually embedded [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] into Fineks's mask. The treatment stopped the whispers and shadowy manifestations, but Nephil found that it also muted her soul song almost completely.

Fineks is more stable, but whether the underlying problem has actually been cured remains uncertain.

See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Cure Fineks's Discord|Cure Fineks's Discord]].

## Session 23: A Journey of Her Own

At [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]]'s workspace, Fineks heard of a passage in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] describing an island associated with Favaro's thumbprint and a numerical pattern. Nephil suggested that visiting the place might help her silenced soul song, although no cure is guaranteed.

Fineks initially doubted [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]]'s declaration that she is the prophesied Zero. She nevertheless chose to pursue the Codex lead and agreed to travel to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]] with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] as a temporary companion, intending to discuss her decision with Lurvius first. She remains a member of Dawn's Bane.

## Session 24: Choosing Her Own Number

Fineks departed [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]] and eventually Apeiron. She challenged the suggestion that her role must be **Zero**, wondering aloud whether it could instead be **infinity**. Neither claim has been verified.

During the roadside encounter at a burning cabin, Fineks used wind magic to clear smoke, rescued an unconscious man, and fought attackers who tried to kill the party. A *Fireball* killed the rescued man, but Fineks brought him back with *Revivify* and healing. The survivor introduced himself as [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]] and provided leads about the harbor. Fineks helped Lheani bury two of the fallen attackers. Her suppressed soul song remains an unresolved concern.

## Session 25: Circle of Embersong

Around the campfire on the road to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], Fineks shared a little of her history before [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]]. She named her former druidic circle, the **Circle of Embersong**, and said she met Lurvius and Ĵura when they were traveling together. Astra joined their company later.

Fineks was reluctant to elaborate on the circle or her past. The reason has not been established.

She also helped entertain a wealthy merchant named **Vuzeevio**, attempting a magical display with fire and wind, and fought alongside the party against dire wolves that attacked their camp. Her soul song remains suppressed, and the journey to Apeiron continues.
