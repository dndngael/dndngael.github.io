Happy is a half-elf Armorer artificer, artisan, traveling merchant, and mask maker who joined [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]] on the road to Arbaroŝirm.

## Memory

Happy has amnesia. His earliest remembered experience is waking naked in the woods roughly twenty years ago, despite being around forty years old.

His missing history has become increasingly difficult to dismiss. Inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], Happy discovered that he could read an ancient language that no one else present could understand.

During a near-death vision, Happy saw a young man who resembled an adult, whole version of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]]. The figure called Happy **"brother."** Happy does not know what that means.
