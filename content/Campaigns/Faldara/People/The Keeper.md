**The Keeper** is the title used by an unseen speaker who warned [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] away from an old forest during the road journey to Stormwatch Harbor.

## What the Party Knows

Some moths bearing deliberately painted purple circles appeared to watch the party. A captured moth, found to carry divination magic, said it worked for the Keeper and led the party toward an ancient forest.

At the forest boundary, an arrow struck the ground near Fineks, and an unseen feminine-sounding voice told the travelers to leave. The speaker claimed to have watched the area for hundreds of years and described herself as a guardian of the boundary, warning that travelers might become lost forever in the forest's paths.

The party departed without trying to cross the boundary. It did not establish the Keeper's identity, appearance, age, or true purpose.
