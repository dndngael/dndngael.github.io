Alvin Draper is a wood elf bard and dancer known on stage as **The Twinkler**. He was one of the original three members of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]].

## Notable Moments

Alvin frequently becomes the group's negotiator, performer, and occasional source of magical improvisation. During the journey to Arbaroŝirm, he performed at the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Mistwind Caravan|Mistwind Caravan]] and acquired his [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Really Cool Cloak|Really Cool Cloak]].

In [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]], Alvin was one of the people invited into [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen's]] confidence. He received a smaller stone connected to her teachings, although touching it directly caused him harm and was accompanied by a strange voice.

## Resonance

Inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], Alvin proved unusually capable of matching and manipulating magical frequencies. During the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/The Rift Crisis|rift crisis]], he successfully tuned himself to the rift's magical frequency. The rift was subsequently closed.

## Session 23

Alvin currently carries the original [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Mask Piece|Lazuli Grotto mask fragment]], which cannot remain attuned to a single wearer indefinitely. Its known rules limit attunement to two consecutive days, followed by a two-day period when the same person cannot attune again. Farinia confirmed the fragment was a piece of the ancient Velmian, the Apotheon.

Like each other member of You Guys, Alvin received **100 gp** from Grand Duke Seren.
