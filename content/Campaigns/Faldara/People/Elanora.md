**Elanora** is an attendant at the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]] who guided You Guys toward the **Triumph of Saint Ludavica**.

## Session 28

Elanora inspected Lheani's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Medallion of Solano|Medallion of Solano]] and revealed that crushing it in one's hand provides the healing effect of a **Greater Healing Potion** to its holder, consuming the medallion.

She described the story of Saint Ludavica, who reportedly calmed a warlord through magic and conversation rather than simply defeating him. When Elanora asked what gods are and where they came from, she appeared to appreciate the group's frank answers. She hinted that the trial requires participants to **extinguish the flames of anger**, possibly by deeds instead of words alone.

She escorted them to the third-floor chamber and opened the door. The trial itself had **not begun** by the end of Session 28.
