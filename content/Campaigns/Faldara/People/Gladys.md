**Gladys** is the businesslike and sometimes exasperated human landlady responsible for [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Sev's Office|Sev's Office]].

## Session 28

Gladys was displeased to learn Sev had offered his premises to other people without telling her. About a week earlier, white-robed visitors had also sought permission to use it, though Gladys found their claim of authorization unconvincing. Once You Guys showed her Sev's familiar hat, she gave them **three keys** and access for the remainder of his paid term.

On **Vuana 26**, Sev's rent covered the space through **Vuana 28**. Gladys quoted **60 gp** for the following month and said they could pay by the **1st**. The party has not yet paid for another month. Her secretary is [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nedry|Nedry]].

The identity and motives of the previous white-robed visitors remain unknown.
