# Velmian

Velmian is the name used by a small, slightly glowing, child-shaped spirit first encountered in an abandoned inn in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]]. He does not wear a mask and claims to remember the area from an impossibly long time ago.

Velmian remembers [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]] as a place associated with Farinia before "weird people" arrived wearing masks. He dislikes Vual and speaks about ancient events with the unreliable sense of time one might expect from a ghost who says things happened a "million billion years ago."

## Lurvius

Velmian once took one of Happy's glowing objects and brought it to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]], explaining only that it felt like it should go there.

Later, inside Tur'Farinil, the party discovered [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Hero's Tapestry|an ancient tapestry]] depicting an adult man who looked remarkably like Lurvius. The inscription named that man **Velmian**.

The child-spirit also resembles a younger version of the same figure.

## Happy

Happy has described a near-death vision in which an adult, whole-looking version of Velmian called him **"brother."**

No one yet knows how these pieces fit together.
