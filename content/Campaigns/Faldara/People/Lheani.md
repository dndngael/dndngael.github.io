Lheani is a young tortle adventurer who joined [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]] inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

## Before the Party

Before meeting the group, Lheani had been trapped in the temple for roughly five days. He had entered with a group of religious explorers, but his companions were killed or carried away by kobolds.

The party freed him while navigating the temple's early chambers, and he continued deeper into Tur'Farinil with them.

## Tur'Farinil

In Session 20, Lheani was the first to detect open space behind an apparently solid wall in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|the Great Library]]. After the party opened the hidden route, he poured water into a pair of silver and gold altar bowls in the pristine courtyard beyond, revealing reflections of Faldara's twin moons.

When [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] remembered that Farinia used to meditate there, Lheani tried it first. He encountered a mysterious long-haired, robed being bearing an hourglass symbol, who asked to speak with the whole group rather than Lheani alone.

## Current Status

Lheani helped resolve the rift crisis and returned to Arbaroŝirm before setting out with the party toward Stormwatch Harbor.

## Session 23: Home, Faith, and Doubt

Lheani spoke about the **Dolian Coast**, the **Skykin**, and a sun-and-two-moons design on his shell that reflects his faith. He also voiced discomfort with the choice to kill Faylen rather than spare her. Other geographic or cultural details discussed at the table have not all been finalized.

## Session 24: Mercy on the Road

Traveling south, Lheani spotted a concealed spike pit before Fineks stepped onto it. At a burning cabin, he attempted to subdue hostile spellcasters without killing them, despite the danger. A *Fireball* knocked him unconscious, but he was healed after the fighting. He later joined Fineks in burying two of the slain attackers. He considered buying a costly returning handaxe in Arbaroŝirm but did not purchase it.
