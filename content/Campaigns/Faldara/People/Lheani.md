Lheani is a young tortle adventurer who joined [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]] inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

## Before the Party

Before meeting the group, Lheani had been trapped in the temple for roughly five days. He had entered with a group of religious explorers, but his companions were killed or carried away by kobolds.

The party freed him while navigating the temple's early chambers, and he continued deeper into Tur'Farinil with them.

## Current Status

In the latest documented events, Lheani is helping the group deal with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/The Rift Crisis|the rift crisis]] after Faylen's defeat.
