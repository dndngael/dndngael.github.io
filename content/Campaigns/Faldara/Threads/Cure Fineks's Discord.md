# Cure Fineks's Discord

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] developed a severe condition involving paranoia, hallucinations, whispers, shadow figures, and dark veins beneath her skin.

The party's investigation led from a strange black crystal found inside a rift creature to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]], who identified the substance as [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]] and explained its connection to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul songs]].

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] embedded [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] into Fineks's mask. Her whispers and shadow manifestations stopped, but her own soul song was also nearly or completely suppressed.

The immediate symptoms are under control. Whether Fineks has actually been cured, and whether her normal soul song can safely return, remains unresolved.

## Session 23: A New Lead

Nephil found an account in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] of an island with a special relationship to Favaro, expressed through the numerical pattern he called **Favaro's thumbprint**. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized this pattern and connected the possible destination with his homeland, **[[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]**.

Nephil believes the island might offer a path to restore Fineks's soul song. Fineks has agreed to travel with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] to investigate. The Codex does not yet establish a cure.
