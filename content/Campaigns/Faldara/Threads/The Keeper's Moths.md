# The Keeper's Moths

## The Mystery

While traveling south, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] repeatedly noticed moths with **precisely painted purple circles** on their backs. They hovered around campfires in a way that made the party feel watched. One marked moth was killed, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] captured another alive.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] detected **divination magic** on the living moth. Speaking with it through a Ring of Animal Influence revealed that it had been told to watch the area by an entity it called **the Keeper**.

## Current Objective

Learn who or what [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Keeper|the Keeper]] is, why the marked moths watch travelers, and whether the warning about the forest conceals a danger. The party chose not to enter the forest, so pursuing this mystery is optional for now.

## Leads and Clues

- The purple circles appear to be carefully painted rather than naturally occurring patterns.
- The marked moths differ slightly in their flight and hovering behavior from ordinary moths.
- A captured moth showed a faint purple divination aura.
- The moth claimed one Keeper watches the area and that other travelers had also been watched.
- It led the party to an old forest containing unusually large numbers of moths.
- Fineks found marks and deliberately arranged moth-attracting structures on a tree.
- Happy saw briefly visible eyes in the dark forest, but could not identify their owner.
- An arrow struck near Fineks. An unseen speaker claimed to be the Keeper and warned the group to turn away.

## Current Status

The party obeyed the warning and left the forest without entering. The Keeper claimed to have protected its boundary for centuries and warned that anyone entering could become lost on its paths forever. Those claims have **not** been tested.

## Session Log

### Session 026 - The Keeper's Moths
- Discovered and investigated the marked insects, captured one, and identified divination magic.
- Followed the moth to the forest and spoke to the unseen Keeper.
- Withdrew rather than crossing the boundary.

## Open Questions

- Who placed the purple circles on the moths?
- Who commands or watches through the divination magic?
- Why monitor travelers on the nearby road?
- Is the Keeper protecting travelers from the forest, guarding something within it, or both?
- Does the forest really trap those who enter?

No connection has been established to Happy's map, Sev's investigation, or any other suspected organization.
