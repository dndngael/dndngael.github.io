# The Other Altars

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Three Altars Tapestry|The Three Altars Tapestry]] in Tur'Farinil depicts three ancient altars.

Paul and 27 recognized one as the altar in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], where the party encountered the Apotheon's vision and found the mask fragment.

The other two remain unidentified.

## Questions

- Where are the other altars?
- Are they connected to Narvono, Farinia, or other deities?
- Do they hold objects or visions like the Lazuli Grotto site?
- Why were the three sites depicted together in Tur'Farinil?
