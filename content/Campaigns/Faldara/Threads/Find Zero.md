# Find Zero

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] left his home island seeking a prophesied figure called Zero. The island's current leader is **Eight (8)**.

The island is in danger, and 27 was told to seek a prophesied hero called **Zero**, who is believed to be important to saving it.

The party does not yet know who Zero is or how to find them.

The word "zero" has gained additional resonance since [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] described [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] Silentstone-suppressed soul song as something like a void or zero, though no connection has been established.

## Session 23: Fineks and Apeiron

27 believes [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] is Zero because Silentstone has suppressed her soul song to something resembling a void. Fineks has not accepted this as proof of her role in the prophecy. She did, however, choose to join the party temporarily because Nephil discovered a possible way to help her through Favaro's association with an island 27 believes is **[[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]**, his homeland.

The party set out toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]] in Session 24, intending to sail for Apeiron. Whether Fineks is truly Zero remains unconfirmed.

## Session 24: Zero or Infinity?

On leaving Arbaroŝirm, 27 reiterated his belief that Fineks is Zero. Fineks wondered whether she might be **infinity** instead. She is traveling with the party to pursue answers about her suppressed soul song, not because the prophecy has been proved. The party has not yet reached Apeiron or identified the prophesied figure with certainty.
