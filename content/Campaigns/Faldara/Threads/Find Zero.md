# Find Zero

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] left his home island on a mission from Archdruid 7.

The island is in danger, and 27 was told to seek a prophesied hero called **Zero**, who is believed to be important to saving it.

The party does not yet know who Zero is or how to find them.

The word "zero" has gained additional resonance since [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] described [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] Silentstone-suppressed soul song as something like a void or zero, though no connection has been established.
