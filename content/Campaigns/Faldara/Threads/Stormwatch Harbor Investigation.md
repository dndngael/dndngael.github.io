# Stormwatch Harbor Investigation

The party's rescue of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]] in Session 24 opened a possible investigation tied to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]]. The group has not yet decided how far to pursue it.

## Evidence the Party Has Seen

- Five hostile spellcasters were burning Sev's cabin. Two died in the fight and three escaped. Their employer was not established.
- A dead spellcaster carried a **contract** bearing the image of a hooded or masked face with a dark, empty center.
- Sev recognized the symbol and suggested it had a connection to something he calls **the Faceless**. The party has not verified his identification.

## Leads Reported by Sev

- A tall, gray-coated woman allegedly hired him to investigate a conspiracy. Sev did not know her name.
- He described repeated lines: “They look without eyes, speak without mouths, and hear without ears” and “Why do we wear masks?”
- He claimed his **former office in the Dry Docks District** still holds research notes. His landlady's name is **Gladys**.
- He reported burning seawater, closed dry docks, and mutual accusations between followers of Solano and Eravona.

These reports are **canonically known to the party as hearsay**. They are not yet independent confirmation of a Faceless organization, a burning sea, or the alleged political and religious circumstances.

## Possible Next Steps

- Find Sev's former office and read the notes he described.
- Ask Gladys about the office and Sev's tenancy.
- Examine the contract's mark and determine who issued it.
- Decide whether this mystery affects the planned voyage to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]].

## Open Questions

- Are the Faceless a real organization? If so, what do they want?
- Was Sev the intended target of the cabin attack?
- Is the gray-coated woman connected to the attackers?
- How much of Sev's account will hold up once the party reaches the harbor?
