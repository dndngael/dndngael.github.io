# Stormwatch Harbor Investigation

The party's rescue of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]] in Session 24 opened a possible investigation tied to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]]. The group has not yet decided how far to pursue it.

## Evidence the Party Has Seen

- Five hostile spellcasters were burning Sev's cabin. Two died in the fight and three escaped. Their employer was not established.
- A dead spellcaster carried a **contract** bearing the image of a hooded or masked face with a dark, empty center.
- Sev recognized the symbol and suggested it had a connection to something he calls **the Faceless**. The party has not verified his identification.

## Leads Reported by Sev

- A tall, gray-coated woman allegedly hired him to investigate a conspiracy. Sev did not know her name.
- He described repeated lines: “They look without eyes, speak without mouths, and hear without ears” and “Why do we wear masks?”
- He claimed his **former office in the Dry Docks District** still holds research notes. His landlady's name is **Gladys**.
- He reported burning seawater, closed dry docks, and mutual accusations between followers of Solano and Eravona.

In Session 28, the party independently witnessed an apparently blazing sea and open religious hostility. That does **not** establish a Faceless organization or either faith's responsibility for the crisis.

## Separate Lead: Happy's Map (Session 25)

During a later camp watch, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] drew a coastal map seemingly without knowing what he was drawing. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], including the Tidal Spire, Church of Solano, and Temple of the Deep. A specific building was marked with a circle, but Happy did not recognize it or know why he had drawn it.

This is a **separate, unexplained personal lead** for Happy, now tracked in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Find the Circled Building|Find the Circled Building]]. No connection between his drawing, Sev's investigation, or the alleged Faceless has been established. The Session 26 forest encounter and the Keeper's moths have likewise not been connected to Sev's reports.

## Additional Religious Leads (Session 27)

These are separate reports and contacts, not verified proof of Sev's theory, the existence of the Faceless, or the cause of the burning-water reports.

- **Fisherman Mac**, who follows Eravona, referred the group to **Captain Lannery** at the seaside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Churning Chapel|Churning Chapel]]. He said she leads the **Churning Church**, served as his captain, and should receive his regards.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]], a pilgrim of Solano, referred Lheani to **Cardinal Pedro**, reportedly from **New-Doleh**, at the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]]. She gave Lheani a medal that she said would permit him to attempt a trial for an audience.

The group has since **visited the Tidal Spire**, but has not entered the **Churning Chapel**. The chapel's relationship to the Temple of the Deep from Happy's sketch remains uncertain.

## New Evidence (Session 28)

On **Vuana 26**, the party entered Stormwatch Harbor, saw the sea apparently burn, and obtained access to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Sev's Office|Sev's Office]]. The case file hidden in his desk says a tall gray-coated woman hired him to investigate a cult, and names **Errin Ridge** as a man who stole a sacred relic from the Tidal Spire and attempted to sell it through a gambling den associated with **the Perch**. Sev described finding Ridge apparently hanged with strange writing about eyes, ears, mouths, and masks on the walls. He also recorded being followed and discovering a severed hand at his door before fleeing.

These are **Sev's accounts**. The employer, Ridge's death, the theft, and the threats have not been independently explained. The recovered files do not prove a relationship to the Faceless.

The party dispersed a hostile dispute between Eravona and Solano adherents. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Captain Exx|Captain Exx]] expressed concern about rising violence. At the Tidal Spire, the group reached the **Triumph of Saint Ludavica**, but has neither attempted it nor met Cardinal Pedro.

## Possible Next Steps

- Investigate **Errin Ridge**, the alleged stolen relic, and the Perch gambling den.
- Use Sev's recovered files as leads while keeping the rented office. The next month's rent is payable by the **1st**.
- Examine the contract's mark and determine who issued it.
- Decide whether this mystery affects the planned voyage to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]].
- Compare Happy's sketch with the city and identify the circled building.

## Open Questions

- Are the Faceless a real organization? If so, what do they want?
- Was Sev the intended target of the cabin attack?
- Is the gray-coated woman connected to the attackers?
- How much of Sev's account will hold up once the party reaches the harbor?
- What, if anything, explains Happy's apparent familiarity with the harbor and its circled building?
