# Roadside Ambush

## The Situation

On approximately the twelfth day of travel toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] heard a halfling calling for help from beneath a fallen tree.

The party worked to free the apparent victim. When [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] tried to heal him, her magic had no apparent effect. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] noticed that the supposed injuries showed no blood. The halfling smiled, **the illusion vanished**, and armed figures emerged from the surrounding trees.

## Current Objective

Survive or otherwise escape the ambush. If circumstances allow, discover who arranged the illusion and what the attackers want.

## What the Party Knows

- The trapped halfling was an illusion rather than a person successfully rescued from the log.
- The false emergency was timed with the appearance of armed attackers.
- Their identities, employer, motives, and whether they have any connection to the group's earlier enemies remain unconfirmed.
- The party has rolled initiative, but the battle's outcome was **not determined in Session 26**.
