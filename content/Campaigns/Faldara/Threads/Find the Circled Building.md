# Find the Circled Building

## The Mystery

During the final watch of Session 25, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] began drawing in the dirt without consciously intending to produce a map. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized the sketch as [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], even though the party had not yet reached it.

Happy had drawn the coastline and landmarks, including the **Dawn Spire**, **Church of Solano**, and **Temple of the Deep**. Most strikingly, he had **circled a building** whose identity neither he nor 27 could explain. Happy felt a strange familiarity with the city.

## Current Objective

Reach Stormwatch Harbor and try to identify the building marked on Happy's sketch. Determine whether it can shed light on Happy's missing memories.

## Leads and Clues

- Happy drew the harbor almost automatically during a night watch, without explaining how he knew its layout.
- 27 recognized several landmarks, giving the party reference points to compare with the real city.
- The circled building remains unidentified, and Happy's apparent familiarity has no established cause.
- The sketch is a **separate lead** from [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Stormwatch Harbor Investigation|Sev's harbor investigation]]. No connection between the two mysteries has been confirmed.

## Session Log

### Session 025 - A Pink Dome and a Forgotten Map
- Happy made the inexplicable drawing, and 27 recognized Stormwatch Harbor and its landmarks.
- The group learned there was a circled building it could not identify.

### Session 026 - The Keeper's Moths
- The group continued toward the harbor but did not reach it.
- No new explanation for the map or building emerged.

## Open Questions

- Which building did Happy circle?
- Why did he draw Stormwatch Harbor without intending to?
- Has he been there before, or does the familiarity have some other cause?
- Will identifying the building reveal anything about his missing history?
