The **Medallion of Solano** is a sun-marked token given to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]], a pilgrim devoted to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Solano|Solano]], during [[Campaigns/Faldara/Sessions/Session 027 - A Fish, Two Faiths, and a Questionable Rope|Session 27]].

## How Lheani Received It

On the road to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], the party met Lucille tending a wounded wolf. Lheani calmed the frightened animal so she could treat its injuries. During their conversation, Lucille learned that Lheani came from **New-Doleh** and saw the markings painted on his shell. Although she did not recognize their meaning, she suggested that **Cardinal Pedro**, a Solano religious leader she said was also from New-Doleh, might know more.

In thanks, Lucille gave Lheani this medallion. She also asked him to pass along word to the cardinal that her pilgrimage continues.

## Known Purpose

According to Lucille, the medallion serves as an **invitation to the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]]** in Stormwatch Harbor. There, Lheani may attempt a **trial** before being permitted an audience with Cardinal Pedro.

The medallion does not itself guarantee an audience or success in the trial. **No magical powers, rarity, or attunement requirements have been established.**

## Current Status

**Lheani holds the medallion.** The party has not yet visited the Tidal Spire, attempted its trial, or met Cardinal Pedro.
