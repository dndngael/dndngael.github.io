# Mask Piece

A fragment of a black-and-red mask covered in glittering golden markings. It is attached to a golden chain that magically resizes so the piece can be worn as a necklace.

The party found it at the ancient prayer site in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]] immediately after the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Apotheon vision]].

## Known Properties

The item requires attunement.

- The wearer gains a +1 bonus to AC.
- It can shed bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for another 15 feet.
- It holds 3 charges that return at dawn. A charge can be used to cast *invisibility* on the holder or an ally.
- Attunement can only be maintained for two consecutive days. After that, attempts to attune again within the next two days fail.

The design resembles the mask worn by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|the Apotheon]] in the vision and another cloaked figure depicted in an ancient Tur'Farinil tapestry.
