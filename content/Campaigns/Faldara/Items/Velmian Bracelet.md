The **Velmian Bracelet** appeared in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Sessions/Session 023 - You Guys, You Can't Ask Me for Names|Session 23]], when [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] appeared with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]]. Farinia explained that the spirit was a piece of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|the Apotheon]]. Velmian became a magical thread that wrapped around [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]]'s wrist.

## What the Party Knows

The bracelet is one known fragment of the Apotheon. The [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Mask Piece|mask piece]] carried by Alvin is another. Farinia urged the party to seek more fragments. Whether Velmian can speak, reappear, or respond to other fragments has not been established.
