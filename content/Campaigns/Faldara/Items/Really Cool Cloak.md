# Really Cool Cloak

A cloak owned by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin Draper]]. Its exterior looks like simple dark wool, while the inside swirls with an iridescent rainbow shimmer. It is surprisingly light and rustles softly like leaves when it moves.

Alvin acquired it at the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Mistwind Caravan|Mistwind Caravan]] after the group put on a performance to raise money and morale.

## Known Properties

- The cloak can be commanded to billow dramatically as a bonus action.
- Once attuned, it awakens to sentience and tries to protect its wearer.
- It grants +1 AC and +1 to saving throws.
- It can float nearby and move within 30 feet as directed.

It is, by all available evidence, a really cool cloak.
