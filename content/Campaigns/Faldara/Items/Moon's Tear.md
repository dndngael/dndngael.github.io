The **Moon's Tear** is the name [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen Nemyris]] gave to the dark stone she wore around her neck. A red light pulses within it.

Faylen believed the stone came from the moon and treated it as an important part of her teachings about the Twin Light. The party has gathered considerable evidence that the stone can interact with creatures, people, and the rift beneath [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], but its true nature remains uncertain.

## Known Properties

- Faylen used the stone while interacting with creatures she called "moon children."
- A smaller similar stone given to Alvin burned him when touched directly.
- During the final confrontation with Faylen, Paul heard an indistinct voice apparently coming from the stone while Faylen was magically influencing Ĵura.
- An intangible image resembling the Moon's Tear appeared on a pillar in the rift chamber. Alvin's hand passed through the image.
- Paul removed the real Moon's Tear from Faylen while she was incapacitated and tried placing it into the pillar image. No obvious effect followed.
- After Faylen died, the surviving Vesperaŝ stopped behaving like straightforward attackers and focused instead on whoever possessed the stone.

## Current Status

At the end of Session 21, Paul handed the Moon's Tear to Happy while the Vesperaŝ gathered around him.

During [[Campaigns/Faldara/Sessions/Session 022 - The Rift Crisis|Session 22]], the stone began moving toward the still-open rift. Paul grabbed it and tried to hold it back while the party searched for another way to interfere with its movement.

A silver thread of energy then appeared connecting the rift and the stone through or along Paul.
