# Farinia's Codex

Farinia's Codex is an ancient crystal archive associated with Farinia and hidden inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|the Great Library of Tur'Farinil]].

Unlike an ordinary book, the Codex stores information as psychic frequencies. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] believed his resonance-based abilities would allow him to read it.

Nephil asked the party to recover the Codex in exchange for the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] used to help Fineks.

After navigating Tur'Farinil's puzzles and discovering the hidden library, the party finally recovered the Codex in Session 19.

What knowledge the Codex contains, and what Nephil can learn from it, remains an important unanswered question.
