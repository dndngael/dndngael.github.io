# Eye-Bearing Sapphire

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] found a clear blue sapphire inside a fish purchased from Fisherman Mac. In the light, an image resembling a small moving eye could be seen within the stone.

Fineks recognized the image as a sign associated with invitations attributed to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]]. It is **not established** who placed the sapphire in the fish, whether it carries a genuine invitation, or whether it has magical powers.

She kept it without telling her companions in character.
