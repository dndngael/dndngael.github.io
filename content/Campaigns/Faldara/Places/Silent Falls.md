# Silent Falls

Silent Falls is an inn and tavern in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]] that became an early home base for Alvin and 27 during the festival.

Paul's mask was stolen nearby at the very beginning of the campaign. Later, the party returned here repeatedly between festival events and celebrated becoming Champions of the Lowgates.

The area outside Silent Falls also hosted the Dragon Cake competition where Paul earned the title **The Hurlin' Earl**.
