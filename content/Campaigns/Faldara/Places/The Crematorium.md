# The Crematorium

The Crematorium in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] handles the dead from a city that has endured generations of attacks from creatures associated with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

The party first came here after helping carry the body of a guard killed during the attack that greeted their arrival in the city.

Later, the Crematorium became the site of the party's improvised autopsy of a rift creature they nicknamed **Jeff**. Inside the corpse they found a jagged, black, non-reflective crystal connected by dark tendrils that resembled the markings appearing on [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]]. Removing it caused the surrounding remains to animate into a much larger flesh creature.
