The party visited the former office of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]] in Stormwatch Harbor's fire-scarred, lower-income district on **Vuana 26**. The upstairs suite includes an office, a modest bedroom, and a desk with a concealed compartment.

## What the Party Found

After securing access from [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Gladys|Gladys]], the group discovered **20 gp** and Sev's latest case file hidden in his desk. The money was split evenly into five shares of **4 gp**. The records discuss **Errin Ridge**, a reportedly stolen Tidal Spire relic, the **Perch** gambling den, and the threats that drove Sev out of the city. See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Stormwatch Harbor Investigation|Stormwatch Harbor Investigation]].

Lheani hung a sign reading **“Lheani (and You Guys) Detective Agency.”** [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nedry|Nedry]] offered to direct potential customers to the office and watch for suspicious visitors.

## Rental Terms

Gladys gave the group **three keys**. The current rental is paid through **Vuana 28**, the last day of the month. Next month's **60 gp** rent remains unpaid, with the group allowed **through the 1st** of the next month to pay.
