# Churning Temple

> [!infobox]+ Churning Temple
> | | |
> | --- | --- |

> | **Location** | [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor\|Stormwatch Harbor]] |

Fisherman Mac described the Churning Temple as a seaside place of Eravona worship in Stormwatch Harbor. He called **Captain Lannery** the head of the associated **Churning Church**, said he once served under her command, and asked the party to pass along his regards.

Mac interpreted the harbor's reported trouble as Eravona's anger at Solano's influence. His account is not independently verified. The party has not visited the temple or met its leader.

The Churning Temple is **not confirmed to be the same building** as the Temple of the Deep recognized from Happy's earlier harbor map.
