Vualio City is the capital and heart of the Kingdom of Vualio, a large inland metropolis built along the Kruma River. Wealth, religion, commerce, and the kingdom's mask-centered traditions all converge here.

The city is arranged in a broad circular pattern with major gates, bridges, districts, and farmland surrounding much of the outer city. Its western districts hold much of the kingdom's wealth and authority, while areas such as [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lowgates|Lowgates]] are busier and more mercantile.

## Masks and Religion

The influence of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones|The Masked Ones]] is visible throughout Vualio. Masks are central to social identity, ceremony, and public life, especially during **La Festo de Maskoj** and the Maskovo.

## Campaign History

The campaign began here when Paul, Alvin, and 27 met after Paul's mask was stolen. Their investigation soon became entangled with festival competitions, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]], and eventually the strange events in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]].
