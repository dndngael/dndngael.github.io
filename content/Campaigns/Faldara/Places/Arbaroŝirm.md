# Arbaroŝirm

Arbaroŝirm is a fortified small city in Kaŭro Duchy, built in the Mistwind Forest beneath the black spires of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]]. The settlement is heavily militarized and defended by the Mistwind Army under the authority of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]].

The city has a population of roughly 10,000 and is historically connected to the surrounding timber industry.

## Life Under the Temple

Arbaroŝirm has endured attacks by strange creatures from Tur'Farinil for generations. During the party's stay, those attacks became increasingly frequent. Residents are practiced at responding to alarms, boarding buildings, helping soldiers, and moving to safety.

The party arrived during one such attack and immediately joined the city's defense.
