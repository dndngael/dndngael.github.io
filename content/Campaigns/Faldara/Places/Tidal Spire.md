# Tidal Spire

According to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]], the **Tidal Spire** is Stormwatch Harbor's huge lighthouse and a cathedral dedicated to Solano. **Cardinal Pedro**, reportedly from **New-Doleh**, can be approached there after a trial for which Lucille gave Lheani the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Medallion of Solano|Medallion of Solano]].

The party entered this towering marble lighthouse on **Vuana 26 (Session 28)**. The entrance is guarded and the interior displays Solano's iconography, statuary, religious artwork, and healing rooms where people of different means receive care. The landmark recognized on Happy's earlier sketch is this **Tidal Spire**.

Tidal Spire guards recognized [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]] as a **saint-elect** and admitted Lheani and the group when he presented her medallion. Inside, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Elanora|Elanora]] confirmed the medallion's one-use healing ability and led the party upstairs toward the **Triumph of Saint Ludavica**.

According to the saint's tradition, Ludavica confronted a warlord threatening the country and calmed him rather than defeating him through force. Elanora explained the challenge as **“extinguish the flames of anger,”** reminding the party that actions may be needed when words are insufficient.

The group reached a third-floor chamber with a statue blocking the far door. **The trial had not started and Cardinal Pedro had not been met** when Session 28 ended.
