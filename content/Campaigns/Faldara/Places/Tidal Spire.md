According to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]], the **Tidal Spire** is Stormwatch Harbor's huge lighthouse and a cathedral dedicated to Solano. **Cardinal Pedro**, reportedly from **New-Doleh**, can be approached there after a trial for which Lucille gave Lheani an invitation medal.

The party has not visited it or attempted the trial. There is **no confirmed identification** between the Tidal Spire and the Dawn Spire or Church of Solano shown on Happy's map.
