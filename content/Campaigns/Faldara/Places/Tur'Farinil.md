# Tur'Farinil

Tur'Farinil is an ancient black-stone temple looming above [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]]. The temple is closely associated with Farinia, the Spinner, and has become the center of the campaign's largest cluster of mysteries.

For generations, strange creatures have emerged from a rift somewhere deep inside the structure. The attacks became more frequent during the party's time in Arbaroŝirm.

## What the Party Has Discovered

Tur'Farinil is built around memory, music, resonance, time, and ritual. Its puzzles often respond not to brute force but to remembered songs, paired tones, symbolic choices, and specific sequences.

The party has encountered:

- Farinia's disembodied voice and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Riddle of Tur'Farinil|riddle]].
- Three identical early doorways that resisted ordinary exploration while "the loom" remained tangled.
- Memory rituals involving Past, Present, and Future.
- Musical and resonance puzzles navigated with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]].
- Batlike Vesperaŝ that appeared to act as guardians when rituals were respected.
- Ancient tapestries referencing [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Dyad War|the Dyad War]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]], and three mysterious altars.
- A sequence of runestones spelling **D-E-F-A-C-E**. Playing the melody from its root note opened the path deeper into the temple.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|the Great Library]], hidden deep beneath the temple.
- A second use of the DEFACE melody, completed with the lyric **"Don't ever forget. All choices echo,"** which opened a concealed wall in the library.
- A previously unreachable open-air courtyard that appeared impossibly pristine. Its plants were manicured, bugs stood motionless, and the stars did not match the night sky from when the party entered the temple.
- A large altar in that courtyard with silver and gold bowls. Water poured into them reflected Farin as full and Zil as a waning crescent.
- A meditative encounter begun in the courtyard with a mysterious robed figure bearing an hourglass symbol. The session ended before the party learned the figure's identity.
- A rift associated with the creatures attacking Arbaroŝirm and with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen's]] work.

## Current Status

The rift was **closed in Session 22** when Paul used a staff to insert Alvin's smaller stone into it, after Lheani shattered Faylen's larger Moon's Tear. In Session 23, the party returned with Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro, who personally confirmed that the rift was gone. A previously strange pool appeared to behave normally.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] subsequently revealed the ancient hero depicted in the temple's tapestries as [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|the Apotheon]] and turned the child-spirit fragment Velmian into a [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Velmian Bracelet|bracelet]] carried by Happy. The ancient tapestries remain intact.

The group has returned to Arbaroŝirm. The rift crisis is resolved, although the temple still holds mysteries.
