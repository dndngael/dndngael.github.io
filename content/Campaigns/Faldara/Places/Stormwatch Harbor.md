**Stormwatch Harbor** is the party's intended route to the sea and onward to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]. [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] departed [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] heading south in Session 24 and reached the harbor in Session 28, on **Vuana 26**.

## Accounts Heard on the Road

Sailors' earlier stories described frightening weather and water that appeared to burn. In Session 24, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]], a man who claimed to have worked as a private investigator in the harbor, gave the party further accounts:

- The seawater is allegedly burning, with some waves reportedly rising like walls or tsunamis of fire.
- Sev claimed the **Dry Docks District** had shut down.
- According to Sev, followers of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Solano|Solano]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]] blame one another for the trouble.
- He said his former investigative office and research notes remain in the Dry Docks District, where his landlady is called **Gladys**.

In Session 28 the party **witnessed** the sea appearing to blaze orange, fire-scorched waterfront buildings, a conspicuous absence of ships, and a heated dispute between the two faiths. The cause of the apparent burning and any complete closure of shipping are still unproven.

## Leads

A contract recovered from a spellcaster at a burning cabin bore an unusual hooded, blank-faced emblem. Sev said he recognized the mark and connected it to something he called **the Faceless**. The physical contract is real, but its meaning, the existence and nature of the Faceless, and Sev's explanation remain unverified.

See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Stormwatch Harbor Investigation|Stormwatch Harbor Investigation]].

## Religious Leads Heard on the Road (Session 27)

**Fisherman Mac**, an Eravona devotee, described the seaside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Churning Chapel|Churning Chapel]] and said its associated **Churning Church** is headed by **Captain Lannery**, his former captain. Mac attributed the harbor's reported troubles to Eravona's anger with Solano's influence. That is his belief, not an independently verified cause.

A Solano pilgrim, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]], told the travelers that the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]] is a cathedral and huge lighthouse in Stormwatch Harbor. She said **Cardinal Pedro**, reportedly from **New-Doleh**, is based there. After Lheani helped her care for an injured wolf, she gave him a medal permitting him to attempt a trial for an audience.

The party entered the **Tidal Spire** in Session 28 but has not yet met Cardinal Pedro. They have not visited the **Churning Chapel** or Captain Lannery. The chapel's relationship to the **Temple of the Deep** from Happy's sketch has not been confirmed.

Session 27 ended with the party camped for **one final night before arriving at Stormwatch Harbor**.

## Happy's Unexpected Map (Session 25)

Before the party reached the harbor, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] absentmindedly drew a map of a coastal settlement during a night watch. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized it as Stormwatch Harbor and identified the **Tidal Spire**, the **Church of Solano**, the **Temple of the Deep**, and the coastline in the sketch.

Happy had also **circled a particular building**. Neither he nor 27 explained what the building was, and Happy could not say why he had drawn this place. He felt it was strangely familiar, but the reason remains unknown. The party has now entered Stormwatch Harbor, though Happy was absent for the initial exploration and the circled building remains unidentified.

## The Harbor on Vuana 26 (Session 28)

The group saw an apparently burning, orange sea, scorched buildings, no ships in sight, a tall white marble lighthouse, and a dark chapel above the opposite cliffs. **Paul estimated** that the city could hold around 8,000 residents, but this was not an official count.

On the way to the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tidal Spire|Tidal Spire]], the group dispersed a dispute between devotees of Solano and Eravona. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Captain Exx|Captain Exx]], the watch captain, rewarded them and warned that prolonged trouble could lead to greater violence. That was his concern, not a firm prediction.

The party now has access to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Sev's Office|Sev's Office]], where a sign advertises **Lheani (and You Guys) Detective Agency**. Its existing rental is paid through **Vuana 28**, and next month's **60 gp** rent is payable by the **1st**. They recovered Sev's case file and established a connection with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nedry|Nedry]].

At the Tidal Spire, they met [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Elanora|Elanora]] and reached the third-floor chamber for the **Triumph of Saint Ludavica**, but did not begin the test or meet Cardinal Pedro.
