**Stormwatch Harbor** is the party's intended route to the sea and onward to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]. [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] departed [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] heading south in Session 24 and have not reached the harbor.

## Accounts Heard on the Road

Sailors' earlier stories described frightening weather and water that appeared to burn. In Session 24, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]], a man who claimed to have worked as a private investigator in the harbor, gave the party further accounts:

- The seawater is allegedly burning, with some waves reportedly rising like walls or tsunamis of fire.
- Sev claimed the **Dry Docks District** had shut down.
- According to Sev, followers of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Solano|Solano]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]] blame one another for the trouble.
- He said his former investigative office and research notes remain in the Dry Docks District, where his landlady is called **Gladys**.

**These accounts are established as things the characters were told, not as confirmed conditions in the harbor.** The party has not yet witnessed any burning sea, port closure, or religious dispute firsthand.

## Leads

A contract recovered from a spellcaster at a burning cabin bore an unusual hooded, blank-faced emblem. Sev said he recognized the mark and connected it to something he called **the Faceless**. The physical contract is real, but its meaning, the existence and nature of the Faceless, and Sev's explanation remain unverified.

See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Stormwatch Harbor Investigation|Stormwatch Harbor Investigation]].
