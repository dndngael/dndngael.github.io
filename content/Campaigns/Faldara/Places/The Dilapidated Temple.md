# The Dilapidated Temple

The Dilapidated Temple is an overgrown, partly ruined shrine in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] tended by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]], a firbolg follower of Favaro.

Collapsed walls have been repurposed into shelves for earthenware pots and planters, and the plants are deliberately maintained despite the surrounding decay.

The party connected this place to the phrase **"Return to the root"** in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Riddle of Tur'Farinil|Farinia's riddle]]. Donneran also became one of their most useful sources of spiritual advice, helping them commune with Favaro about Faylen and Fineks.

The exact ancient history of the structure remains unclear to the party.
