# Churning Chapel

> [!infobox]+ Churning Chapel
> | | |
> | --- | --- |

> | **Location** | [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor\|Stormwatch Harbor]] |

Fisherman Mac described the Churning Chapel as a seaside place of Eravona worship in Stormwatch Harbor. He called **Captain Lannery** the head of the associated **Churning Church**, said he once served under her command, and asked the party to pass along his regards.

Mac interpreted the harbor's reported trouble as Eravona's anger at Solano's influence. His account is not independently verified. The party has not visited the temple or met its leader.

Whether the chapel is also the **Temple of the Deep** identified from Happy's earlier sketch remains unconfirmed. In Session 28, Nedry gave directions to this cliffside chapel. The party has not yet visited it.
