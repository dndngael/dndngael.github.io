# Lazuli Grotto

The Lazuli Grotto was the site of the final festival challenge that pitted [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]] against [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]].

During the race, a shark broke open a passage glowing with golden light. The party followed it into a ruined chamber with ancient columns and a crystal pedestal.

There they found a red-and-gold object attached to a fine golden chain. When Paul touched it, Paul, Alvin, and 27 received the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Vision in the Lazuli Grotto]].

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] later identified the chamber as an ancient prayer site connected to Narvono, the Dream Weaver.

The altar in the grotto later appeared again in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Three Altars Tapestry|an ancient tapestry]] inside Tur'Farinil, alongside two other altars the party has not yet identified.
