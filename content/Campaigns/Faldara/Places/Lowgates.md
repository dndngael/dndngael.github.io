Lowgates is a busy district of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]] and the center of many of the festival competitions that brought the original party together.

Paul, Alvin, and 27 competed in events including Dragon Cake eating, Riddles and Rhymes, Horizonback Tortoise herding, and the final challenge through the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]].

Their success in the grotto made them that year's **Champions of the Lowgates**.

## Places and People

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Silent Falls|Silent Falls]] served as an early base for the party.
- Captain Roseshade led the local guard during the investigation into Paul's stolen mask.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] ran the Riddles and Rhymes competition and later became an important source of information about the Apotheon's vision.
