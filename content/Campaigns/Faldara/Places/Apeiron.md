**Apeiron** is the home island of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]], a druid who follows [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Favaro|Favaro]] and believes nature can be understood through mathematics. 27 left because his island faces an unspecified danger. Apeiron's current leader is **Eight (8)**. 27 was instructed to seek a prophesied figure called **Zero**.

## Favaro and the Soul Numbers

27 has described the island's interest in numerical patterns and classifications associated with people's souls. His account of how those classifications are treated raises serious questions among his companions.

In Session 23, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] found a passage in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] describing an unnamed island linked to **Favaro's thumbprint** and the sequence **1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13**. 27 believes that this may describe Apeiron. Nephil suggested that such a connection could possibly help [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] regain her suppressed soul song. None of these connections has yet been verified on the island.

## Planned Journey

The party departed Arbaroŝirm in Session 24, heading south toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]] to seek passage by ship to Apeiron. Sailors have reported strange storms and flames on the water in that direction, but the party has not witnessed these phenomena.

## Open Questions

- What do the sailors' accounts of burning seawater mean?

## Session 24: Accounts of Eight

Before departing Arbaroŝirm, 27 described the current leader of Apeiron as **Eight** and spoke of the island's numerical identity traditions. Fineks questioned whether her own number could be infinity rather than zero. This exchange establishes what was discussed, not Fineks's prophetic identity or the full workings of Apeiron's society. The party is still traveling and has not reached the island.
