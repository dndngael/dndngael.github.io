# Mistwind Caravan

The Mistwind Caravan was a travel stop on the party's journey toward Arbaroŝirm.

It became memorable for two major additions to the campaign: [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] met the group there and decided to travel with them, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] put on a performance to earn enough money for his [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Really Cool Cloak|Really Cool Cloak]].

The performance raised morale, earned the group money, and cemented Alvin's stage name, **The Twinkler**.

The road onward included a shambling mound fight and the beginning of Happy's travels with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|the party]].
