# Vision in the Lazuli Grotto

After [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] touched a red-and-black object glowing with golden light on a pedestal in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], Paul, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] were pulled into a vision.

A spectral man formed from dark, swirling red-and-black vapor. He wore a black cloak and a red mask marked with magnificent golden patterns. Behind the mask, deep purple eyes looked out with overwhelming sadness.

He pleaded:

> "I am imprisoned. Please help me."

The party then felt themselves falling through a vortex of golden light before becoming suspended in a pitch-black expanse, weightless beneath crushing pressure. A vermilion light revealed the figure again, shackled by fleshy crimson strands.

He cried out to the Dream Weaver, begging for someone capable of saving him to be guided to the place where he had first prayed. He identified himself as **the Apotheon** and said he had once prayed to **the Spinner** in the heart of a temple of evil.

Before the vision ended, he begged the party to take his mask and save him.

## What Followed

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] later identified the chamber as an ancient prayer site associated with Narvono, the Dream Weaver, and identified the Spinner as Farinia.

The vision became the origin of the party's ongoing quest to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Save the Apotheon|Save the Apotheon]].
