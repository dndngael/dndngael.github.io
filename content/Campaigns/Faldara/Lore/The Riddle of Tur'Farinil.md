# The Riddle of Tur'Farinil

A disembodied voice associated with Farinia spoke this riddle to the party at [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]]:

> The loom is tangled. Threads fray before they are woven.  
> You seek what is hidden, but the path is broken.  
> One who is lost remembers the altar.  
> One who is blind sees the way.  
> One who believes must be unmade.  
> Return to the root and the door shall yield.

## What the Party Learned

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] filled the role of the **lost one** who remembered the ancient temple.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] became the **blind one who sees the way**, using resonance rather than ordinary sight.
- The phrase **return to the root** eventually connected to a sequence of runestones spelling **D-E-F-A-C-E**. Playing that melody from its root note opened the path into the lower temple.
- The meaning of the **believer who must be unmade** became entangled with the party's investigation of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]] and the events that followed.

Although the riddle opened the way through Tur'Farinil, the larger meaning of its imagery about tangled threads and broken paths remains open to interpretation.
