# The Hero's Tapestry

Inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], the party discovered an ancient tapestry depicting a grown man who looked strikingly like [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]].

The inscription identified the figure as:

> **Velmian. Hero apotheosized. Lost yet unforgotten.**

The figure also resembled an adult version of the child-shaped spirit the party already knew as [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]].

Three sigils accompanied the figure: the crescent-eye symbol of Narvono, the hourglass of Farinia, and an unidentified impossible-triangle symbol.

The tapestry is one of the strongest pieces of evidence the party has found connecting Lurvius, Velmian, the ancient temple, and the mysteries surrounding the Apotheon.
