# The Three Sigils

Three symbols appeared alongside Velmian on [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Hero's Tapestry|the Hero's Tapestry]] in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

The party identified two of them:

- A **crescent eye**, associated with Narvono, the Dream Weaver.
- An **hourglass**, associated with Farinia, the Spinner.

The third symbol resembles an **impossible triangle**. Its identity and significance remain unknown.

The three symbols appear to mark important connections surrounding Velmian, but the party does not yet know what those connections mean.
