# Soul Songs

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil Salŝius]] uses the term **soul song** for the unique natural frequency of a person's soul.

His research suggests that psychic and magical phenomena can interact with these frequencies through resonance.

## Fineks

Nephil connected [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks's]] affliction to interference with her soul song. After [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] embedded [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] into Fineks's mask, the whispers and shadow figures stopped, but Nephil could no longer perceive her normal soul song. He described her as something like a **void** or **zero**.

## Wider Resonance

The idea of unique frequencies has become important throughout [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]]. Nephil uses resonance to navigate ancient mechanisms, and Alvin has also shown an unusual ability to match magical frequencies.
