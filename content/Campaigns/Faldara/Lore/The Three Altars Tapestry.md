# The Three Altars Tapestry

An ancient tapestry inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]] depicts three distinct altars.

Paul and 27 recognized one of them as the altar from the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], where the party received the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Apotheon vision]] and found the mysterious mask fragment.

The other two altars remain unidentified.

The discovery suggests that the Lazuli Grotto site is part of a larger pattern rather than an isolated ruin.

See [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/The Other Altars|The Other Altars]].
