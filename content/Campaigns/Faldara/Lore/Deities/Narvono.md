Narvono is the deity of sleep, dreams, nightmares, and the subconscious. Their visions may inspire, warn, confuse, or torment, and their worshipers often study dreams as messages worth interpreting rather than dismissing.

## Worship

Followers are commonly called **Narvonites** and include mystics, artists, seers, and scholars of dreams.

The **Night of Whispers** is a vigil of meditation and dream ritual, while the **Festival of Dreams** celebrates shared visions and creative expression.

## Relevance to the Party

Narvono is directly connected to the mystery that began in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]]. The party discovered an ancient prayer site associated with Narvono and received the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|vision]] that sent them searching for the Apotheon.

Umbral Kaz later recognized the site as a prayer place of the Dream Weaver.
