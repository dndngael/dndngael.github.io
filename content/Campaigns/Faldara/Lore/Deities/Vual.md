Vual is the god most closely associated with masks, secrecy, hidden knowledge, illusion, and the layers separating truth from appearance. Vual is commonly depicted masked, with the true face concealed.

In Vualio, worship of Vual is woven deeply into everyday culture and civic identity. Masks are not simply decoration. They communicate role, calling, social place, and religious meaning.

## Worship and Masks

The official faith is organized through [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones|The Masked Ones]]. One of Vualio's central rites is the **Maskovo**, when a person receives a mask representing a new stage, role, profession, or calling.

Other traditions include the **Night of Veils**, centered on obscured identity, riddles, and trickery, and twilight rites where worshipers seek hidden truths through divination.

## Questions Raised by the Campaign

The party has repeatedly encountered signs that Vualio's masking traditions may be much younger than some of the ancient places and beings they are investigating.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]], for example, remembers a time before masks were commonplace and reacts negatively to Vual. Ancient imagery in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]] has also made the relationship between masks, the old gods, and forgotten history increasingly important.

What that history actually means remains unresolved.
