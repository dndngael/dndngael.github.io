Vendako represents justice as a balancing of scales, whether that balance comes through courts, punishment, or personal vengeance. His traditions are generally less concerned with mercy than with ensuring that a wrong receives an answer.

## Worship

His followers are known collectively as **the Order of Vendako** and include judges, soldiers, executioners, and people seeking retribution.

The **Scales of Truth** is a public trial tradition associated with settling disputes, while the **Night of Vengeance** is a more secretive rite for those asking Vendako's help against someone who wronged them.
