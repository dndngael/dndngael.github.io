Zilera governs both tender affection and fierce passion. Her traditions recognize romance, friendship, family bonds, artistic desire, and other forms of heartfelt connection.

## Worship

Followers are known as **Heartkeepers** and include couples, friends, poets, artists, and performers.

The **Festival of Hearts** celebrates relationships of many kinds. During the **Flame of Desire**, worshipers cast written hopes into a sacred fire and ask for Zilera's favor in pursuing them.
