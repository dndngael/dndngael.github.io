# Deities of Faldara

The peoples of Faldara worship a broad pantheon whose names shape holidays, temples, months, customs, and even the moons overhead.

## Especially Relevant to the Campaign

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] — time, fate, and the Spinner whose ancient temple is [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Narvono|Narvono]] — dreams, nightmares, and the Dream Weaver connected to the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto vision]].
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Favaro|Favaro]] — harvest, abundance, and nature; worshiped by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]].
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Vual|Vual]] — masks, secrecy, illusion, and one of the central divine figures of Vualio.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Kreiĉiza|Kreiĉiza]] — transformation, artistry, magic, and self-remaking.

## Other Known Deities

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]] — storms and the sea.
- Flamiso — the forge.
- Lumosca — light and stars.
- Sernoa — decay and disease.
- Solano — the sun and healing.
- Vendako — justice and vengeance.
- Vireva — craft and trade.
- Zilera — love and passion.
