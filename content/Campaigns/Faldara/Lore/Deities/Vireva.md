Vireva governs skilled craft, commerce, and the prosperity created when ingenuity meets exchange. Her traditions praise good workmanship, fair dealing, useful invention, and the ability to turn labor into lasting value.

## Worship

Followers are called **Virevans** and commonly include merchants, smiths, artisans, and traders.

**Crafting Day** celebrates artisans by displaying and offering their best work. The **Merchant's Blessing** is recited before important deals in hopes of both fairness and profit.
