Kreiĉiza is associated with creation, transformation, magic, artistry, and the idea that identity itself can be shaped. Their traditions treat change as a creative act rather than merely a loss of what came before.

Followers historically included artisans, wizards, outcasts, and revolutionaries. Modern worship appears to be rare, and surviving followers describe Kreiĉiza as distant or absent.

## Worship

The **Shifting Magic** encourages magical experimentation and pushing beyond familiar limits. The **Artisan's Trial** asks a devotee to craft an object containing something of the creator's own identity or spirit.

## Relevance to the Party

The party encountered an **impossible triangle** among the three sigils surrounding the adult Velmian in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Hero's Tapestry|the Hero's Tapestry]]. That symbol matches Kreiĉiza's known sigil.

This places Kreiĉiza alongside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Narvono|Narvono]] in the ancient imagery surrounding Velmian, although what that relationship meant remains unclear.
