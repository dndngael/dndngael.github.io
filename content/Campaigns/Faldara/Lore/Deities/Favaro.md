Favaro is a gentle god of agriculture, fertility, natural abundance, and stewardship. His blessings are associated with successful harvests, healthy livestock, and prosperous communities, but his worship emphasizes caring for the land that provides those gifts.

## Worship

Followers of Favaro are often called **Favarans**, especially among farmers, brewers, and rural communities.

The **Festival of Green** marks the planting season with offerings of seed, while the **Feast of Abundance** celebrates the harvest and traditionally offers the first and best fruits of the field.
