# Solano

> [!infobox]+ Solano
> **Pronounced:** sohl-AH-noh
> **Titles:** God of the Sun, God of Healing
> **Pronouns:** He/Him
> **Domains:** Light, Life, Sun, Radiance, Healing
> **Symbol:** Radiant sun
> **Sacred animals:** Lions, eagles, roosters, snakes
> **Colors:** Gold, white, orange, yellow, red
> **Followers:** Sunbearers

Solano embodies the sun's warmth, healing power, and life-giving light. His worshipers include healers, physicians, farmers, and paladins who carry light into dangerous places.

## Worship

Followers are called **Sunbearers**.

During **the Sun's Blessing**, worshipers gather to provide healing and medical aid. The **Dawn Ritual** is a daily prayer offered to the rising sun for Solano's blessing on the day ahead.

The party encountered Solano's worship in the infirmary at [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]].
