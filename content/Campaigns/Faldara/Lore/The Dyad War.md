# The Dyad War

The Dyad War is the name of an ancient conflict the party encountered through the tapestries and archives of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

From 27's examination of one tapestry, the party learned that **gods fought on both sides of the war**.

The name itself triggered a strange reaction in [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]]. He felt as though he had heard it before, even though he could not remember where.

Later, inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|Tur'Farinil's Library]], Happy discovered that he could read an ancient language and found a book titled **In a Time Before the Dyad War**.

The causes, sides, outcome, and true age of the Dyad War remain largely unknown to the party.
