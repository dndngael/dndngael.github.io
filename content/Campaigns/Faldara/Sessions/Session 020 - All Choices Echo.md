# Session Recap: All Choices Echo

## The Very Short Version

Having already discovered an ancient library full of impossible secrets, the party naturally spent a significant amount of time trying to defeat a wall. After singing at the architecture, rearranging suspicious words, debating whether time itself was broken, and conducting what can generously be called a scientific experiment, they found a pristine courtyard where the stars were wrong and the bugs had apparently stopped participating in reality. Naturally, the solution was to sit down, close their eyes, and accidentally meet someone important.

## Previously

Deep inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], the party had discovered [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|the Great Library]], recovered [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] for [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]], and learned that Happy could read at least some of an ancient language he had no memory of learning.

One problem remained immediately in front of them. The library seemed smaller than the space around it suggested it ought to be.

## What Happened

The party began by thoroughly, and increasingly desperately, searching the Great Library for another way forward.

Their suspicions proved justified when Lheani examined one of the stone walls and realized there was open space beyond it. Somewhere behind the wall was another chamber or passage, but the stone itself was far too thick to simply knock through.

Eventually they dragged Nephil's attention away from Farinia's Codex long enough for him to investigate. The Codex contained an old clue describing an atrium that would yield to a tune beginning at its root.

That immediately brought the party back to the six runestones they had already used to play **D-E-F-A-C-E**.

Playing DEFACE caused the outline of a hidden doorway to glow in the library wall, but the passage still refused to open.

The missing piece was hiding in the Codex.

As Nephil continued listening to it, he discovered words tucked into its margins. Once the party rearranged them alongside the familiar notes, they formed a sentence:

**Don't ever forget. All choices echo.**

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] recognized the words as the missing part of the song he remembered Farinia singing.

The group performed the words with the DEFACE melody. This time, the wall seemed to fold backward like the pages of a book being rapidly turned until the stone vanished entirely.

Beyond it was a hidden staircase.

The stairs emerged into an open-air courtyard the party had never been able to access from elsewhere in the temple. Despite its immense age, the entire place was pristine. Plants were carefully maintained, the stonework showed little sign of weathering, and even the altar appeared untouched.

More strangely, the night sky overhead was wrong.

27 studied the stars and determined that they did not match the sky from the night when the party entered Tur'Farinil. The courtyard appeared to be showing, preserving, or somehow connected to another time.

Other details were equally strange. Small insects stood completely motionless. A sundial remained in the courtyard, but the piece needed to cast its shadow was missing.

At the center stood a large altar similar to one the party had encountered earlier in the temple, except this one held two bowls. One was silver. The other was gold.

Nearby, the party found two buckets of water. When Lheani poured the water into the bowls, each surface perfectly reflected one of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Faldara's Twin Moons|Faldara's twin moons]]. Silver Farin appeared full. Golden Zil appeared as a waning crescent.

Remembering [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|the Apotheon's]] plea, the party began to wonder whether they had finally reached the place where he once prayed to the Spinner.

They experimented with the mask fragment, the bowls, the sundial, and prayer itself. Nothing immediately responded.

They even tested whether time was actually passing differently inside the courtyard. Despite the motionless insects and impossible sky, five minutes experienced outside the courtyard matched five minutes experienced within it.

Velmian said the place felt familiar. Eventually, he reminded the party of something Farinia used to do there.

Meditate.

Lheani tried it first.

While meditating, he became aware of a figure who greeted him and remarked that explaining everything to Lheani alone probably would not make much sense. It would be better to speak with the whole group.

When Lheani opened his eyes, he saw a being in long robes with pointed ears, very long blond hair, and an **hourglass symbol** upon their clothing.

Lheani told everyone else to join him.

One by one, the party began to meditate.

And that is where the session ended.

- The recovered lyric was: **"Don't ever forget. All choices echo."**
- Velmian recognized the completed song.
- Performing the song opened a wall by making it disappear like the turning pages of a book.
- The party finally entered Tur'Farinil's previously unreachable courtyard.
- 27 discovered that the courtyard's stars did not match the current night sky.
- The silver and gold altar bowls reflected Farin and Zil.
- The party performed an actual experiment to determine whether the courtyard was experiencing time dilation.
- Lheani was the first to encounter the mysterious hourglass-marked figure.

## Clues, Rumors, and Questions

- **"Don't ever forget. All choices echo."** appears to be part of an old Farinia-associated song.
- The DEFACE melody can activate more than one mechanism inside Tur'Farinil.
- The courtyard appears connected to another moment in time, although the party found no measurable difference in the passage of time while inside it.
- Farin appeared full in the altar's silver bowl while Zil appeared as a waning crescent in the golden bowl.
- The altar may be the place where the Apotheon once prayed to the Spinner, but this remains unconfirmed.
- The figure encountered through meditation bears Farinia's hourglass symbol. Their identity remains unknown.
