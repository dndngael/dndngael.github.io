# Session Recap: Pre-Festival Games

## Previously

Paul's mask had been stolen shortly after the original trio met in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]]. The group planned to follow up with the Lowgates guard while the festival gathered momentum.

## What Happened

Captain Roseshade told the group that the guard was still searching for Paul's mask and asked them to remain in the Lowgates. Alvin briefly turned Paul invisible to snoop around the guards, but the effort produced no new information.

The day then became a tour of increasingly ridiculous festival competitions.

Paul won a Dragon Cake eating contest after consuming an heroic and medically inadvisable amount of cake, earning the title **The Hurlin' Earl** and meeting [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] and her owl-cat-like companion Torĉ.

At a riddles competition run by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]], the party met [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]] and successfully solved all three puzzles. One riddle drew on [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Faldara's Twin Moons|Faldara's twin moons]], Farin and Zil.

The trio also herded baby Horizonback Tortoises outside the city gates and ended the evening back at [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Silent Falls|Silent Falls]].

## Notable Moments

- Paul earned the title **The Hurlin' Earl** in the most literal possible way.
- Fineks, Torĉ, Astra, and Kaz entered the campaign.
- The party demonstrated that they are significantly better at riddles than at subtle investigation.
- Alvin painted Paul's temporary mask gold in hopes of making it tempting bait.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources

- Paul: Medal of the Dragon Cake.
- Paul, Alvin, and 27: one Medal of Wit each.
- Paul, Alvin, and 27: one Medal of the Horizonback each.

## Quests and Open Threads

### Continued

- Retrieve Paul's Mask.
- Become Champions of the Lowgates.

## Where We Left Off

Paul's real mask was still missing, but the party had accumulated several medals, several new acquaintances, and a rapidly expanding festival reputation.
