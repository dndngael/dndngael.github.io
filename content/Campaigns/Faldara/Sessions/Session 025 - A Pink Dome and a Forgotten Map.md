# Session Recap: A Pink Dome and a Forgotten Map

## The Very Short Version

The party continued its grand journey toward Apeiron, somehow managing to turn a rainy afternoon into a profitable career in creative fiction. Then Paul fell asleep on watch, six dire wolves decided everyone needed to wake up, and Alvin finally remembered he could summon a magical shelter whenever he wanted. Naturally, the night ended with Happy drawing a mysterious map to a city the party had never visited. An entirely ordinary road trip.

## Previously

After leaving [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] traveled south toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], hoping to find passage to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]].

Their journey was interrupted by a burning cabin, hostile spellcasters, and the rescue of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]], a private investigator who shared troubling rumors about Stormwatch Harbor and a possible organization called the Faceless.

With the cabin destroyed and Sev departing, the party resumed its journey.

## What Happened

### The road continues

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] used healing magic to tend to everyone's injuries after the cabin encounter. Once recovered, the travelers continued through the open countryside.

They made camp that evening, with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] lighting the campfire with magic.

Around the fire, 27 became curious about [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] and his mysterious history. He asked how Happy had gotten his name and what he remembered about waking alone in the wilderness.

Happy explained that he had found clothing hanging near a vacation cabin and taken it, having awakened without any clothing of his own. He could not explain why people called him Happy or identify the first person he encountered afterward.

The conversation eventually shifted to Fineks. She explained that she first met Lurvius and Ĵura while they were traveling together, before Astra joined their group. Their travels brought them into contact with Fineks's druidic circle, the **Circle of Embersong**.

Fineks seemed reluctant to discuss much more about her former circle.

With the evening's questions unanswered, everyone settled in for the night.

### A wealthy traveler in the rain

The following morning brought heavy rain. Rather than continue through miserable weather, the party sheltered beneath a large tree, using their equipment to stay relatively dry.

Eventually, an impressive caravan appeared along the muddy road. Armored riders accompanied a supply wagon and an unusually luxurious carriage.

One of the guards approached and politely requested permission for the caravan to shelter nearby.

The travelers agreed.

The carriage belonged to a wealthy merchant who introduced himself as **Vuzeevio**. He was an older, bald man with an impressive gray beard, elaborate clothing, and an enthusiasm for entertaining stories.

Paul, Alvin, Fineks, and Lheani entered the carriage while Happy and 27 remained outside.

Vuzeevio was delighted to have company and asked the adventurers to tell him about their travels.

Naturally, the party told him a completely reliable and entirely truthful story about saving a city from evil, star-robed wizards.

The tale included a famous detective, a heroic resurrection, and a threat to the world itself. Some details were loosely inspired by their recent adventure. Others were considerably more imaginative.

Vuzeevio was entertained regardless.

Fineks even attempted to create a dazzling display of fire and wind to accompany their supposed divine feats. The performance did not quite achieve the intended effect, but the merchant appreciated the effort.

He instructed his guard captain, **Roderick**, to reward the storytellers with five gold pieces each.

Before leaving, Vuzeevio mentioned that he was traveling toward Arbaroŝirm to conduct business selling weapons.

When the party informed him that the city's demonic troubles had been resolved, he laughed in disbelief.

Perhaps he would discover the truth for himself.

### An unwelcome midnight visitor

After continuing their travels, the party established another roadside camp.

Paul took a watch during the night, spending some time writing in his journal. Unfortunately, exhaustion got the better of him.

He fell asleep.

He awoke to something biting his arm.

A pack of **six dire wolves** had slipped into camp.

Paul shouted a warning, waking the others into a rather unpleasant battle.

The wolves attacked fiercely, taking advantage of the party's sleeping arrangements and the confusion of their sudden arrival.

Lheani met them with his greataxe and ancestral spirits, enduring repeated attacks while his companions fought around him.

Happy delivered electrifying punches with his armored gauntlets, while Alvin combined healing, dance, and surprisingly effective unarmed strikes.

Fineks summoned Torĉ and supported the party with restorative magic and her battle fan. Her displeasure at being woken up became increasingly apparent.

Paul fought with rapier and fists, eventually managing to stun one of the surviving wolves.

The battle ended with all six wolves defeated.

Everyone was alive, although considerably less enthusiastic about camping in the wilderness.

### The world's most fashionable fortress

Before the party could settle down again, distant howls echoed across the plains.

Another pack was approaching.

Lheani recognized that the sounds came from some distance away, but could not determine how many wolves were involved.

Rather than risk another battle, Alvin offered a magical solution.

He cast *Leomund's Tiny Hut*, creating a protective dome around the party.

Its appearance?

**Pink. Glittery. Impossible to ignore.**

The group immediately began questioning why they had not been using this spell every night.

The answer remained unclear.

The second wolf pack, consisting of approximately seven wolves, eventually arrived. The animals investigated the mysterious dome, sniffed around the campsite, and examined the bodies of the fallen wolves.

Unable to enter the magical shelter, they eventually departed.

For once, the party was allowed to sleep.

### A map from nowhere

During the final watch, Happy passed the time drawing in the dirt.

He began with ordinary sketches of his surroundings and ideas for his armor. Then, almost without thinking, he started drawing unfamiliar shapes.

The scattered lines slowly became something recognizable.

A map.

It appeared to show a coastal city, several prominent structures, and a particular building marked with a circle.

Happy did not understand what he had drawn, although something about it seemed familiar.

When 27 noticed the drawing, he recognized the city immediately.

It was **Stormwatch Harbor**.

He identified several landmarks, including the **Dawn Spire**, the **Church of Solano**, and the **Temple of the Deep**.

But one detail caught his attention.

Happy had marked a particular building with a circle.

27 asked whether Happy knew what it was.

Happy did not.

Neither could explain why he had drawn a map of a place the party was still traveling toward.

With a new mystery accompanying them, the adventurers prepared to begin the fifth day of their journey.
