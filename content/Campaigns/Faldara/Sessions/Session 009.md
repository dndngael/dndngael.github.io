# Session Recap: Session 9

## Previously

The party was still traveling toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]], with Dawn's Bane moving along the same route at least some of the time.

## What Happened

The travelers regrouped at the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Mistwind Caravan|Mistwind Caravan]]. Alvin found a dark cloak with a sparkling, iridescent interior and desperately wanted it.

To raise money, the group put on a performance. Alvin performed under the stage name **The Twinkler**, Fineks considered helping despite some reluctance about making a spectacle of herself, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] used the audience as an opportunity to sell masks.

The performance raised 25 gp and improved morale enough that the cloak's seller reduced the price to 50 gp. Alvin acquired what would become known as the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Really Cool Cloak|Really Cool Cloak]].

Happy also met Ĵura and decided to travel with SPP3 4eva rather than remain only a passing acquaintance.

The expanded group later fought a shambling mound, shared stories around the campfire, and settled in for a long rest.

## Notable Moments

- Alvin formally became **The Twinkler**.
- The Really Cool Cloak joined the campaign.
- Happy joined the party's journey.
- Fineks showed that even she has limits on how much public spectacle she wants to participate in.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources

- 25 gp earned from the performance.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Really Cool Cloak|Really Cool Cloak]] purchased for 50 gp.

## Where We Left Off

The road party was now larger, with Happy joining the adventure as the journey toward Arbaroŝirm continued.
