# Session Recap: Champions of the Lowgates, Vision about The Apotheon

## Previously

The party had reached the final festival challenge in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], racing against [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]].

## What Happened

After a shark smashed through part of the grotto, the party followed a newly opened tunnel toward a golden light. Beyond it they found a ruined cavern containing ancient columns and a crystal pedestal. On the pedestal rested a red-and-gold object on a fine golden chain.

When [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] touched it, Paul, Alvin, and 27 received the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto|vision]] that would change the direction of the campaign. A masked figure calling himself the [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Apotheon|Apotheon]] pleaded for rescue from imprisonment and darkness.

Dawn's Bane found the group unconscious afterward. They had not received the vision, but members of the group said the voice sounded strangely familiar.

The two parties cooperated through the remaining dangers of the grotto, including carnivorous moss. You Guys emerged with the Lazuli Eye and became the year's **Champions of the Lowgates**.

The next morning, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz|Umbral Kaz]] met with the group. He recognized the hidden site as an ancient prayer place of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Narvono|Narvono]], identified the "Spinner" from the vision as [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]], and suggested the party investigate [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]].

## Notable Moments

- The party became Champions of the Lowgates.
- The Apotheon mystery began.
- The party found the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Mask Piece|mask fragment]].
- Dawn's Bane recognized something familiar in the Apotheon's voice.
- Kaz connected the hidden shrine to Narvono and the Spinner to Farinia.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources

- One free night each at [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Silent Falls|Silent Falls]].
- Level up to level 4.
- The mysterious mask fragment from the hidden prayer site.
