# Session Recap: A Study in Shared Brain Cells

## Previously

Farinia's riddle seemed to require three people: one who was lost, one who was blind, and one who believed. The party suspected [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] might be the lost one, but the other pieces were still missing.

## What Happened

The party explored more of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] while trying to interpret the riddle. After an uncomfortable but productive conversation with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]], Seren pointed them toward a blind scholar from the Institute who spent time near the temple.

That scholar was [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil Salŝius]]. Nephil studies magical **frequencies** and uses a staff that helps him perceive resonance in ways sighted people cannot. He could hear a strange humming from deep within [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]] and agreed to enter the temple with the party if they protected him.

The group made a brief reconnaissance trip into the temple with Nephil before returning to the city.

On the way, they met [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen Nemyris]], a street preacher speaking about the two moons, "moon children," and the **Twinlight**. She carried a dark necklace with a pulsing red stone that she called a **Moon's Tear**.

Faylen invited Happy and Alvin to meet her privately that night.

The party also discovered signs that Dawn's Bane was having problems of its own. Lurvius was absent, while Fineks appeared deeply unwell.

Seeking guidance, the group visited [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]] and communed with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Favaro|Favaro]]. The answers were carefully worded: many people had trusted Faylen, and Faylen herself believed the stone came from the moon. Neither answer actually confirmed that her beliefs were correct.

## Notable Moments

- The party found the likely "blind one" from Farinia's riddle: Nephil.
- Nephil introduced the idea of magical frequencies and resonance.
- Faylen and the Twinlight mystery entered the campaign.
- 27 revealed that he had been sent to seek a prophesied hero named **Zero**.
- Fineks's worsening condition became difficult to ignore.
