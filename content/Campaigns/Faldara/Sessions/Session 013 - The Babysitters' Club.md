# Session Recap: The Babysitters' Club

> [!note]
> The surviving legacy file for this session contains extensive encounter preparation but only a short record of the final outcome. This recap intentionally preserves only what the actual-play record supports.

## Previously

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen Nemyris]] had invited Happy and Alvin to a private nighttime meeting after speaking about the Twinlight, the moons, and the creatures coming from [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

## What Happened

The planned midnight rendezvous took place, and Faylen attempted to show the group more of what she believed about the creatures she called **moon children**.

Creatures were summoned during the meeting. Whatever possibility there may have been for a peaceful demonstration did not last. A fight broke out, and the party was expelled from the meeting.

The night immediately became dangerous again when the group was drawn into another combat encounter. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]] ultimately had to intervene and save them.

## What Changed

- Faylen's relationship with the party became much more complicated.
- The Twinlight Wake was no longer merely a strange street-preaching curiosity.
- The creatures from Tur'Farinil remained at the center of a disagreement over whether they were monsters, victims, or something more complicated.
- Seren once again found himself dealing with the consequences of the party's choices.
