# Session Recap: Traveling to Arbaroŝirm

## Previously

After becoming Champions of the Lowgates and receiving the Apotheon's vision, the group decided to travel toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] in search of the temple Kaz believed might be connected to their mystery.

## What Happened

The party secured horses and rations with help from Paul's connections and began the journey out of Vualio.

The road quickly became unsettling. An ill omen appeared during the first night, followed by a fight with a will-o'-wisp. The moon Farin also disappeared from the sky on two consecutive nights, adding another strange celestial event to the journey.

Between dangers, Alvin won a stone-tossing game.

Later the party heard what seemed to be a bear, only to be attacked by a gloomstalker. Paul was brought down during the fight, and the session ended in the middle of the danger.

## Notable Moments

- The journey to Arbaroŝirm properly began.
- Farin disappeared on two nights in a row.
- Alvin won the road's stone-tossing contest.
- A gloomstalker attack left Paul downed at the cliffhanger.
