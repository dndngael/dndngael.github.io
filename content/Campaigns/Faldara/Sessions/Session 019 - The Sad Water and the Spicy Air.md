# Session Recap: The Sad Water and the Spicy Air

## Previously

The party had uncovered tapestries linking [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]] to the ancient name Velmian, discovered signs of two additional altars, and cleared a kobold nest deeper inside [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

## What Happened

Looting the kobold room produced another runestone and a small kobold doll, but the temple still had several hazards between the party and its deeper secrets.

A bridge crossed a pool of magical water that reflected more than faces. Characters who looked into it were confronted with cruelly personal versions of their own fears and insecurities. The effect eventually faded, leaving the water clear.

Another chamber was filled with poisonous "spicy air" rising through floor grates. The group endured the fumes long enough to free a stubborn lever and reduce the hazard.

A later puzzle involved four pillars ticking at different tempos and overhead strings that changed their rhythm. Matching the tempos produced another runestone, but the party eventually realized they were still missing part of a larger musical sequence.

That realization triggered extensive backtracking. A previously overlooked note and the graffiti on a defaced statue finally revealed the answer: the collected runestones spelled **D-E-F-A-C-E**. Playing the sequence opened a staircase into the lower temple.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] appeared after hearing the song and said it was one Farinia used to sing.

Below, the party found [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library|the Great Library of Tur'Farinil]], untouched for an extraordinary length of time. Hidden archives lay behind the shelves. Happy discovered that he could partially read an ancient language no one else understood, and the group found two books connected to the era before the Dyad War.

Most importantly, they recovered [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]], completing Nephil's main objective.

The group then brought Velmian back to the tapestry room and compared him with the ancient images. The child-spirit, the grown hero named Velmian, and Lurvius all appeared connected by the same face.

## Notable Moments

- The runestones formed the musical sequence **DEFACE**.
- Velmian recognized the song as one Farinia used to sing.
- The hidden Great Library was discovered.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] was recovered.
- Happy discovered he can read an ancient language despite having no memory of learning it.
- The Velmian/Lurvius resemblance became impossible to dismiss.
