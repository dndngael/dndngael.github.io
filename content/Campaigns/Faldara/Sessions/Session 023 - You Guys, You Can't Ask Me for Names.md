# Session Recap: You Guys, You Can't Ask Me for Names

## The Very Short Version

After spending what felt like several lifetimes in Tur'Farinil, the party finally accomplished something generations of soldiers had failed to do. Naturally, the next priorities were interrogating everyone's backstories, frightening a ghost child, and using a random wheel to decide what name should appear in the king's report. Somehow **"You Guys, You Can't Ask Me for Names"** became the official name of the heroes of Arbaroŝirm. Oh, and Farinia revealed the Apotheon's identity, Happy acquired a portable ghost child, and the next adventure may involve an ocean that is on fire.

## Previously

In [[Campaigns/Faldara/Sessions/Session 022 - One Ass to Save the City|Session 22]], the party defeated the immediate danger left behind by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]]. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] shattered her larger Moon's Tear. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] used a staff and Alvin's smaller stone to close the rift, but the smaller stone disappeared. Before leaving [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], the adventurers still wanted to show [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]] the ancient tapestries.

## What Happened

### The ancient hero and the familiar face

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] remembered taking the hidden route shared with them, entering a chamber with a dark pool, and then hearing something strange. Their memory of what happened afterward remained unclear. The party explained how Faylen had brought Lurvius, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Ĵura Senkret|Ĵura]] into the battle under an influence and how the adventurers brought them back to themselves.

At the tapestries, Lurvius could hardly deny that the ancient hero named **Velmian** looked like him. He could not explain why. Asked about his face, he revealed that one eye was blind and badly scarred after a betrayal by someone he had considered a dear friend more than twenty years earlier. He did not wish to say much more.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] also revisited his own mysteries. He recalled waking in the woods about twenty years ago with no memory of his earlier life, even though he appears to be around forty. In the temple, he had somehow been able to read an ancient language no one else understood. The party proposed any number of theories about forgotten identities and old connections. Answers remained elusive.

The child-shaped spirit [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] appeared as well, though he was frightened by Lheani and hid behind Happy. Velmian also said he had been feeling unwell. Paul discovered he could touch the child's shoulder rather than passing straight through him.

### The night after the dungeon

At last, the party left the temple. As it was late, they accepted Lurvius's invitation to stay with his friend Carl. They finally slept in real beds and woke to breakfast and the novelty of an ordinary morning.

The conversation ranged from Happy's past and Paul's responsibilities to Lheani's homeland and religious traditions. Lheani also admitted discomfort with the group's decision to kill Faylen. Dawn's Bane continued to recover from its ordeal.

### A Grand Duke sees the impossible

The adventurers gathered the available members of Dawn's Bane and went to report their victory. Soldiers had noticed something remarkable: no new monster attacks. Still, the claim that the rift had closed seemed almost impossible to believe.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]] accompanied the party back into the temple to see for himself. The group led him through previously concealed passages and into Faylen's former workshop, where the rift had once stood.

Seren was overwhelmed. His family had spent generations defending [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] against the horrors from Tur'Farinil. Facing the empty place where the rift had been, he fell to his knees. For the first time, the burden his family had inherited appeared to be lifted.

He promised to tell the king what the party had accomplished and awarded **100 gp to each member of You Guys**.

Unfortunately, he also needed the heroes' official name.

The party debated names, including *Time Lords*, before delegating this monumental decision to a random wheel. It selected Happy's suggestion: **You Guys, You Can't Ask Me for Names**. Seren, emotionally exhausted and in no position to argue, wrote it down. The shorter name **You Guys** would do for everyday use.

### Farinia's answer

The group had scarcely escaped its latest round of questions when [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] appeared with child Velmian.

The Spinner explained that they had made the ancient tapestries and finally clarified their meaning: **the hero Velmian depicted in them was the Apotheon**. The ghost child beside Farinia was only one piece of that imprisoned hero. The mask fragment from the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], currently carried by [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]], was another.

Farinia asked Happy to hold out his hand. Velmian became a magical thread that wrapped around Happy's wrist, forming a bracelet. Happy now carried another piece of the Apotheon. The group asked whether the child could still speak, but Farinia did not answer.

Farinia urged them to seek the remaining pieces and said more of Velmian's past would become clear on their travels. They also stated that Lurvius was not the imprisoned Apotheon, but did not explain his striking resemblance to the hero.

### A clue for Fineks

Back in Arbaroŝirm, the party found [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] speaking with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]]. He had been studying [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]] and discovered an account of a particular island associated with **Favaro's thumbprint**, along with the sequence **1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13**.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized the familiar pattern in nature immediately. Nephil suspected that the island's connection to Favaro might offer a path toward restoring Fineks's soul song, currently suppressed by the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]] in her mask. It was a possibility, not a guaranteed cure.

27 proposed traveling to his homeland, **Apeiron**, believing Fineks might be the prophesied Zero who could help save it. Fineks admitted she had initially thought this idea rather ridiculous, but Nephil's discovery finally offered a lead she could pursue for herself. She agreed to accompany the party, intending to discuss her plans with Lurvius first. Happy welcomed her as a temporary member of You Guys.

27 described their route south toward **Stormwatch Harbor**, where they would need to find a ship for Apeiron. There were ominous sailor rumors of violent storms and seawater catching fire, the so-called **Burning Sea**. The group was excited, alarmed, or both.

27 also explained how his homeland categorizes souls by numbers and made deeply unsettling remarks about the treatment of supposedly irrational numbers. The others had questions. Many questions.

Before departing, the party agreed to shop, celebrate its achievement, and prepare for the journey.

## Notable Moments

- **Lurvius's scar:** His blind eye came from a betrayal by someone once close to him.
- **Happy's forgotten history:** His twenty-year memory gap and unexplained ancient literacy became topics of serious speculation, along with a good deal of nonsense.
- **Astra and the tapestries:** The artist was understandably displeased to learn the party had contemplated cutting pieces from priceless ancient art.
- **Seren's reaction:** A rare quiet moment as the Grand Duke realized the threat that defined generations of his family might actually be gone.
- **The naming wheel:** An official heroic name settled by chance and immediately regrettable to every royal clerk who will have to write it.
- **Velmian's bracelet:** A fragment of an imprisoned hero is now around Happy's wrist.
- **Favaro's thumbprint:** A mathematical pattern in the Codex provides the first promising new lead for Fineks.
- **A burning sea:** The next adventure may be very wet, very hot, or both.

## Clues, Rumors, and Questions

- Farinia identifies the ancient Velmian as the Apotheon. The ghost child and the Lazuli Grotto mask fragment are pieces of him.
- Lurvius looks strikingly like the ancient hero, but Farinia says he is not the imprisoned Apotheon.
- Happy's forgotten life and ability to read an ancient language remain unexplained.
- Nephil believes an island linked to Favaro's thumbprint might help Fineks.
- It is not established that Fineks truly is Zero, or that the island will cure her.
- Reports of burning seawater near Stormwatch Harbor remain rumors.
- The smaller stone that vanished into the rift remains missing.
