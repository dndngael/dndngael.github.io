# Session Recap: The Keeper's Moths

## The Very Short Version

Apparently traveling south was getting too straightforward, so the party spent several nights debating moth surveillance, murdered a suspicious insect, and then interrogated another one. Following their tiny informant led them to an ancient forest and a very clear warning to mind their own business. They actually listened, which was impressive, right up until they tried to rescue a stranger from under a tree and discovered that the stranger was bait. So much for a quiet road trip.

## Previously

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] were traveling toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], hoping to find passage to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]. Following a dire-wolf attack, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] had drawn a surprisingly accurate map of the harbor without knowing why, including one [[Campaigns/Faldara/Threads/Find the Circled Building|circled building]] no one could identify. The party began Session 26 on roughly the fifth day of its expected sixteen-day journey.

## What Happened

### Long days and restless nights

The travelers continued south, passing time with increasingly elaborate discussion of hypothetical pickle-based spellcasting. Rain, sleep schedules, rations, and the unpleasant prospect of exhaustion proved rather more practical problems.

During one night watch, several moths gathered near the campfire. Nothing seemed unusual at first. On a later watch, however, Paul noticed something strange: two of the moths bore precisely painted **purple circles** on their pale backs. They hovered differently from the others, almost as though they were observing the camp. Paul killed one of the marked moths and examined it. The marking remained after its death, and the party concluded it did not look like a natural pattern.

Further down the road, the group found an old, congealed pool of blood beside tracks suggesting something large had been caught or injured there. They did not establish what had happened and continued their journey.

### An extremely suspicious moth

At another camp, Fineks noticed more marked moths around the fire. She drew one toward herself with carefully controlled flame and managed to catch it alive. Bringing the creature inside Alvin's magical hut proved impossible, so she waited outside with it.

The night became more complicated when **two owlbears** approached and investigated the hut. Fineks hid in the grass with her captive moth while the others stayed within the magical shelter. Eventually the owlbears lost interest and moved on.

When the immediate danger had passed, Happy cast *Detect Magic* on the moth and perceived a faint purple **divination** aura. The suspicion that someone was watching the party through these creatures suddenly seemed much more reasonable.

Happy then remembered that he had a **Ring of Animal Influence** and used it to speak with the captive moth. The creature explained, in its own limited fashion, that it had been told to watch the area by someone it called **the Keeper**. It described the Keeper as a large, two-eyed being rather than another moth, said there was one Keeper, and indicated that other travelers had been watched as well.

The party asked the moth to lead them to its Keeper and released it.

### The forest boundary

Following the marked moth took the party toward an old forest with towering trees and great numbers of moths. There was a conspicuous line between the grassland and the trees, almost like a boundary.

Happy peered into the darkness without crossing that line and glimpsed eyes looking back before they disappeared. Fineks examined a nearby tree and found signs of deliberate arrangements to attract moths.

An arrow suddenly struck the ground near Fineks. An unseen voice instructed the travelers to turn away.

The speaker called herself **[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Keeper|the Keeper]]**. She warned the group not to enter the forest, claiming that she had watched its boundary for hundreds of years and that those who went inside might become lost on its paths forever. The party could not see the speaker well enough to establish who or what she was.

Rather than force the issue, the adventurers backed away and resumed their journey. The marked moths, the Keeper's identity, and the truth of her warning remained unsettled.

### The road springs a trap

After resting and recovering from the exhaustion accumulated on the road, the party traveled for several more uneventful days. They gathered **15 gp worth of herbalism supplies**, though no potion was finished. By approximately the twelfth travel day, they encountered a more wooded stretch of road.

A halfling appeared to be pinned beneath a fallen tree, pleading for help. Members of the party worked together to move the trunk, and Fineks attempted healing magic.

Something was wrong. The magic had no effect, and Happy noticed that the supposed injured man showed no blood. The halfling smiled and **vanished**, revealing himself to have been an illusion.

Armed figures emerged from the surrounding woods.

The trap had been sprung, and the party rolled initiative.

## Notable Moments

- A long-running argument about a hypothetical pickle spell became the preferred roadside entertainment.
- Paul's response to apparent moth surveillance was to stab a moth.
- Fineks captured a marked moth alive and stayed outside the magical hut with it while two owlbears investigated.
- Happy's Ring of Animal Influence turned an insect into a very reluctant source of information.
- The party respected the Keeper's warning rather than forcing entry into her forest.
- The group's attempt to help an injured halfling ended with the discovery of a staged ambush.

## People Encountered

- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/The Keeper|The Keeper]]: An unseen speaker who claimed to guard the forest boundary and warned the group not to cross it.
- **The apparent halfling victim**: A figure beneath a fallen tree who vanished when his illusion was recognized.
- **Unknown roadside attackers**: Armed people who emerged after the illusion failed. Their identities and motives remain unclear.

## Places Visited

- **The southern road**: Campsites, a suspicious bloodstain, and continued travel toward Stormwatch Harbor.
- **The moth-filled forest boundary**: A densely wooded area where the party followed a marked moth, heard the Keeper's warning, and chose to leave.
- **A wooded stretch of road**: The site of the deceptive halfling rescue and the ensuing ambush.

## Clues, Rumors, and Questions

- Why do some moths bear carefully painted purple circles and behave as though watching the party?
- Who is the Keeper, and how does she use divination magic?
- Are the forest's supposedly endless, disorienting paths real?
- Who prepared the illusion of the trapped halfling, and why did they target travelers?
- The building circled on Happy's map remains unidentified. Nothing from this session establishes a connection between that map and the moths.
