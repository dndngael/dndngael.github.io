# Session Recap: One Ass to Save the City

## The Very Short Version

Faylen was dead, a magical stone was drifting toward a hole in reality, and the party had only a handful of turns to prevent whatever the fuck was about to happen. Naturally, this became an extensive tactical symposium on whether **Paul's ass could save Arbaroŝirm**, featuring discussions of clenching strength, magical attunement, interior dimensions, and whether the stone had a flared base. Somehow, between the butt-related research and the occasional actual good idea, the party smashed the dangerous stone, learned what Faylen had really been working on, and **closed the rift**. The city may someday build them a statue. There are several reasons it should not be anatomically accurate.

## Previously

In [[Campaigns/Faldara/Sessions/Session 021 - Unmake Faylen, Go|Session 21]], the party finally defeated [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen Nemyris]] and brought Lurvius, Ĵura, and Astra back to themselves. Happy ended up holding Faylen's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Moon's Tear|Moon's Tear]], and the surviving Vesperaŝ seemed strangely interested in its new owner.

Faylen was dead. The rift, unfortunately, had not received the memo.

## What Happened

### First, a very normal magical emergency

The session opened with the group attempting to remember exactly what had happened last time. The party was in the middle of sorting out who had taken which stone, whether the Vesperaŝ had actually followed anyone's orders, and where [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] had disappeared to. Nephil, it turned out, was behind a concealed stone wall. Or, as the table immediately diagnosed it, **behind a paywall**.

Then the actual problem asserted itself.

The Moon's Tear floated up out of [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]]'s hands and started moving toward the open rift. It wasn't moving particularly fast, but it was moving *steadily*, and nothing suggested it planned to stop for a spirited group discussion.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] grabbed it. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] tried to help. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] turned into an ape to contribute the traditional problem-solving technique known as **more muscle**. The stone kept going, carrying its would-be rescuers along with it.

The party calculated how far it had to travel and how many turns remained. This was good, practical thinking. It was also the last time the conversation would resemble anything from a respectable emergency-response manual.

### The Ass Plan

At some point, someone suggested using Paul himself as the tool for stopping the stone. If he could tense every muscle, the others could grab him and get better leverage. This began as a reasonably stupid idea.

Then someone suggested he stick his ass out.

From there, the conversation achieved escape velocity.

Could Paul clench hard enough to stop the Moon's Tear? Was his monk training sufficient preparation? Could the stone magically attune to his lower intestine? Could his ass function as a Bag of Holding? Would the stone get lost in the darkness? Did it have darkvision? Would it require a map of Paul's internal tunnels? Was there oil available? **Did the stone have a flared base?**

These were not revelations about Faldara's cosmology. These were actual subjects of debate at the table while an ominous magical artifact drifted toward a tear in reality.

The proposed insertion procedure, mercifully, remained hypothetical. The very real plan was to grab onto Paul and use him for leverage, with Paul proudly offering an assortment of limbs and other potential handholds. The party's collective effort produced some excellent imagery and absolutely no meaningful reduction in the stone's speed.

Someone suggested Paul could save the city with his ass. Someone else proposed that the resulting legend might give him a flying speed. The table was laughing so hard that continuing to play became a challenge in its own right. Emily actually declared, **"This is the best session we've ever had."** Gael announced that their head hurt from laughing. It was, by any reasonable standard, a triumph of collaborative storytelling.

And somewhere in the middle of all this, **the stone was still moving**.

### The actual magic, featuring a monk who refused to stop punching things

While everyone was busy inventing new reasons to never tell this story to the city guard, 27 remembered that resonance had been the key to several of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]]'s mysteries. He urged [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] to focus on the rift's magical frequency rather than the increasingly doomed group-strength check.

Alvin succeeded. He could recognize the rift's distinct magical frequency, even if he couldn't immediately make it obey him.

As the stone approached, **silver and gold threads of energy** began appearing between the rift and the stones. One silver thread passed through or along Paul without him feeling a thing. More strands appeared as the party experimented. They looked significant. They were also completely uninterested in explaining themselves.

Paul experimented with doing burpees while involved in the crisis, tried putting his head against the rift, and eventually returned to the thing he understood best: **hitting the problem until the problem respected him**. The rift itself felt like an invisible barrier. The moving stone, however, could actually be struck.

Lheani pulled out his warhammer. Paul unloaded punches. Cracks began to spread across Faylen's Moon's Tear.

There was a brief but passionate disagreement about whether a rock that was simply floating toward a portal counted as a hostile creature for the purposes of Rage. The stone's position was that it had never attacked anyone and was just minding its own ominous business.

Meanwhile, Happy had the deeply inconvenient realization that they were standing in **Faylen's magical workspace**, full of potentially useful shit. Happy went to investigate, and 27 followed. Among the research equipment, they found a staff with a magical setting that could hold a stone. When 27 placed the smaller stone Faylen had previously given Alvin into the staff, it burned his hand. Alvin tried using the staff to resonate with the rift, but there was no immediate breakthrough.

So the punching continued.

Eventually, Lheani landed a mighty final swing. Faylen's floating Moon's Tear shattered in midair.

**The environment had been defeated.** As the table triumphantly put it, they had *killed the environment*.

One small issue remained.

The rift was **still open**.

### Looting the evil moon lady's laboratory

With the immediate threat no longer trying to drag everyone across the room, the party had a chance to investigate. Happy took Faylen's beautiful silver-and-gold mask. The red veins that had previously glowed across it were now gone, prompting the entirely fair theory that Faylen's mask had simply run out of RGB lighting. Truly, a fallen gamer.

Happy and 27 began sorting through Faylen's messy notes and celestial charts. They found research into how jagged, pitch-black [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]] might be transformed into a smoother, almost breathing stone like the Moon's Tear, with the rift supplying power for the process.

More importantly, her notes made one thing clear: **Faylen had been trying to make the rift bigger.** She wanted an amplifier because her own magic wasn't strong enough to enlarge it. She hadn't been looking for instructions on how to close the damn thing.

Naturally, someone suggested finding a *smallifier* instead.

The party also helped themselves to the magical supplies she had left behind, including several potions and two powerful rings. After all, it would have been irresponsible to leave valuable items lying around in an abandoned murder laboratory.

### How to close a rift, and how not to get rich doing it

Freed from turn-by-turn panic, the party continued experimenting. Alvin tried resonating with the rift and requesting money from it. The attempted **infinite money glitch** failed. The interdimensional hole had no intention of financing the adventuring lifestyle.

Paul tested the barrier with his body and fists. The barrier remained unconvinced. The group also retrieved Nephil from behind the concealed wall, where he had been studying [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]]. Nephil explained that the temple stood in a place of unusual magical power, but even he didn't have a convenient *Close Portal* button hidden in his scholarly pockets.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]] was around to witness the investigation and seemed understandably baffled by some of the party's methods. Lurvius, Ĵura, and Astra were still recovering from their ordeal with Faylen.

At last, the party decided to try **putting Alvin's smaller stone directly into the rift** using the staff.

Paul took the staff, and the others held onto him in a chain so he wouldn't be lost along with it. Given everything that had happened earlier, this was a remarkable return to the concept of *using Paul as a handle*, now with significantly less discussion of his anatomy.

Paul pressed the stone into the rift, then withdrew the staff.

The setting was empty. **The smaller stone was gone.**

For one horrible second, the rift grew brighter.

Then it dimmed.

Then it shrank.

And finally, after an entire session of violence, resonance, questionable physics, and extremely detailed butt-related hypotheticals, **the rift closed**.

Holy shit. They actually did it.

### The eternal curse of never leaving this temple

With the crisis over, the party searched Faylen's body and the chamber, distributed or debated their new treasures, and remembered that they still wanted to discuss the mysterious tapestries with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]].

There was also a proposal to set the tapestries on fire, because apparently solving the immediate apocalypse had not exhausted everyone's urge to destroy the scenery. No tapestry-burning actually occurred.

By the end of the night, Emily even made sure **all the ass stuff had been recorded** for posterity. Historians of Faldara, please forgive us.

Several people began expressing the sincere wish to get the hell out of Tur'Farinil. They had found a route connecting the chamber to the earlier pool area and the outside. But the tapestry question remained, and the party hadn't actually left by the time the session ended.

The rift was gone. The dungeon, unfortunately, was not.

## Notable Moments

- **The Ass Plan:** Paul was nominated as a multipurpose emergency tool, with prolonged debate over clenching, magical attunement, internal cartography, and whether the Moon's Tear was appropriately shaped for the proposed maneuver. The discussion was a joke, not an action the party actually carried out.
- **The world's least effective strength contest:** Ape-form 27, Lheani, Paul, and friends all tried to stop a slowly floating rock. The rock won every round of tug-of-war.
- **Monk versus geometry:** Paul considered burpees, punched the Moon's Tear repeatedly, and introduced his face to the rift's invisible barrier.
- **A genuinely excellent hit:** Lheani shattered the Moon's Tear with his warhammer, earning the table's triumphant declaration that they had killed the environment.
- **The smart people remember the laboratory:** Happy and 27 finally investigated the workspace, found the staff, and uncovered Faylen's research.
- **A disappointing business opportunity:** Alvin attempted to ask the rift for money. No money was forthcoming.
- **The actual save:** Paul used the staff and the smaller stone to close the rift, with the others holding onto him to keep him safe.
- **The dungeon that will not end:** Even after saving the day, the party found itself debating tapestries, loot, and whether it was ever going to see the outside world again.

## Clues, Rumors, and Questions

- **Resonance matters:** Alvin successfully recognized the rift's magical frequency, although matching it did not grant automatic control.
- **The threads remain a mystery:** Silver and gold strands appeared between the rift and the stones. Their purpose, origin, and possible connection to Farinia remain unconfirmed.
- **Faylen wanted expansion, not closure:** Her notes focused on refining Umbracite and gaining enough power to enlarge the rift using an amplifier.
- **Two stones, two very different endings:** Lheani destroyed the large moving Moon's Tear. The smaller stone Alvin had been carrying vanished when Paul inserted it into the rift with the staff.
- **The larger mysteries survived:** The Apotheon's plea, the meaning of the tapestries, and the resemblance involving Lurvius and Velmian are still unresolved.
