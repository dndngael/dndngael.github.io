# Session Recap: The Rift Crisis

> [!note]
> The surviving record establishes that [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]] had already been defeated before this session began. The archival notes for Sessions 20-21 do not reliably preserve the fight itself, so this recap begins with the confirmed aftermath.

## Previously

Faylen had been defeated in the rift chamber, but the rift itself remained open.

## What Happened

After Faylen's defeat, a mysterious stone associated with the Vesperaŝ began moving toward the open rift.

The stone lifted away and began drifting steadily toward the portal. The party entered a frantic environmental struggle to stop it before it reached the rift.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] grabbed onto the stone and tried to physically hold it back. The others piled into the effort, using Paul as the nearest available point of leverage, but brute force did not stop its movement.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] recognized that the temple's repeated emphasis on resonance might offer a better answer and directed [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] toward the stone and rift's magical frequency.

Alvin succeeded spectacularly. He tuned himself to the rift and learned that it has a distinct magical frequency that can be perceived and potentially interacted with.

As the stone continued moving, a **silver thread of energy** emerged from the rift and connected to the stone through or along Paul. The thread appeared intangible and Paul could not feel it, but the others could see it.

The party ended the session still in the middle of the crisis, with the stone continuing toward the rift and no confirmed solution yet.

## Notable Moments

- Faylen was confirmed dead before the session's main crisis began.
- The stone resisted repeated attempts to stop it through strength alone.
- Alvin successfully identified the rift's magical frequency.
- A visible silver thread revealed a direct magical connection between the rift and the stone.
- Paul became an unwilling but highly committed handle.

## Clues, Rumors, and Questions

- The rift is not simply a passive tear. It has a magical resonance that can be perceived.
- The stone is being actively drawn toward the rift.
- The silver thread suggests that the stone and rift are linked, but the nature of that link is unknown.
- The Vesperaŝ that had been involved in the preceding conflict were no longer present in the chamber.
