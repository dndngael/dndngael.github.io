# Session Recap: A Fish, Two Faiths, and a Questionable Rope

## The Very Short Version

Only You Guys could turn a fairly ordinary walk to the coast into divine correspondence by fish, religious networking by injured wolf, and a late-night cattle recovery operation conducted with one companion mostly asleep. They also decided that three hungry grizzly bears were not worth a fight, demonstrating a frankly suspicious level of good judgment. Then somebody gave them a magical rope, and naturally the conversation became much less dignified.

## Previously

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] are heading south toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]], intending to find passage to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]. The harbor might also hold answers about [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|Sev]]'s investigation and the unexplained building on [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]]'s sketch.


## What Happened

### Fish, faith, and a message for a captain

With only a few days left on the road, the group discussed what to do in Stormwatch Harbor. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] still wanted to arrange passage to Apeiron, while the others considered looking into Sev's old office. 27 again expressed his belief that Fineks is the prophesied Zero, though neither the prophecy's deadline nor her actual role is known.

At a well-maintained bridge over a stream, the travelers met **Fisherman Mac**, who was enjoying a good catch. They bought a large perch for **1 sp**. Mac had less reassuring things to say about their destination. As a devotee of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]], he believed the reports of burning seawater reflected his goddess's anger, and blamed the influence of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Solano|Solano]] and his followers. That was Mac's interpretation, not proof of why anything is happening at the harbor.

Mac pointed them toward the **Churning Temple**, a seaside center of Eravona's worship, and asked them to carry a message to **Captain Lannery**, head of the **Churning Church**. Mac said he used to serve aboard her crew and still prayed daily in her honor.

That evening Fineks roasted and ate the perch, only to bite into something that was very much not a fish bone: **a clear blue sapphire** containing what looked like a tiny, moving eye when light struck it. Fineks recognized the image as a sign associated with Eravona, one said to be sent as an invitation to certain people. She pocketed the sapphire and kept her discovery to herself.

### Three bears and one pilgrim

On the following stretch of road, Paul and 27 spotted **three grizzly bears** searching for food. After a surprisingly sensible discussion, the party decided to sneak through the undergrowth rather than start a fight. The bears never noticed them.

Later the group came upon an older woman in white robes tending to an injured wolf. She introduced herself as **[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lucille|Lucille]]**, a pilgrim of Solano associated with the **Tidal Spire** in Stormwatch Harbor. She explained that her religious tradition treats injuries as affronts to Solano, though her way of speaking about the faith surprised some of the group.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] calmed the frightened wolf, allowing Lucille to bandage it and heal its wounds. During their conversation, Lheani mentioned his origins in **New-Doleh** and showed Lucille the painting on his shell. She did not recognize the imagery, but said **Cardinal Pedro**, a Solano cleric at the Tidal Spire who also comes from New-Doleh, might know more.

Lucille handed Lheani a **sun-marked medallion of Solano**. She said it would grant him an invitation into the Tidal Spire, where he would have to pass a trial before being allowed to speak with Cardinal Pedro. She also asked him to tell Pedro that her pilgrimage continues. The Tidal Spire, she explained, is the harbor's enormous lighthouse and should be difficult to miss. Of course, that sounded like a challenge.

### The great cattle caper

During a night watch inside Alvin's pink magical hut, the party observed **three figures** moving through the forest with **six cows and five goats**. The animals seemed calm, so at first there was no reason to intervene. About ten minutes later, a battered, exhausted farmer struggled into view and begged for help. He said three strangers had attacked him and stolen his family's herd, leaving him fearful for their livelihood.

The party healed the farmer and decided to help. That meant waking their sleeping companions, including Fineks, whom Lheani carried as they followed the tracks. They caught up with the three figures and the animals. Alvin overheard one of the strangers whisper that the man accompanying the party looked like the person from whom they had taken the herd.

Rather than fight, the group confronted and intimidated the rustlers. The three backed down, abandoned the animals, and fled. **All six cows and five goats were recovered.**

The grateful farmer offered the party a family heirloom, a **Rope of Climbing**. He described it as a sixty-foot magical rope that can move, fasten itself, and bind a target on command. The party immediately began investigating increasingly questionable uses for it, including whether Paul's shadow-stepping would allow him to escape its grip. The farmer, wisely, went home with his animals rather than join the discussion.

The party returned to rest, continued along the road, and gathered a little more material for herbalism. The recording ends with **one final night's camp before Stormwatch Harbor**.

## Notable Moments

- Fineks found a mysterious Eravona-associated sapphire in a perfectly ordinary-looking perch, then declined to tell the rest of the group.
- The party managed to avoid three hungry grizzlies without any bloodshed or elaborate magical nonsense.
- Lheani's kindness to an injured wolf earned him a possible personal connection to someone from his home region.
- The cattle-rescue mission included a deeply sleepy Fineks being carried along with the rest of the adventurers.
- The table immediately dubbed its new magical rope the **“Rope of Shibari.”** Whether that name is ever going on an inventory sheet is another matter.

- What is the Tidal Spire's trial, and what might Cardinal Pedro know about Lheani's home or the markings on his shell?
- Are the increasing roadside thefts actually a consequence of problems in the harbor, as the farmer believes?
- The relationship, if any, between the **Tidal Spire** and the **Dawn Spire** previously recognized on Happy's sketch has not been established.
