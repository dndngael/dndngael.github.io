# Session Recap: An Autopsy for Jeff

## Previously

Fineks had become increasingly withdrawn and unwell while the party investigated [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], and the strange creatures coming from the rift.

## What Happened

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]] arrived in a panic: [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] had deteriorated badly. She was paranoid, seeing clawed shadows and mouths in the dark, hearing buzzing and whispers, and developing black vein-like marks along her arm.

The small resonating stone associated with Faylen caused an especially violent reaction from Fineks, suggesting that her illness was connected to the same strange magical phenomena surrounding the temple.

The party consulted [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]]. Both concluded that Fineks was not suffering from an ordinary disease. Nephil described the problem as something closer to a psychic infection and remembered ancient accounts of a material capable of causing madness.

Guidance through Favaro pointed the group back toward the place where Fineks had first been injured. That led to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/The Crematorium|the Crematorium]] and its pile of corpses from rift attacks.

After a deeply unpleasant search, the party identified a large horned corpse and named it **Jeff**. An improvised autopsy revealed a jagged, pitch-black crystal where its heart should have been, with dark tendrils extending through the body.

Happy removed the crystal.

The entire corpse pile then animated and attacked.

## Notable Moments

- Fineks's illness was confirmed to be psychic or magical rather than an ordinary disease.
- The party found the first major specimen of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]].
- "Jeff" received a name only shortly before becoming significantly more mobile than a corpse pile should be.
- The session ended with the party trapped inside the Crematorium fighting the animated amalgam.
