# Session Recap: A Symphony of Headaches

## Previously

The party had entered [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], lost Yhefff-Two Ua to a trap, rescued [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]], and reached the next sequence of chambers.

## What Happened

The group entered a chamber containing twelve stone pillars arranged in six pairs. Stepping onto a golden cross activated the room, producing musical tones, dissonance, and, when handled incorrectly, lightning.

The inscription eventually pointed them toward the intended pattern: the pillars had to **sing in pairs**. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] became essential to the solution, using his sensitivity to resonance to identify matching harmonics. Solving the room awarded more of the smooth, rune-marked stones the party had been collecting.

The next chamber contained skulls, guano, several Vesperaŝ in the rafters, and a pristine altar dedicated to **the Spinner**. An inscription asked for a token of the past, a truth from the heart, and a memory retold.

The party eventually realized the altar wanted meaningful stories rather than valuables. Personal objects and memories were offered, including Nephil's story of a promise connected to his deceased brother and 27's nautilus shell and search for **Zero**. The altar returned a key and another runestone.

Beyond the locked door, magical darkness concealed ancient looms and tapestries. One tapestry named an ancient conflict: **[[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Dyad War|the Dyad War]]**. The name triggered an unexplained flash of recognition in [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]].

## Notable Moments

- Nephil's resonance abilities proved essential to the musical puzzle.
- The altar demanded memories and personal truth rather than conventional offerings.
- The party learned the name **the Dyad War**, an ancient conflict in which gods fought on both sides.
- Happy reacted to the name as though he had known it before, despite not knowing why.
- The group continued assembling mysterious musical runestones.
