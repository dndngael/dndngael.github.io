# Session Recap: Stolen Mask

## Previously

This was the beginning of the campaign. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] had only just crossed paths in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Vualio City|Vualio City]].

## What Happened

As people were turning in for the evening near a tavern, a cloaked figure mixed into the crowd, knocked Paul over, and stole his mask.

Paul, Alvin, and 27 chased the thief. During the pursuit they managed to grab a soldier's mask rather than Paul's, while the original thief escaped. They also stopped and questioned another cloaked person nearby, with Alvin using magic to put him to sleep, but found no stolen masks on him.

A guard advised the group to speak with the captain in the morning about the theft.

Without his own mask, Paul obtained a child-sized festival mask shaped like a bear so he would not have to remain publicly unmasked.

## Notable Moments

- The campaign began with the three original party members meeting over a stolen mask.
- Alvin's magic turned an attempted interrogation into an involuntary nap.
- Paul spent the night wearing a child's bear mask.
