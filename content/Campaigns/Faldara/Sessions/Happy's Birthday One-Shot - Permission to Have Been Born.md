# Session Recap: Permission to Have Been Born
*Happy's Birthday One-Shot*

> **A special birthday episode, outside the main campaign's established continuity.** Whether this actually happened, was a shared dream, or was a horrifyingly real bureaucratic operation is a question the Department would prefer you stop asking.

## The Very Short Version

Four adventurers went to sleep. Four adventurers were forcibly summoned to the Department of Birthdays and Other Important Occasions because [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] had apparently neglected to obtain **permission to have been born**. What followed was a craft fair, a vehicular assault, a musical-chairs dating scandal, the attempted murder of an upholstered aristocrat, and the promotion of a fellow adventurer to piñata. The birthday was approved. The friendships, furniture, and relevant paperwork may never recover.

## Previously (Allegedly)

[[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] were camping on the road to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Stormwatch Harbor|Stormwatch Harbor]]. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] was on watch and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] was sound asleep. Those two, through luck or divine intervention, were spared what happened next.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] woke up somewhere else entirely.

## What Happened

### 1. A very serious failure to exist correctly

They awoke in a featureless, aggressively yellow room. There were no doors, no obvious light sources, and no explanation. Naturally, the immediate theories included poisoned rations, death, and Fineks having murdered everyone in their sleep. Excellent investigative work.

Then an envelope appeared in front of Happy.

Its lovely pink seal suggested a party. Its contents suggested an audit.

**NOTICE BD-0** informed the individual *presently identifying as Happy* that the Department had no valid birthday on file. This was a **most serious administrative oversight**. Happy was therefore considered *provisionally uncelebratable* and was summoned, with all associates, to an emergency official celebration. Attendance was mandatory. Fun was specifically requested. Noncompliance risked permanent birthday cancellation.

A pink door materialized under a plaque reading **Temporary Festivity Annex**. The door seemed increasingly eager for Happy to use it. On the other side waited lavender walls, a chandelier, a suspiciously pleasant scent, and an unseen voice that repeatedly reminded everyone:

**“Happiness is mandatory.”**

The hallway also contained a Maintenance door, a door belonging to Madame Buttercream, and a Complaints Department door that turned out to be painted on the wall. The complaints process was going swimmingly.

At last they entered the **Ballroom of Obligatory Merriment**, where a ten-foot, purple-frosted cake stood beneath floating candles and mysteriously self-cleaning confetti.

Happy immediately tried to cut into the cake with an axe.

### 2. The right to have been born must be earned

A tiny, pink-haired pixie in a fabulous dress intervened before the cake could become a crime scene. This was **Madame Buttercream**, the party's relentlessly enthusiastic birthday administrator.

Buttercream presented Happy with an **Application for Permission to Have Been Born**. To have a birthday, Happy and the others needed three official approvals:

1. **Acceptable Gift**
2. **Acceptable Amount of Merriment**
3. **Acceptable Act of Traditional Party Violence**

Nobody asked why the third requirement existed. Everyone was clearly overqualified.

The first stop was the **Gift Assembly Workshop**, where a sign explained that **a gift is not a gift until the recipient has been mildly inconvenienced**. This should have been the clearest possible warning about where the afternoon was headed.

The birthday guests were instructed to build Happy presents by hand. Happy was placed on a throne to judge them.

**Alvin** produced beautifully fitted **sparkling purple slippers** that shed an apparently inexhaustible quantity of glitter. Happy immediately put them on, then used them to contaminate Paul's tail. The glitter was not coming off. The glitter was never going to come off. Alvin, the Twinkler himself, also acquired a very enthusiastic fan in Madame Buttercream, who briefly made his head her personal VIP seating.

**Lheani** remembered a fancy leatherworking swivel knife Happy had admired and attempted to make a version of it. The resulting object was wooden, meticulously painted, not especially functional, and shaped just unfortunately enough to inspire an entirely separate conversation. Happy appreciated the effort, because sometimes the best present is the one you can never throw away without feeling terrible.

**Paul** somehow built Happy an entire **miniature landship**, complete with curtains for privacy. It was large enough for one person, technically moved, made wonderfully convincing engine noises, and required the occupant to propel it with their feet like a child's toy.

Happy climbed in immediately and drove straight into Paul.

**Four bludgeoning damage. No license. No regrets.**

When asked to select the best present, Happy chose Paul's landship. The primary deciding factor appeared to be the opportunity to hurt Paul with it. The **Acceptable Gift** box was stamped.

Buttercream also learned that Paul was an earl and hurriedly bestowed an embarrassingly homemade **Earl Seragon** sash upon him. For one fragile moment, the glitter-covered nobility was treated with respect.

### 3. Musical chairs, but the chairs have rights

Next came the **Chamber of Competitive Merriment**, containing a disco ball, music, and four ornate chairs. A sign warned:

**“The music will stop. The chairs may not.”**

Nobody appreciated the legal significance of that second sentence.

When the music stopped, the chairs dodged everyone's attempts to sit down and *giggled*. The group promptly discovered that the chairs were alive, named, capable of speech, and burdened with emotional needs.

The contestants were introduced to:

- **Dame Upholstria**, an elegant, fiercely dignified chair with standards
- **Lord Sidemus Maximus**, a sturdy aristocrat of considerable cushioning
- **Sir Lazy Boy**, relaxed, slightly reclined, and apparently not interested in anyone's nonsense
- **Gerald**, just Gerald, a polite chair who only wanted someone to ask nicely

Paul attempted to win Dame Upholstria's favor after tackling and sitting on her without warning. She explained that she would appreciate being asked first. Alvin, meanwhile, politely asked Gerald for a seat and received one without a fight.

**A new political philosophy emerged: consent-based musical chairs.**

Then came ball bearings on the dance floor, chairs sprinting away when the lights flickered, desperate negotiations, rejected advances, and a growing number of situations that could only be described as *complicated relationships with furniture*.

Lheani withdrew into his shell and started spinning. Sir Lazy Boy and Lord Sidemus Maximus climbed aboard. Alvin balanced atop the pair in a display of acrobatics that turned a children's party game into an experimental chair-stacking performance. Gerald expressed discomfort about stacking in public, and his boundaries were respected.

For a brief, beautiful moment, the whole room seemed to be learning something about kindness, dignity, and upholstery.

Then Paul drew his rapier.

### 4. The Earl of Terrible Decisions

Paul approached Happy, who was sitting on Dame Upholstria, and **stabbed through the chair into Happy**.

The justification appeared to be that the party needed to complete the Traditional Party Violence requirement. Never mind that they had already run him over with a landship. Never mind that the point of a birthday party was supposedly to celebrate Happy. Never mind that **DAME UPHOLSTRIA HAD FEELINGS**.

She was devastated. Happy was bleeding. The other chairs were furious. Paul continued attacking Happy, apparently determined to make this the most thoroughly documented case of birthday violence in departmental history.

The room split into factions with admirable speed.

Alvin healed Happy. Happy healed Dame Upholstria. Lheani entered a rage and came after Paul. The chairs revealed that their upholstery concealed **actual teeth**, a detail that would have been useful considerably earlier.

Paul tried to survive the consequences of his decisions by casting magical **Darkness**, blinding essentially everyone in the immediate vicinity. It did not stop the furniture.

Dame Upholstria, Lord Sidemus Maximus, Sir Lazy Boy, and Gerald descended upon the earl in a four-chair reckoning. Paul was bitten, grabbed, and eventually knocked unconscious. His Darkness vanished along with his chances of winning any popularity contests.

The surviving guests stabilized him, then tied him up using **all fifty feet of rope**.

A new birthday tradition was born:

**The Earl Piñata.**

Madame Buttercream, who had been taking notes through the carnage, decided that this qualified as sufficient merriment. Alvin was recognized as the winner of musical chairs, which was a somewhat understated prize considering he had just watched the upholstery declare war.

The **Acceptable Amount of Merriment** box was stamped.

### 5. The piñata passes inspection

Back in the main ballroom, a towering, impossibly thin man with a handsome handlebar mustache and the disposition of an audit introduced himself as **Reginald No-Fun**.

He inspected the application. Two stamps were present. One remained.

Then he noticed the unconscious, bound, glitter-coated Paul.

The party explained that Paul had **volunteered** to be Happy's birthday piñata. He was unable to dispute this because he was, at that point, unconscious. Reginald observed that the Department had planned to provide a piñata anyway, so this was technically a cost-saving measure.

He accepted the substitution.

With a stamp of appropriately excessive dimensions, Reginald approved the **Acceptable Act of Traditional Party Violence**.

All three boxes were complete. Happy could finally exist on paper.

There was just one final problem.

**The Department needed Happy's actual date of birth.**

Happy had no idea. The memories simply weren't there. After extensive negotiations about birthdays, calendars, and how much bureaucratic flexibility a birthday emergency could justify, Alvin offered to **share his birthday with Happy**.

Happy loved the idea. A birthday with a friend sounded better than an invented date.

Reginald reluctantly allowed it. The precise translation to Vualio's calendar became its own gloriously unnecessary debate, but the agreement was clear: **Happy and Alvin would share a birthday celebration**.

The application was approved.

The ten-foot cake was finally served. Happy received two enormous slices and promptly deposited one on the unconscious Paul, who remained unavailable for comment. Attempts were made to store extra cake in a shell. The staff considered their work done.

Madame Buttercream said her goodbyes. Even the chairs were sent affectionate farewells, especially Gerald, whose insistence on politely asking permission had changed lives.

### 6. It was just a dream. Probably. Legally speaking.

And suddenly everyone was back in **Alvin's magical hut**.

They were healthy. Paul was no longer tied up. Nobody had stab wounds. No time seemed to have passed. 27 and Fineks remained mercifully outside this entire experience.

So it had obviously been a dream.

Except all four of them remembered it.

And Happy woke up with **the glittery purple slippers still on their feet**.

The group discussed what had happened. Paul claimed not to remember stabbing Happy, apparently hoping that a lack of recollection would function as a complete legal defense. Some of the others were willing to entertain the possibility that he'd forgotten. Happy was not.

Then Paul remarked that it had been a rather good opportunity: **one chair, one person sitting in it, one rapier, two targets**.

Everyone noticed.

Paul insisted this was not an admission of guilt.

The Department has not issued a statement.

## Notable Moments

- Happy was officially labeled **provisionally uncelebratable**, which is a devastating thing to hear from a government office.
- The **Complaints Department** was literally a painting of a door. Frankly, efficient.
- Three thoughtful gifts were exchanged. Naturally, the winner was the tiny landship used to run over its creator.
- Madame Buttercream became an extremely excited fan of **the Twinkler**, while simultaneously having to process Paul as nobility.
- **Gerald** proved that good manners are a competitive advantage, even when you're a chair.
- Lheani became a spinning furniture carousel, and Alvin managed to sit on a stack of sentient chairs without violating their boundaries.
- Paul discovered that a chair's consent to being sat upon is not consent to being stabbed.
- The Department accepted a fellow party member as a substitute piñata, presumably under its emergency procurement authority.
- Happy gained a birthday, and Paul gained the kind of reputation that not even a full-body glitter treatment can improve.

## People Encountered

- **Madame Buttercream:** A tiny, pink-haired pixie who took mandatory joy, stamps, and celebrity dancers very seriously.
- **Reginald No-Fun:** A towering departmental auditor with an enormous stamp and an alarming willingness to approve unconventional piñatas.
- **Dame Upholstria:** An aristocratic chair betrayed by a man who should never again be trusted near upholstered furniture.
- **Lord Sidemus Maximus:** A sturdy, noble chair who eventually joined the anti-Paul coalition.
- **Sir Lazy Boy:** A reclining chair whose day improved considerably when he climbed onto Lheani's rotating shell.
- **Gerald:** A genuinely sweet chair. Possibly the healthiest relationship anybody had all evening.

## Places Visited

- **The Temporary Festivity Annex:** A suspiciously cheerful administrative space where no one had the option to refuse happiness.
- **The Ballroom of Obligatory Merriment:** Home to an enormous purple cake and the Department's birthday paperwork.
- **The Gift Assembly Workshop:** Where thoughtful presents were manufactured to the legally required standard of mild inconvenience.
- **The Chamber of Competitive Merriment:** A dance hall in which musical chairs became an interpersonal tragedy and then a small-scale war.
- **Alvin's magical hut:** Where the group appeared to have been sleeping the entire time, despite all evidence to the contrary.

## Clues, Rumors, and Deeply Unhelpful Questions

- Is the Department of Birthdays and Other Important Occasions a real institution, or merely a very elaborate shared nightmare?
- Does Happy now officially have a birthday? What about the one they might have had before losing their memories?
- If a government office can grant permission to have been born, who gives *the government office* permission to exist?
- Why did the slippers survive the return to camp?
- Does Gerald ever think about Alvin?
- Is there an administrative form for reporting assault by an earl to a chair?
- How, exactly, does one get glitter out of fox fur?

## Quests and Open Threads

**One-shot objective: Obtain Permission to Have Been Born.** Completed, at least according to the paperwork.

No new quests or consequences are being added to the main campaign. This episode stands alone. The Department's jurisdiction over canon remains **unconfirmed**.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources

- **Sparkling purple slippers:** Made for Happy by Alvin. They continued to exist after the apparent dream, which is extremely inconvenient for the dream explanation.
- **Hand-painted wooden swivel knife:** Lheani's lovingly awkward birthday gift. Its status after returning to camp is unclear.
- **Miniature foot-powered landship:** Paul's spectacularly impractical present and the gift-contest winner. Its status after returning to camp is unclear.
- **One official birthday, shared with Alvin:** The most important reward, assuming the paperwork holds up.
- **One homemade earl sash, several emotional wounds, and an unspeakable amount of glitter:** Acquired during the proceedings. Their long-term persistence is anybody's guess.
- **A large quantity of birthday cake:** Enjoyed, weaponized, and possibly stuffed into a shell. None was reliably recovered after waking.

## Where We Left Off

The four adventurers were back at camp, alive and restored, with memories of an impossible birthday party and at least one suspiciously tangible souvenir. The main journey toward Stormwatch Harbor remained exactly where it had been before.

Whether the whole affair actually happened is anyone's guess.

But if Happy's birthday comes around again, **Paul should probably stay away from the chairs.**
