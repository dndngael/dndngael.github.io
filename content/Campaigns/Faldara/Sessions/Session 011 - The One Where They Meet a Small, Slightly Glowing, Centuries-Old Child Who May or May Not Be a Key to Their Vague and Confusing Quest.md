# Session Recap: The One Where They Meet a Small, Slightly Glowing, Centuries-Old Child Who May or May Not Be a Key to Their Vague and Confusing Quest

## Previously

The party's first night in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]] ended with childlike crying in an abandoned inn and the disappearance of one of Happy's glowing glasses.

## What Happened

The group began by investigating the vanished glass and the strange crying. A visit to the infirmary produced little help with ghosts, so the party turned its attention back toward [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]].

At the temple entrance, a disembodied voice delivered the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Riddle of Tur'Farinil|Riddle of Tur'Farinil]], speaking of a tangled loom, a lost one who remembers the altar, a blind one who sees the way, and a believer who must be unmade.

Following the riddle's reference to "the root," the party visited [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/The Dilapidated Temple|the Dilapidated Temple]] and met [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]], a firbolg caretaker devoted to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Favaro|Favaro]]. Some of the party also became acquainted with Donneran's supply of Juwanna.

Elsewhere in the city, the party discovered that [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]] had somehow ended up with Happy's missing glowing glass.

Back at the abandoned inn, a rat described a strange "kid" who moved through the building without using doors. Soon after, the party finally met [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]], a small, maskless, faintly glowing spirit.

Velmian admitted that he had moved the glass to Lurvius because it felt like it belonged there. He also remembered Tur'Farinil as a temple of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] from an impossibly long time ago, before "weird people" arrived wearing masks.

Velmian led the party back to Tur'Farinil. When the riddle sounded again, he recognized the voice as Farinia's.

## Notable Moments

- The party received Farinia's riddle.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]] entered the campaign.
- The mystery of Happy's missing glass led directly to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Velmian|Velmian]].
- Velmian connected the ancient temple to Farinia and suggested that mask-wearing came later.
- Velmian's unexplained interest in Lurvius became a new mystery.
