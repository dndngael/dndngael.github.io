# Session Recap: Tides of Probability

## The Very Short Version

The heroes finally escaped the temple, only to discover a new and perhaps more terrifying obstacle: shopping with a budget. They bought sparkly boots, acquired a suspiciously literal bag of gold, and tried very hard not to kill five people who were busy burning down a cabin. By the end, someone had been killed and brought back to life, the group had inherited the potential beginnings of a detective agency, and the sea was apparently on fire. A perfectly normal start to a road trip.

## Previously

After closing the rift in [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], receiving Grand Duke Seren's reward, and learning from [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]] that the ancient Velmian was the Apotheon, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/You Guys|You Guys]] prepared to travel to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Apeiron|Apeiron]]. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] believes [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] might be the prophesied Zero, while Fineks hopes the island may offer a way to restore her suppressed soul song. First, the party needs to reach Stormwatch Harbor.

## What Happened

### One last shopping spree

In the market of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]], soldiers still kept watch even though no new attack had come from the temple. The wider city had yet to fully grasp what You Guys had accomplished. The party stocked up on travel provisions for a journey that 27 estimated at **roughly sixteen days on foot** to Stormwatch Harbor.

A conversation with Fineks offered another glimpse of Apeiron. 27 described a leader called **Eight** and suggested that a person's numerical designation might be more complicated than Fineks assumed. Fineks wondered whether her own number could be infinity rather than zero. No one had an answer yet.

Shopping proved more eventful than expected. The party acquired healing supplies, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] finally settled his debt to the traveling merchant **Temu**. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] purchased glittering purple **Boots of Slight Swiftness**, which grant extra movement speed after attunement. Fineks picked up a magical glove that strengthens *Fire Bolt*, although [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] noticed that its missing maker's mark made its craftsmanship questionable. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] admired a returning handaxe but could not justify the price. Temu offered to hold it for a possible future meeting in Lakeville.

### Farewell to Dawn's Bane

At the city gates, the remaining members of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] came to see Fineks off. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Ĵura Senkret|Ĵura]] said they would stay to help [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Seren]] with further investigations of the temple before traveling on to **Lakeville**, where they hoped to reunite with the group. [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]] was enthusiastic about joining the journey to Apeiron, but [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]] firmly insisted he remain with them. Fineks set out with You Guys and the small fiery companion **Torĉ** at her side.

### Four gold pieces and a very bad hole

The first stretch south led through woodland. Someone noticed a little **blue sack marked “gold”** hanging from a tree. Against reasonable advice, the party used *Mage Hand* to retrieve it and found exactly **four gold pieces** inside. They kept both the gold and its unusually well-labeled container.

Farther along the road, Lheani noticed an unnatural patch of ground just before Fineks stepped onto it. The covering gave way to reveal a **ten-foot pit lined with crude, bloodstained wooden spikes**. No one fell in. The adventurers heard someone fleeing through the forest but failed to track the watcher down.

### The cabin on fire

On the **third day of the journey**, smoke rose beyond a ridge. Five spellcasters stood around a small cabin, methodically setting it ablaze. Their explanation was simple: *demolitions*. It did not quite add up, especially when Fineks approached the building and they kept hurling fire in her direction.

The encounter erupted into combat. The spellcasters countered magic, used protective spells, and tried to keep the party at a distance. You Guys attempted to subdue rather than slaughter them, although the enemy seemed considerably less interested in returning the favor.

Fineks used a gust of wind to clear smoke and reduce the flames. Through a window, she spotted an unconscious man inside the cabin. She entered, aided him, and got him out into the open while the others fought. Lheani's axe and ancestral defenders held the line, Paul and Alvin pressed the attackers, and Happy and 27 supported their companions.

When one of the cornered spellcasters unleashed a devastating *Fireball*, the violence turned horrifying. The blast killed the rescued man and one of the spellcasters' own companions, and it brought both Lheani and 27 down. Fineks then ended the fight by killing the remaining immediate threat with her war fan. The other surviving attackers had already fled.

Even death was not quite the end of the story. Fineks used *Revivify* and further healing to **bring the man from the cabin back to life**. After tending to their companions, the party finally had a chance to ask what they had stumbled into.

### Reginald Maxwell III, apparently

The survivor introduced himself as **Reginald Maxwell III**, a minor noble whose vacation had been interrupted. Alvin noticed that the performance was not entirely convincing. Pressed for answers, the man dropped his elaborate accent and admitted that the name and persona were false. He introduced himself as [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Sev|**Sev**]].

Sev said he was a **private investigator from Stormwatch Harbor**. A towering woman in a gray coat, whom he believed to be part Goliath, had hired him to investigate a troubling conspiracy. His inquiries had attracted the attention of dangerous people, and the five spellcasters appeared to have been sent after him. He would not reveal the woman's identity because he did not know it.

He warned the party about something he called **the Faceless**. Whether this is an actual group, or whether his interpretation is right, remains unverified. While investigating, he had encountered strange writing repeated among outcasts and in hidden corners:

> They look without eyes, speak without mouths, and hear without ears.

Another recurring question was even more disturbing in a society built around masks: **“Why do we wear masks?”**

Sev's warnings did not stop there. He claimed that the water near Stormwatch Harbor was **constantly burning**, with waves sometimes gathering into something like a tsunami of flame. According to him, followers of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Solano|Solano]] and [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Eravona|Eravona]] blamed each other for the disaster. The Dry Docks had closed, and tensions threatened to boil over. These were Sev's reports, canonically heard by the party but not yet personally witnessed or verified.

### A contract, a safe, and an office

Searching a fallen attacker, the party recovered **five gold pieces, a dagger, and a contract** bearing an unusual symbol. It resembled a hooded, masked face, with part of the image looking like a dark void. Sev recognized the mark from his investigation and thought it might be connected with the Faceless, although he could not explain it.

In gratitude for his rescue, Sev offered the party access to what remained of his possessions in the burning cabin. He revealed the combination **1-4-2** for a safe beneath the bed. Alvin retrieved **120 gold pieces and a Potion of Invisibility**.

Sev also gave the group the location of his former office in Stormwatch Harbor's **Dry Docks District**. He said he had left investigation notes there, that his landlady was named **Gladys**, and that the rent was already paid for a little longer. He had no intention of returning to the harbor himself.

Before moving on, Fineks and Lheani buried the two dead attackers. It was a quieter ending than the battle had suggested. As the group prepared to continue, one of them briefly thought they saw something moving among the trees, but nothing could be made out.
