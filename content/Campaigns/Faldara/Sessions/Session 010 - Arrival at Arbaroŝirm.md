# Session Recap: Arrival at Arbaroŝirm

## Previously

The party had crossed the long road from Vualio, gained [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] as a traveling companion, and finally reached the black-spired silhouette of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Arbaroŝirm|Arbaroŝirm]].

## What Happened

At the city gates, the guards took one look at the party's equipment and assumed they were mercenaries arriving to help. [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] was arriving as well, with Ĵura driving their cart.

Before anyone could properly catch up, alarm horns sounded. The people of Arbaroŝirm responded with practiced speed, boarding buildings and preparing for another attack.

The party followed an elf commander and two guards into battle against horrifying fleshy creatures covered in mouths and eyes. One of the guards died during the fight. Afterward, the elf introduced himself as [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]].

Seren explained that the attacks come from a rift deep inside the ancient temple [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], and that the attacks have been getting worse.

The party helped carry the fallen guard to [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/The Crematorium|the Crematorium]], visited the barracks, and learned more about the temple and the rift. At the infirmary, 27 and Happy helped heal injured people.

Their stay at the barracks ended less gracefully. A drunken soldier mistook Paul for one of the creatures and started a fight. After the party knocked him unconscious, Seren arrived and was not impressed. The group was told to sleep somewhere else.

They found an abandoned inn instead. During the night, everyone except 27 heard a childlike crying sound. Happy left one of his glowing glasses on a table. By morning, it had vanished.

## Notable Moments

- The party arrived in Arbaroŝirm during an active monster attack.
- They met Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro.
- They learned that a rift inside Tur'Farinil is believed to be the source of the city's recurring attacks.
- The party's first night in the city ended in a haunted-looking inn with a missing glowing glass.
