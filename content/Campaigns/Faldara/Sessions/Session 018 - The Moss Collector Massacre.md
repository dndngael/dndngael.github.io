# Session Recap: The Moss Collector Massacre

## Previously

The party had reached a loom room filled with ancient tapestries after solving Tur'Farinil's musical and memory puzzles.

## What Happened

The tapestries turned out to be some of the most revealing things the party had yet found.

One depicted a purple-eyed man who looked almost exactly like [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]]. An inscription named him **Velmian**, calling him a hero who had been "apotheosized" and was "lost yet unforgotten." Three sigils surrounded him: the crescent eye of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Narvono|Narvono]], the hourglass of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Farinia|Farinia]], and an impossible triangle later associated with [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Deities/Kreiĉiza|Kreiĉiza]].

The discovery prompted [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] to share another piece of his strange past. During a previous near-death vision, he had seen an adult version of the same face, and that figure had called him **"brother."**

A second tapestry depicted three altars. Paul and 27 recognized one as the hidden altar in the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto|Lazuli Grotto]], suggesting that two related altars may still exist elsewhere.

Another tapestry showed a purple-eyed figure wearing a crimson-and-black mask with gold markings resembling the party's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Mask Piece|mask fragment]].

After finishing their archaeological investigation, the party opened a trapped door into a kobold nest. Alvin successfully disabled the trap, but stealth failed almost immediately. The resulting fight was short and extremely decisive.

## Notable Moments

- The [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Hero's Tapestry|Hero's Tapestry]] connected the name Velmian to a figure identical to Lurvius.
- Happy revealed that an adult version of that face had called him "brother" in a vision.
- The [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Three Altars Tapestry|Three Altars tapestry]] suggested that the Lazuli Grotto shrine is only one of three related sites.
- Three divine sigils linked Velmian to Narvono, Farinia, and an impossible triangle.
- Alvin successfully disarmed the door trap.
- The party cleared the kobold room in only a few rounds.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources
