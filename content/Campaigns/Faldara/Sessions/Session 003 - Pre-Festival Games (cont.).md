# Session Recap: Pre-Festival Games (cont.)

## Previously

The group had spent the previous session competing in festival events while the Lowgates guard searched for Paul's stolen mask.

## What Happened

Over breakfast, Paul revealed that he is the only surviving member of his family. The others had died after becoming sick, while Paul survived.

When the party returned to [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Captain Roseshade|Captain Roseshade]], they finally received good news: Paul's mask had been found. An elderly woman named Hazel had reported seeing it sticking out of a bag outside her building. By the time guards returned to investigate the bag itself, however, the bag had disappeared.

Paul's enthusiasm for questioning Hazel and the guards became intense enough that Alvin and 27 physically dragged him away.

With the immediate crisis resolved, the group returned to the festival and headed toward an arm-wrestling competition, where they learned that challengers had to defeat one of Maryl Bronzefang's companions before earning the right to face her.

## Notable Moments

- Paul's stolen mask was recovered.
- The party learned about the deaths of Paul's family.
- Alvin and 27 had to extract Paul from an increasingly enthusiastic investigation.

## Quests and Open Threads

### Resolved

- **Retrieve Paul's Mask:** Paul's mask was recovered by the Lowgates guard.

### Continued

- **Become Champions of the Lowgates:** The festival competitions continued.

## Where We Left Off

With Paul's mask back where it belonged, the group was free to concentrate on the festival and the remaining path toward becoming Champions of the Lowgates.
