# Session Recap: In Which a Wallet Dies a Hero's Death, and So Does His Owner, Spit on that thing

## Previously

The party had stabilized Fineks and assembled a plan to enter [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]] with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] and allies.

## What Happened

Preparation began with shopping. Nephil introduced the party to a human wizard named [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Yhefff-Two Ua|Yhefff-Two Ua]], immediately shortened to **Jeff**, who proceeded to spend an impressive amount of money on gear and potions.

At the temple, guards announced that [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro|Grand Duke Seren Kaŭro]] had officially forbidden entry. A long confrontation with [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]] resulted in most of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] being sent toward another entrance while the smaller group continued inside.

Jeff's membership in the expedition lasted only a few hours. In the first major chamber he opened a trapped door and was killed instantly by a falling chandelier.

His remains then transformed into a hostile goo-creature, forcing the party to fight their extremely recent companion. They collected what remained afterward in a bottle.

The survivors tackled a puzzle built around **past, present, and future**. Three bowls responded not to physical offerings but to spoken memories, present observations, and future intentions. Solving it lowered a magical barrier.

Behind that barrier they found [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]], a tortle barbarian who had been trapped inside the temple for roughly five days after the religious expedition he had been guarding was attacked.

Lheani gave the group a key and joined them as they continued deeper into Tur'Farinil.

## Notable Moments

- Tur'Farinil became officially off-limits by ducal order.
- Yhefff-Two Ua joined the party, financed an extraordinary shopping trip, and died to the first obvious temple trap he interacted with.
- The temple's first major puzzle reinforced Farinia's themes of past, present, and future.
- [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]] joined the party.

## Loot, Rewards, and Resources

- Various potions and supplies purchased before entering the temple.
- A bottle containing Yhefff-Two Ua's remaining goo.
- A key recovered by Lheani and used to continue through the temple.
