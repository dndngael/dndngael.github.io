# Session Recap: Unmake Faylen, Go

## The Very Short Version

The party entered this session facing the timeless philosophical question: what exactly does it mean to "unmake" someone? Their answer involved magical daggers, several near-death experiences, the power of friendship, Fineks, and telling Astra that he was simply too beautiful to die. By the end of it, [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Faylen Nemyris|Faylen]] was dead, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] had been talked back into being themselves, and the suspicious rock around Faylen's neck had immediately become everyone else's problem.

## Previously

The surviving record of Session 20 ended with the party meditating in a hidden courtyard deep within [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]], where a mysterious robed figure bearing an hourglass symbol had appeared to them.

The surviving Session 21 transcript begins later, with the confrontation against Faylen already underway. The exact transition between those scenes is not preserved.

## What Happened

The party was already locked in combat with Faylen and two Vesperaŝ when the surviving transcript begins.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Alvin Draper|Alvin]] filled part of the battlefield with a Cloud of Daggers while [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Paul Seragon|Paul]] pressed the attack. Faylen responded by clutching her necklace and calling for the **Believers of the Twin Light** to come to her aid.

A doorway appeared.

Through it came three extremely familiar faces: [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]], [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Ĵura Senkret|Ĵura]], and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]].

Something was clearly wrong.

Lurvius's eyes were silver, and [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] quickly realized that none of the three were behaving like themselves. Even while they attacked the party, Lurvius showed visible hesitation.

The group initially tried to keep their attacks against Dawn's Bane nonlethal. Then they discovered a much better weapon.

Emotional damage.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/27|27]] called out to Ĵura, reminding him of their history together and asking why he was doing this after everything they had been through. Ĵura's strange, distant expression broke. Suddenly himself again, he was horrified by what was happening and immediately wanted to help save the others.

Happy tried something even more personal with Lurvius.

He reminded Lurvius that [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] was waiting for him outside and appealed to the people and things that actually mattered to him.

That worked.

Lurvius's eyes changed from silver to blue. He thanked Happy and said that he had needed someone to remind him of the things that mattered.

Astra proved somewhat more difficult.

Several heartfelt attempts failed spectacularly until Alvin finally found the correct solution: tell Astra that he was far too beautiful to die like this, deserved better than 27, and needed to survive long enough to find it.

Astra immediately came back to himself.

Apparently friendship comes in many forms.

Meanwhile, Faylen's connection to the moons became increasingly visible. A silver crescent appeared beneath her as she called upon Farin, insisting that Farin still guided her. She turned her moonlight against Ĵura for doubting the Twin Light and later compelled him to attack [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lheani|Lheani]].

During that spell, Paul heard what sounded like someone speaking from the dark stone around Faylen's neck. He could tell there was a voice, but he could not understand its words.

Another version of that stone had appeared elsewhere in the chamber.

Alvin investigated a glimmer high on one of the pillars and discovered what looked exactly like Faylen's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Moon's Tear|Moon's Tear]]. When he tried to touch it, however, his hand passed straight through the image.

With Dawn's Bane restored, the party finally turned its full attention toward Faylen.

That led to another debate.

Was the line from [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/The Riddle of Tur'Farinil|Farinia's riddle]], **"One who believes must be unmade,"** actually telling them to kill Faylen? Did "unmaking" mean something else? Was Faylen herself being influenced by something?

Lheani argued for trying to spare her, referring back to divine guidance the party had received that characterized Faylen as misguided rather than simply evil.

The discussion continued while Faylen was still actively trying to kill them, which did somewhat limit the available time for philosophy.

Alvin finally incapacitated Faylen with Tasha's Hideous Laughter. With Faylen helpless on the ground, Paul managed to pull the Moon's Tear from her.

He immediately tried putting the real stone into the strange image of it on the pillar.

Nothing happened.

He tried wearing it himself.

Still nothing obvious happened.

The fight continued.

Lurvius turned his blades against Faylen. 27 struck her with radiant magic. The rest of the party piled on.

Eventually, Faylen could take no more.

She died.

The silver moonlight beneath her disappeared.

The Vesperaŝ did not immediately continue attacking.

Instead, both creatures stared directly at Paul.

He was still wearing Faylen's necklace.

Paul handed the [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Moon's Tear|Moon's Tear]] to Happy.

The Vesperaŝ turned toward Happy instead. One moved closer while the creatures stared and chattered at him.

And beyond all of them, the rift was still open.

## Notable Moments

- Faylen called for the "Believers of the Twin Light," bringing Lurvius, Ĵura, and Astra into the battle while they were apparently under an outside influence.
- 27 broke Ĵura free by reminding him of their shared history.
- Happy broke Lurvius free by reminding him of Fineks and the things that mattered to him. Lurvius's eyes changed from silver to blue.
- Alvin discovered that the correct password for Astra's soul was apparently **"you are too beautiful to die and deserve better than 27."**
- Alvin found an intangible image of Faylen's Moon's Tear on a pillar.
- Paul heard an indistinct voice coming from the real Moon's Tear.
- The party seriously debated what **"unmake"** meant before eventually deciding that Faylen had to be stopped permanently.
- Faylen died, but her Moon's Tear and the Vesperaŝ immediately became a new mystery.
- The party earned a level up.
