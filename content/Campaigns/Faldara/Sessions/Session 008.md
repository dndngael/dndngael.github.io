# Session Recap: Session 8

> [!note]
> The surviving legacy notes for this session are brief. This recap records only the events that are clearly preserved there.

## What Happened

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Astra|Astra]] came to the party's rescue during the journey, then made sure they knew exactly what he thought about their lack of grace. The group obtained horses and continued traveling, leaving the rest of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] behind for the moment.

The road continued to produce unsettling signs. The party witnessed a fire dance and recorded an event in which the sun appeared to be on fire.

They also learned more about [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Lurvius|Lurvius]], including that his eye color changes with his emotions, and more about the sickness that had affected Lakeville and Paul's family.

Astra's attitude and the sense that Dawn's Bane knew more than they were saying left the party suspicious of their rivals-turned-allies.

## Notable Moments

- Astra rescued the party and immediately insulted their grace.
- The journey continued under increasingly strange celestial signs.
- The party learned that Lurvius's changing eye color tracks his emotions.
- Suspicion toward Dawn's Bane increased.

## Resources

- A healing potion was purchased for 50 gp in connection with Alvin's rescue.

## Where We Left Off

The party remained on the road toward Arbaroŝirm, increasingly aware that both the landscape and their companions might be stranger than they first appeared.
