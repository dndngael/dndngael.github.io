# Session Recap: A "Plan" of Questionable Merit

## Previously

The party had survived the animated corpse amalgam at the Crematorium and was left with the black crystalline "heart" removed from Jeff.

## What Happened

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Donneran|Donneran]] confirmed that the stone was profoundly unnatural, but [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius|Nephil]] was able to identify it more precisely: **[[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Umbracite|Umbracite]]**, an ancient material he believed no longer existed.

Nephil explained that Umbracite can feed on what he calls a person's [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul song]]. He believed a fragment had pierced [[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Fineks|Fineks]] during an earlier battle and was acting like a parasite.

Nephil possessed a possible countermeasure: [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Silentstone|Silentstone]], a clear crystal that absorbs psychic energy and has almost no resonance of its own. He was reluctant to hand over such a valuable research object without knowing the party had a real plan.

The resulting plan was, generously, a plan.

The party agreed to help Nephil find [[Campaigns/Faldara/Items/Farinia's Codex|Farinia's Codex]], a crystal repository of information believed to be hidden inside the Great Library of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil|Tur'Farinil]]. In exchange, Nephil allowed them to use the Silentstone.

[[Campaigns/Faldara/People/Happy|Happy]] embedded it into Fineks's mask. Her whispers and shadowy hallucinations stopped almost immediately. The result was not entirely comforting, though: Nephil could no longer hear her normal soul song and described her resonance as a void, or **zero**.

With Fineks stabilized, [[Campaigns/Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane|Dawn's Bane]] agreed to join the planned temple expedition.

## Notable Moments

- The black crystal was identified as Umbracite.
- Nephil explained the concept of [[Campaigns/Faldara/Lore/Soul Songs|soul songs]].
- Silentstone stabilized Fineks but also muted her resonance.
- The party took on the search for Farinia's Codex.
- The word **zero** acquired another strange resonance around the campaign.
