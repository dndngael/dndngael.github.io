**Wesmas** is a village reached by a path from an old graveyard in a vast subterranean landscape. The party found its homes and public buildings intact, but no inhabitants came to greet them.

## Geography and Atmosphere

Glowing plants, oversized mushrooms, and unfamiliar vegetation surround the area. The glow softens toward night, giving the impression of dusk without any visible sun or moon.

The nearby graveyard is arranged around an enormous **dead willow tree**. Unmarked graves encircle it, and green pods stood nearby when the party first awakened. During Fen's burial, the otherwise lifeless willow shed a faint blue, luminous tear.

## Important Areas

- **Graveyard:** Where [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Hugh Mann|Hugh Mann]] emerged, Fen's body was discovered, and the party fought [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Spirit of Wesmas|a spirit]].
- **Inn:** Dusty but serviceable shelter where the party found dried mushrooms and rested.
- **Blacksmith's workshop:** Contained a half-finished longsword and a heavy crossbow. The crossbow was not confirmed as taken.
- **Market and homes:** Apparently deserted, with ordinary possessions and spoiled food left behind.
- **Larger civic-looking building:** Contained trade records and writing about a protective spirit.

## What the Party Knows

Records found in Wesmas described a nonviolent protector spirit also called **Wesmas**. Its description resembled the spirit the party killed in the graveyard. The records did not explain the villagers' disappearance.

The buildings had gathered dust, and the party found no obvious signs of a battle or physical struggle. It seems the residents left suddenly, but the manner and cause remain unknown.

## Session History

### [[Campaigns/Moraeth/Sessions/Session 001 - Beneath the Weeping Willow|Session 001]]

The party awoke near the graveyard, fought the spirit, buried Fen, explored the empty settlement, and rested in the inn.

## Open Questions
