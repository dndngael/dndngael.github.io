# Fen and Hugh Mann

[[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Fen|Fen]] died, and [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Hugh Mann|Hugh Mann]] rose from a nearby grave feeling compelled to protect Fen's companions, [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Myrna|Myrna]] and [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Zard|Zard]].

## What the Party Knows

Hugh recognized the other two without understanding how. Fen's damaged autognome body lay nearby. Hugh believes that Fen's dying wish and the strange magic of this place helped bring him into existence, but he is not Fen.

The party buried Fen in Hugh's former grave. The dead willow shed a faint blue tear soon afterward.

## Open Questions

What became of Fen? Who occupied Hugh's grave before him? Why does Hugh carry Fen's protective impulse, and what does the willow's tear mean?
