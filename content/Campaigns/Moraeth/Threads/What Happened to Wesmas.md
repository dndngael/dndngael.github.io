# What Happened to Wesmas?

[[Campaigns/Moraeth/Places/Wesmas|Wesmas]] is silent. The party found abandoned homes, old food, dust, and no obvious signs of violence. A village record described a peaceful protector spirit also named Wesmas, possibly the being the party killed at the nearby graveyard.

## What the Party Knows

- The village appears to have been left in a hurry, but the circumstances are unknown.
- The party did not find clear signs of a fight.
- A written account described a protective spirit resembling the one encountered in the graveyard.
- The party spent the night at the empty inn.

## Possible Leads

More buildings, belongings, and records remain to be examined. The party could also return to the graveyard, investigate the green pods, or leave the village.

## Open Questions

Where are the residents? Why was the spirit hostile? What role, if any, did the graveyard play in the village's abandonment?
