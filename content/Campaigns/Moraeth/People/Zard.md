**Zard** is a lizardfolk warlock who remembers dying and then speaking with an unfamiliar being before awakening in a green pod near [[Campaigns/Moraeth/Places/Wesmas|Wesmas]]. The identity of that being remains uncertain.

## Session History

### [[Campaigns/Moraeth/Sessions/Session 001 - Beneath the Weeping Willow|Session 001]]

Zard broke free of his pod after a spirit attacked an armored stranger, joined the party in exploring the graveyard, and searched Wesmas for evidence of what had happened to the residents. He was cautious about unfamiliar food but eventually sampled the dried mushrooms at the inn.

## Open Questions

Who was the being Zard remembers meeting, and how did the party arrive here?
