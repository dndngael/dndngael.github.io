**Myrna** is a reborn cleric whose faith is associated with joy. She remembers dying before waking in a green, membrane-like pod beside [[Campaigns/Moraeth/Places/Wesmas|Wesmas]].

## Session History

### [[Campaigns/Moraeth/Sessions/Session 001 - Beneath the Weeping Willow|Session 001]]

Myrna broke free of her pod during a fight with a graveyard spirit and helped the armored stranger by healing him. She later joined the burial and remembrance of [[Campaigns/Moraeth/People/Fen|Fen]], and the search through the empty village.

## Open Questions

How did she come to awaken in this unfamiliar world, and what will her faith mean here?
