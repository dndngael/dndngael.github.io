## Overview

An [[Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones#Umbrals\|Umbral]] in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]] district of [[Faldara/Places/Vualio City]].

## Notes

- You met Umbral Kaz during the pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] games, where he ran the Riddles and Rhymes game, although he didn't make it known he was a member of [[Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones#Umbrals\|The Masked Ones]].
- Helped run the final competition in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]].
