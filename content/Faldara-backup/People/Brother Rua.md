## Overview

A member of [[Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones#Fellowship (siblinghood)\|The Fellowship]] in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]] district of [[Faldara/Places/Vualio City]].

## Notes

- You met Brother Rua during the pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] games, where he helped run the final competition in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]] with [[Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz]].
