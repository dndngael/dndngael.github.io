# **Jeff**

> [!profile]+ **Profile**
> 
> #### Physical Description
> **Appearance**: It's a large, moving pile of gloopy flesh, random corpses, and an unsettling number of mouths and eyes. Really brings the room together.
> **Notable Features and Mannerisms**: Likes to form pseudopods to give crushing hugs, whispers sweet nothings that cause psychic damage, and has a crystal heart that pulses with darkness instead of, you know, life. It also has a flair for the dramatic, waiting until the party thinks they're safe to animate.
> 
> #### Personality
> 
> - **Personality Overview**: As charming as a heap of rotting bodies can be. Its primary mode of communication seems to be psychic assault and physical violence, suggesting it's not much for small talk. It's also surprisingly possessive, refusing to let guests leave its loving, acidic embrace.
>     
> - **Primary Traits**: -
>     
>     - **Aggressive**: Didn't waste any time attacking the party once it woke up, immediately grappling and engulfing anyone within reach. So, a real go-getter.
>     - **Terrifying**: The constant whispering and the whole "amorphous blob of death" vibe really seemed to unnerve the heroes. Caused psychic damage and disadvantage on attacks, so it's got a knack for psychological warfare.
>     - **Resilient**: Took a surprising amount of damage, including fire, lightning, and countless stabs and punches, and just kept on trucking. It also apparently took back its heart after the party so rudely carved it out.
>     - **Motivations**: To absorb all living things into its collective mass, probably out of loneliness. Or maybe it just wants to share its unique perspective on interior design.

> [!abstract]+ **Current Status** -
> 
> **Location**: Last seen being repeatedly stabbed, blasted, and having its heart ripped out in the crematorium.
> 
> - **State**: Ostensibly defeated after Happy pulled its heart out. The rest of the pile was just a pile again.
>     
> - **Attitude Towards Players**: Extremely hostile. It tried to kill them all, so it's probably not looking to make friends. It seemed to particularly enjoy engulfing people.
>     
>     - Seemed to have a thing for Happy and Paul, engulfing both of them. Maybe it appreciated their fashion sense.
>         
> - **Latest Interaction**: The party engaged in a long, drawn-out, and frankly embarrassing fight with it. After being repeatedly stabbed and blasted, Happy finally ended the ordeal by ripping out its crystal heart, causing the amalgam to collapse.

> [!locations]+ **Events & Interactions**
> 
> #### The Crematorium Catastrophe
> 
> - **Summary of Interaction**: The party, searching for the cause of Fineks's illness, decided the best course of action was to perform an "autism," as they put it, on a humanoid demon's corpse in the crematorium's "Rift Pile." After much grossness and a surprising amount of damage from stray teeth, they found a strange crystal heart inside the creature. Just as they were about to leave, the entire pile of corpses animated into a giant flesh monster named Jeff and attacked them, leading to a grueling battle where everyone almost died at least once.
> - **Location**: [[Faldara/Places/The Crematorium]]
> - **Characters Involved**: Paul Seragon, Alvin Draper, 27, Happy, Astra, Jeff, and some poor, underpaid crematorium worker who wisely ignored the whole thing.
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: The party barely survived, with most of them getting knocked unconscious at least once. They defeated Jeff by ripping its heart out (again) and now possess the strange crystal. Happy and Paul both have traumatic memories of being engulfed by a flesh monster, which is a fun souvenir.
> - **Relationship Changes:** The party's already questionable competence has been thoroughly confirmed. Their relationship with Astra is now strained, as he was deemed a "selfish asshole" for not wanting to get covered in gore.

> [!combat]+ **Abilities & Items**
> 
> #### Abilities & Skills
> 
> - **Skills/Abilities**:
>     
>     - **Engulf**: Can absorb a creature into its body. The victim is blinded, restrained, and unable to breathe, taking acid damage at the start of Jeff's turn. It's a real party trick.
>     - **Pseudopod**: Can form tentacles or appendages out of its body to bludgeon and grapple victims from a distance.
>     - **Whispers of Madness**: Can whisper horrifying things that cause psychic damage and disadvantage on attack rolls to anyone who fails a Wisdom saving throw.
>     - **Amorphous**: Can move over obstacles, because being a giant blob has its perks.
> 
> #### Inventory & Assets
> 
> - **Possessions**:
>     
>     - **Crystal Heart**: A dark, jagged, non-reflective crystal that served as its heart. Currently in 27's possession.
>         
>     - **Innumerable Corpses**: Its entire being is made of other people's stuff. So, technically, it owns a lot of secondhand gear.
>         
> - **Wealth**: If you count the value of recycled organs, it's loaded. Otherwise, broke.
>     
> - **Properties**: 
> 	- **The Rift Pile**: Claimed a cozy corner of the crematorium as its lair.
>     
> - **Resources/Connections**: 
> 	- **The Crematorium Worker**: An unwilling and unhelpful neighbor who just wants to listen to his music in peace.

> [!info]+ **Meta**
> 
> #### Roleplaying Notes
> 
> -
> 
> **Voice & Speech Patterns**: Doesn't speak, but communicates through horrifying, sanity-blasting whispers that manifest directly in its victims' minds.
> 
> - **Body Language**: Moves as a single, oozing mass. It forms pseudopods to attack and engulfs its prey with a grotesque hug.
>     
> - **Common Phrases/Catchphrases**: -
>     
>     **[Horrifying whispers that disrupt the mind]**: Its go-to conversation starter.
>     
> - **Emotional Tendencies**: Single-minded aggression. Its only discernible emotion is a relentless desire to absorb and kill.
>     
> - **Reference Character:** The Blob. Just a classic, all-consuming monster.
>     
> - **Roleplaying Tips**:
>     
>     - **Consistency**: It's a mindless, consuming horror. It should be relentless and terrifying, with its only goal being to grapple and engulf the nearest target.
>         
>     - **Accent/Dialect**: The sound of a thousand whispering, maddening voices, all at once.
>         
>     - **Behavioral Cues**: Focus on its amorphous and overwhelming nature. Describe the sickening squelch of its movement and the disembodied mouths and eyes churning within its form.
>         
> 
> #### DM Notes
> 
> - **Unrevealed Secrets**: What is the origin of the crystal heart? Is it connected to the "star in the dark" or the "eye that opened" that Fineks mentioned? Is Jeff just a symptom of a larger threat?
>     
> - **Role in Campaign**: Served as a major, and very nearly final, boss for the "Find a Cure for Fineks" questline. It provided the party with the crystal heart, a major clue to the nature of her illness.
>     
> - **Reception**: The players seemed to find Jeff appropriately terrifying and the combat encounter both hilarious and grueling. The sheer absurdity of fighting a monster named "Jeff" and the drawn-out, near-TPK nature of the fight was a memorable, if frustrating, experience for them.
>     
> - **Misc.**: The fact that the party named it "Jeff" somehow makes it even more unsettling. The juxtaposition of the mundane name with the eldritch horror was a highlight.
