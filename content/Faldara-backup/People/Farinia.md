## Overview

Farinia governs the flow of time and the weaving of fate. They ensure the balance of destiny, often seen as impartial and unfeeling, though their followers understand that fate is never cruel, only inevitable.

## Notes
- The moon [[Faldara/Places/Farin]] is named after Farinia

:::h
- Followers: Called "Fates", the followers of Farinia tend to be oracles, diviners, historians, and scholars.
### Divine Domains
Knowledge, Grave, Time, Patience, Fate

### Divine Symbols and Sigils
- An hourglass
- Animals: Spiders, ravens
- Colors: Silver, black, deep red

### Holidays
The Tapestry of Time: a day where followers meditate on their past actions and future decisions, symbolizing the threads of fate. Families create tapestries together of their memories.

The Hourglass Ceremony: an offering made at the birth or death of a loved one, marking the passage of time.

:::
