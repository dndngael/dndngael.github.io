#  **Mask Piece**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> The mask piece is black and red with glittering golden markings on it. It is attached to a golden chain that magically resizes the whole piece to function as a necklace for the creature that wears it.

**In-Game Effects:**

- You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wearing the mask.  
- While wearing or holding the mask, you can use an action to cause it to shed bright light in a 15-foot radius and dim light for an additional 15 feet. The light lasts until you extinguish it (no action required).  
- The mask has 3 charges and regains all its expended charges daily at dawn. While holding the mask, you or your ally can expend 1 charge from it to cast the [[3 Mechanics/spells/invisibility]] spell.
- Attunement can only be maintained for 2 consecutive days, after which if you attempt to attune to this item within the next 2 days, it will fail.

## Notes

This item was acquired after discovering the hidden prayer site in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]] and receiving a vision from the [[Faldara/People/Apotheon]].
