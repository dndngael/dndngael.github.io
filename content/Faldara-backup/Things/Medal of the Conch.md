#  **Medal of the Conch**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> Made of sea glass that has been magically crafted into the shape of a conch shell.

**In-Game Effects:**
When you use an action to rub this medal, you gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed for 1 hour. Once this property has been used, it can't be used again, and the medal becomes nonmagical.

## Notes

Earned by winning the "[[Faldara/Places/Kruma River\|Kruma]] Plunge" pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] game in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]].
