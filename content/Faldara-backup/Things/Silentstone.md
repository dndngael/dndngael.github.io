#  **Silentstone**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> A crystal-like stone that is difficult to see clearly. It is smooth to the touch and relatively small. The stone is clear, and its sides are only visible in the right light.

**In-Game Effects:**
- **Psychic Absorption**: The Silent Stone is known to absorb psychic energy.
- **Null Resonance**: Unlike the chaotic sounds emitted by the Umbracite, the Silent Stone has no discernible frequency or sound. Nephil describes it as "very quiet".
- **Soul Nullification?**: When used on Fineks, who was afflicted by Umbracite, the stone seemed to nullify her "soul song," making her emotionally distant and unlike her former self.

## Notes:

- The party learned about the Silent Stone from Nephil, who retrieved it from a locked chest in his tent.
- Nephil suggested that the stone could be crafted into an item for Fineks to wear to negate the effects of the Umbracite.
- Happy, the party's artificer, successfully embedded the Silentstone into Fineks's mask.
- Nephil mentioned they are "not often used on living beings."

:::h

## DM Notes:

- The Silent Stone is a priceless object given to Nephel for his research by the Institute.
    
- Nephel hypothesized that the stone could either negate the Umbracite's effects or allow it to harmonize with the soul of the afflicted.
    
- The reaction Fineks had to the stone was not what Nephel expected, as it seemed to nullify her soul's resonance rather than just negating the chaos. He noted that using them on living beings is not common.
    
- If the mask with the Silent Stone is removed from Fineks, the parasitic effects of the Umbracite would likely return through her wound.
    
- The current effect is not a permanent solution, and a true cure may still be needed.
