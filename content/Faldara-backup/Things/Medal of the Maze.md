#  **Medal of the Maze**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> Made of smooth driftwood, hung on a piece of rough twine. A labyrinthine pattern has been carved into the front of the medal.

**In-Game Effects:**

When you use an action to trace the maze inscribed on this medal, you gain advantage on Wisdom checks and know the quickest route to the end of any nonmagical path or maze for 1 hour. Once this property has been used, it can't be used again, and the medal becomes nonmagical.

## Notes

Earned by winning the "One-Shot Solution" pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] game in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]].
