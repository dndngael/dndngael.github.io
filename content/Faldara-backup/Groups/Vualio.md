## Overview

**Vualio** is a kingdom of veils and shadows, where tradition and secrecy rule every aspect of life. Named after the god [[Faldara/People/Vual]], the deity of masks, Vualio’s culture revolves around the wearing of masks that symbolize a person's place in society. From the highest noble to the humblest worker, everyone in Vualio wears a mask, a tradition so deeply ingrained that most have never seen another’s true face. These masks are said to hold power, both literal and symbolic, guiding the wearer’s role in the kingdom. Each person participates in the [[Maskovo]] - a coming of age ceremony that marks a citizen’s transition from childhood into adulthood, where they receive their mask that represents their place in society, tied to their role, profession, or calling.

The kingdom itself is a patchwork of majestic cities, each filled with ornate towers and bridges, and sprawling countryside where ancient ruins and enchanted forests dot the landscape. The [[Faldara/Places/Vualio City\|capital city]] is a place of grandeur and mystery, where the masked elite gather to make decisions for the kingdom, hiding their true intentions behind carefully crafted personas. [[The High Council]], shrouded in both mystery and power, guides the fate of Vualio, though not without controversy.

Magic flows freely through Vualio, much of it tied to the masks that its citizens wear. Some masks are enchanted, granting their wearers special abilities, while others hold deeper, more dangerous powers, although that's just a rumor - a story told by children. Everyone knows that the masks make you better at your job, better at doing your duty to Vualio. Beneath the surface of Vualio’s rigid societal order lies a land filled with intrigue, hidden agendas, and ancient magic waiting to be rediscovered. That being said, the use of magic by the citizens of Vualio is heavily frowned upon. Only a select few may study magic, and knowledge is heavily guarded.

For all its beauty and culture, Vualio is a kingdom marked by an ever-present tension. Some speak of the gods’ influence on the land, while others whisper of rebellions forming in the shadows. It is a place where identity is both a source of power and a source of conflict—where anyone can become anything, but few truly know who they are. Secrecy is a strength, but maybe some secrets need to be revealed to change Vualio - and all of Faldara. Many wish to cause change, some seeking control for themselves, some seeking a way out, and some just wish for a change in their own circumstances seeking power from within Vualio.

## Culture
### Masks
In the Kingdom of Vualio, masks play an essential role in daily life. Everyone wears a mask, symbolizing their role in society, with some believing that the masks are more than just symbols—they hold the power to change those who wear them.

## The King and the Royal Family

At the top of Vualio’s government is the King, [[Reĝo Verkom]], who is believed to be chosen by [[Faldara/People/Vual]] himself. The royal family traces its lineage back to ancient times, when they were said to have been granted divine favor by Vual for their dedication to the kingdom’s spiritual values. The king holds the highest position of power and authority, both politically and spiritually, though his role is not one of absolute dictatorship. His responsibilities include: 

- Governance and Law: The king oversees the creation and enforcement of laws, though he does this in consultation with the High Council and the religious leaders of the kingdom.
- Divine Representative: As the spiritual leader of Vualio, the king often presides over major religious ceremonies, such as the [[Maskovo]] and the grand events of [[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj]]. His presence in these ceremonies is seen as a direct link to Vual.
- Military Leadership: In times of war or unrest, the king commands Vualio’s armies, though this power is often delegated to generals from the nobility.
- Diplomatic Relations: The king negotiates treaties, alliances, and trade agreements with neighboring lands.

The royal family plays a significant role in maintaining the legitimacy of the monarchy. Princes and princesses often hold positions within the government or military, and many are trained in the religious orders of The Masked Ones, ensuring that they understand both the divine and secular aspects of ruling. The eldest child of the royal family is the heir apparent, but all royal children are raised with the expectation that they may be called upon to serve the kingdom in a variety of capacities.

## The High Council

Beneath the king is [[The High Council]], a group of influential individuals who advise the king and govern the day-to-day operations of the kingdom. The High Council is composed of representatives from the nobility, the clergy, and military leadership, ensuring that all aspects of Vualio’s society are balanced within the government. Its members include: 

- [[Faldara/Groups/Nobility\|Nobles]]: These council members represent the interests of the nobility, advocating for the rights and privileges of the aristocracy. They also act as local governors of their territories, collecting taxes and ensuring the enforcement of laws within their domains.
- [[Faldara/Groups/Decidantoj\|The Decidantoj]]: As key figures in religious life, the Decidantoj (the ones who assign masks during the Maskovo) have significant sway in the High Council. Their role is not only spiritual but also judicial, as they hold a powerful voice in matters of law, morality, and social order.
- Military Leaders: Generals and commanders from the army sit on the council, providing insight into defense and security matters. They are responsible for maintaining Vualio’s standing forces and managing the kingdom’s borders.
- Chief Artisans and Scholars: Because Vualio places great importance on craftsmanship and knowledge, certain positions on the council are held by master artisans and scholars who guide the kingdom’s cultural and intellectual growth. These individuals help shape the kingdom’s policies regarding trade, technology, and art.

The High Council has several key responsibilities:

- Legislative Power: They draft laws, which the king must ratify. These laws range from trade regulations to moral guidelines set by The Masked Ones.
- Judicial Oversight: They adjudicate high-level disputes, often with the help of the Decidantoj to ensure that decisions align with divine will.
- Resource Management: They oversee the kingdom’s wealth, allocating resources for the maintenance of infrastructure, the military, and public works.

The council’s power is substantial, but their decisions must align with the kingdom’s religious doctrines, which are overseen by [[Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones]].

## The Nobility

[[Faldara/Groups/Nobility\|The Nobility]] in Vualio is a wealthy and influential class, each member receiving their position through family heritage and through the mask they were assigned at their Maskovo ceremony. They hold vast estates across the kingdom, governing their lands in the king’s name. Nobles are responsible for: 

- Local Governance: As regional rulers, nobles enforce laws, manage taxes, and oversee local economies.
- Military Contributions: Nobles are required to provide soldiers and military support in times of conflict. Many nobles also maintain private armies, which they offer to the king in exchange for political favors or land.
- Festivals and Ceremonies: Nobles host smaller versions of La Festo de Maskoj within their territories, ensuring that the kingdom’s traditions are upheld even in remote areas.

The nobility is loyal to the king but fiercely protective of their own power and status. This has led to occasional political rivalries and tensions within the kingdom. Many nobles are also deeply religious, seeing their positions as a divine blessing and often seeking the favor of the Decidantoj to maintain their influence.

## The Religion of Vual: The Masked Ones

[[Faldara/Groups/The Masked Ones]], the kingdom’s official religious order, are deeply embedded in Vualio’s government. The Decidantoj, who lead The Masked Ones, are not only spiritual leaders but also important political figures who influence government decisions. The order follows the teachings of Vual, the god of masks, emphasizing the importance of order, fate, and personal transformation. The role of the religion in the government includes:

- Moral and Judicial Authority: The Decidantoj serve as judges in legal matters, determining the guilt or innocence of those accused of crimes, particularly those related to the divine order. They interpret Vual’s will and ensure that laws align with spiritual doctrines.
- Social Structure Enforcement: The Masked Ones enforce the caste system through the Maskovo ceremony, where citizens are assigned their adult masks. These masks signify their social roles, from artisans to nobles, and the caste system is seen as a reflection of divine will.
- Ceremonial Leadership: The Masked Ones preside over all major religious and state ceremonies, from royal weddings to the Maskovo ceremonies. They ensure that these rituals are performed in accordance with sacred tradition.

## The Caste System

Vualio’s society is divided into rigid castes, each represented by different types of masks. The caste a person belongs to is determined during the Maskovo ceremony, where they are assigned their adult mask by the Decidantoj. The caste system reflects divine order, and each caste has its own responsibilities and privileges. The major castes include: 

- Clergy: While not a formal caste, the clergy of The Masked Ones are a separate, revered group, holding spiritual authority over all other castes.

1. Nobility: The highest caste, assigned masks of prestige and power. They rule over territories, command armies, and influence the kingdom’s political landscape.
2. Artisans and Scholars: Representing the kingdom’s intellectual and cultural elite, these individuals are given masks symbolizing creativity, knowledge, and craftsmanship.
3. Merchants and Traders: This caste is responsible for trade, commerce, and the kingdom’s economy. Their masks symbolize wealth and negotiation.
4. Farmers and Laborers: The backbone of Vualio, these citizens are assigned masks representing hard work and endurance.

 The caste system is seen as a reflection of the divine order established by Vual and the other gods. It is generally accepted by the populace, though there are occasional grumblings from those who feel unfairly placed in lower castes. However, attempts to break free from one’s assigned role are seen as a violation of Vual’s will and are punished severely.

## Public Sentiment Towards the Govermnent

The general populace of Vualio holds a mixture of reverence and unease toward the government. On one hand, the government is seen as divinely sanctioned, with the king and Decidantoj acting as representatives of the gods. This spiritual foundation gives the government an aura of legitimacy and unassailability. Most citizens accept their roles in society as part of the divine plan, believing that their place in life is chosen by Vual and the gods.

However, not all is serene. Some citizens, particularly those in the lower castes, occasionally express dissatisfaction with the rigidity of the system. They may feel trapped by their assigned roles, unable to rise above their station despite their talents or desires. Rumors of discontent and subtle acts of rebellion exist, though they are often kept quiet for fear of punishment.

The nobility and higher castes, on the other hand, view the government more favorably, as they benefit from the privileges of the system. Many of them see themselves as the rightful rulers of Vualio, chosen not only by blood but by divine favor.

## Structure - `=this.hierarchy`

The Kingdom of Vualio is a theocratic monarchy deeply intertwined with religious and societal traditions. The government is a fusion of divine authority, aristocratic power, and spiritual guidance, which together maintain strict control over every aspect of life in the kingdom. The structure is hierarchical, with the king and the royal family at the pinnacle, followed by the High Council, nobility, and clergy. Below them lies the common citizenry, divided into castes based on their assigned roles, as decided by the Decidantoj during the Maskovo ceremony.
