## Overview

The Masked Ones are the official faith dedicated to [[Faldara/People/Vual]], the god of masks. Their influence permeates the spiritual and political spheres, interwoven deeply with the [[Faldara/Groups/Vualio\|Kingdom of Vualio's]] governance. The clergy guide the kingdom’s citizens through their spiritual journeys and help maintain the order of masks, which are central to Vualio’s society and identity.

## Structure
The religious hierarchy of The Masked Ones is tied to the broader caste system of Vualio, with ultimate spiritual and political authority vested in the Decidantoj, a powerful and enigmatic group tasked with assigning societal roles and determining spiritual paths. Below them is a well-defined order of clergy, each with their own responsibilities:

### Decidantoj
The highest rank within both the religious and governmental structures of Vualio, the [[Faldara/Groups/Decidantoj]] are tasked with interpreting the will of [[Faldara/People/Vual]], assigning societal roles during the [[Maskovo]], and maintaining order within the kingdom. Their decisions are seen as divinely guided, and their word is final in matters of faith. The Decidantoj are chosen through a secretive process within The Masked Ones and have direct influence over the kingdom's political decisions. Each Decidanto wears a unique mask designed to represent their role and status, often intricately designed and blessed by Vual to enhance their judgment and insight.

### The Circle of Elders
The Circle consists of twelve high-ranking clergy members who oversee regions of Vualio and provide guidance for the various societal castes. They act as spiritual and political advisors to the Decidantoj and play a significant role in interpreting Vual’s teachings. The Circle is responsible for coordinating large-scale ceremonies and ensuring that Vual's doctrines are followed across the kingdom. They wear masks that completely conceal their features, symbolizing their commitment to the balance between Vual's light and shadow.

### Umbrals
The Umbrals are the middle-ranking clergy responsible for local temples and daily religious rituals. They interact directly with the populace, offering spiritual guidance, performing smaller ceremonies, and maintaining order within the community. Umbrals also serve as mentors to members of the Fellowship, helping them learn the deeper teachings of the faith.

### Fellowship (siblinghood)
Those of the Fellowship are in training to join the higher ranks of the clergy. Chosen for their piety, devotion, or magical ability, they are taught the intricacies of Vual’s teachings and the sacred nature of the masks. They often serve as assistants during religious festivals and ceremonies, learning the complex rituals before they rise to become an Umbral. Their training includes both spiritual lessons and practical experience in magic, particularly illusions and divination.

### Devotees
Devotees are those who serve the temples and the faith but have not yet taken vows. They often come from families deeply devoted to Vual and perform the more mundane duties of temple life, such as cleaning, preparing for ceremonies, and assisting with festivals. Some may later take up the mantle of the clergy, but many remain devoted servants of the faith without becoming full members of The Masked Ones.

## Becoming a Member of The Masked Ones
### Identified from Childhood
Many future clergy members are identified as children, chosen for their deep devotion, affinity for magic, or potential to interpret Vual’s will. These children are raised within the temples, where they receive a rigorous education in religious teachings, philosophy, and the magical arts associated with Vual.

### The Masking of Devotion
Before joining the Fellowship, a candidate must undergo the Masking of Devotion, a formal rite that symbolizes their entry into the service of Vual. This is a private ceremony where they receive their first mask, a plain and undecorated piece that represents their willingness to submit to Vual’s teachings and embrace their role within the order.

### Spiritual Testing and Ascension
After years of dedicated service and study, members of the Fellowship undergo a series of spiritual tests. These tests evaluate their magical aptitude, understanding of Vual’s teachings, and ability to lead others. Passing these tests allows them to ascend to the rank of Umbral. For those who show exceptional promise, there is the possibility of one day joining the Circle or even becoming part of the Decidantoj.

When a vacancy opens within the Circle, the remaining members and the Decidantoj select a new member through a secretive process. This process is shrouded in mystery, with only the highest members of the order knowing the true requirements to ascend to this elite rank.

## Rituals and Ceremonies

- [[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj]]: The most important religious festival is La Festo de Maskoj, a celebration of the power of Vual. During this time, grand processions and rituals fill the capital, with the [[Faldara/Groups/Decidantoj]] sometimes revealing their decisions for key citizens’ societal roles.
- [[Maskovo\|The Maskovo]]: This pivotal ceremony is overseen by the Decidantoj. During the Maskovo, citizens transition from their childhood masks to the masks that symbolize their societal roles. The ceremony is deeply spiritual, symbolizing the transformation of identity and submission to the divine order of Vual.
- Twilight Rites: At dusk, The Masked Ones perform Twilight Rites to honor Vual’s association with the shifting balance of light and darkness. These rites include veiled lanterns and masked dances, where participants seek hidden knowledge and guidance from Vual.

## The Role of The Masked Ones in Society
The Masked Ones have a profound influence on society in [[Faldara/Groups/Vualio]]. Through the Decidantoj, they are responsible for assigning societal roles during the Maskovo. Their decisions shape the lives of citizens, determining not only individual identities but the caste structure itself. The [[Faldara/Groups/Nobility]] frequently consults the Masked Ones for spiritual advice, and even the royal family defers to the Decidantoj on religious matters.

### The Faithful Followers
Followers of Vual come from all ranks of society. The faithful look to The Masked Ones for spiritual guidance, blessings, and wisdom. Nobles often seek the favor of the Decidantoj, while common citizens hope that their assigned roles will align with their personal desires. Many dedicate their lives to Vual, becoming devotees, serving the temples, and helping with the many rituals and festivals of the faith.

### Symbol of Vual
The symbol of The Masked Ones is a mask divided into two halves—one light, one dark—representing the dual nature of Vual’s teachings. It is seen throughout the kingdom, from temple banners to the personal garments of the clergy, reminding everyone of Vual’s presence in both the seen and unseen aspects of life.
