### **The Crematorium**

> [!tip]+ **Description**
> 
> #### Physical Description
> 
> - **General Overview**: A functional, if grim, facility for disposing of the city's demon problem. Features large furnaces, some tools of the trade, and the infamous "Rift Pile," a heap of flesh, eyes, and mouths waiting for incineration. The decor is best described as "abattoir chic."
>     
> - **Sensory Details**: The overpowering stench of decay and ash. The squishing sound of walking too close to the corpse pile. The low hum of the furnaces, soon to be drowned out by screaming.
>     
> - **Points of Interest**:
>     
>     - **The Rift Pile**: A mountain of demon parts. Later, it becomes an animate, hostile mountain of demon parts.
>         
>     - **The Furnaces**: Large, hot, and an obvious but apparently unused solution to their problems.
>         
>     - **The Office**: A small, safe room where the world's most unflappable employee ignores the life-or-death struggle happening twenty feet away.

---

> [!locations]+ **Encounters & Events**
> 
> #### Dumpster Diving for Demons
> 
> - **Summary of Event**: The party begins their search for the specific red demon in the Rift Pile. After a long and painful negotiation over who had to touch the gross stuff, they dive in. Happy gets covered in illusory entrails and takes real damage from a stray tooth for his trouble.
>     
> - **Characters Involved**: The party, one very patient Crematorium Worker.
>     
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: They get gross. They take minor damage. They eventually find their target.
>     
> 
> #### An Autism for Jeff
> 
> - **Summary of Event**: Having located the upper half of the red demon, the party performs a gruesome field autopsy (which they repeatedly call an "autism"). They cut open the chest cavity and discover a jagged, pitch-black crystal where the heart should be, tethered by black, vein-like tendrils. Ignoring every horror movie trope, they cut it out and bag it.
>     
> - **Characters Involved**: The party.
>     
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: They acquire the "Demon Heart," a key quest item. They have also, almost certainly, made things much, much worse.
>     
> 
> #### Jeff's Revenge
> 
> - **Summary of Event**: Just as they prepare to leave, the heartless demon torso and the entire Rift Pile animate into a single, massive flesh monster ("Jeff"). It immediately attacks, locks the only exit, and proceeds to systematically dismantle the party. What follows is a nearly two-hour combat slog of questionable tactics, characters getting grappled and engulfed, and a slow, painful march toward a TPK.
>     
> - **Characters Involved**: The party, Jeff the Corpse Amalgam, Astra (briefly).
>     
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: A total party kill is narrowly averted. The party is battered, traumatized, and no closer to getting out of the locked room. They have, however, defeated Jeff by ripping his heart out again.
>     

---

> [!combat]+ **History & Lore**
> 
> - **Known History**: It's where demon bodies go to get burned. The process is apparently slow, leading to large, animate-able piles.
>     
> - **Rumors & Legends**: None. But there will be after this.
>     
> - **Current Events**:
>     
>     - **Hostile Takeover**: The crematorium is currently under the control of a very angry pile of flesh that used to be its inventory.

---

> [!lore]+ **Secrets & Discoveries**
> 
> _Secrets held within the location or important discoveries made there by the players._
> 
> - **Unrevealed Secrets**:
>     
> - **The Crystal's Origin**: What the black crystal heart is, where it comes from, and its exact connection to the demons is unknown.
>     
> - **The Worker's Deal**: Why the crematorium employee is so completely unfazed by anything.
>     
> - **Player Discoveries**:
>     
> - **The Crystal Heart**: The party found the source of the infection, a black crystal inside the demon's chest.
>     
> - **Tendrils of Evil**: The crystal was connected by tendrils that looked just like the veins on Fineks's arm.
>     
> - **Don't Poke the Corpse Pile**: A valuable, if painful, lesson was learned about desecrating demon remains. Removing the heart seems to be the trigger for animation.
>     
> - **Happy's Amnesia**: Happy gets a flashback while being engulfed by Jeff, revealing he has amnesia.

> [!warning]+ **Meta**
> 
> #### DM Notes
> 
> - **Future Plot Hooks**: The party still has the crystal heart. What does it do? Who wants it? Also, they probably need to explain the property damage.
>     
> - **Importance to Campaign**: This was the climax of the session's quest. The discovery of the heart is a major step forward in the plot.
>     
> - **Reception**: The players seemed to love the body horror and the ridiculousness of the situation. The combat was long and grueling, but the constant threat and absurd circumstances (the nonchalant worker, the "autism") kept it engaging. The near-TPK added real stakes.
>     
> - **Misc.**: The party's inability to coordinate is their greatest weakness. "Jeff" is a fantastic name for a flesh amalgam. The DM should be proud of this nightmare fuel.
