# **The Dilapidated Temple**

> [!recite]- Introduction
> Glass crunches underfoot amid overgrown weeds. Collapsed walls have been replaced with rows of wooden shelves that hold earthenware pots. Numerous alcoves are being used as planters for stubborn flora. Just outside the far end of the enclosure, a smashed mosaic of stained glass sits in a splintered window frame next to a withered, skeletal tree.

> [!tip]+ **Description**
> 
> #### Physical Description
> 
> - **General Overview**: It's a run-down, swirl-shaped building with collapsed walls that someone's tried to patch up with shelving. Despite the decay, it's weirdly full of life thanks to a bunch of surprisingly well-maintained plants. The whole place is basically a hermit's dream greenhouse. - **Sensory Details**: The ground crunches with broken glass. The air smells of damp earth and greenery. It’s the kind of place you’d expect to find either a wise old sage or a guy who makes questionable life choices. Turns out, it's both.
> 
> - **Points of Interest**: - **Smashed Mosaic**: There’s a busted-up mosaic outside that the party’s greatest minds couldn't identify. A real loss for art history. - **Donneran's Garden**: The main feature is a collection of thriving plants, lovingly cared for by the resident firbolg. - **The Hotbox Room**: The single, swirly room serves as Donneran's living quarters and, apparently, smoking lounge.

---

> [!locations]+ **Encounters & Events**
> 
> #### A Perfectly Legal Botanical Consultation
> 
> - **Summary of Event**: The party, in their infinite wisdom, followed a bird's advice and stumbled into this temple. Inside, they met Donneran, a firbolg who has turned this former holy site into his personal apartment and nursery. After learning the temple is now dedicated to the math-god Favaro, they were invited to "chill" and smoke a plant called "Juwanna," which conveniently helped them remember crucial details about their quest they'd completely forgotten.
> 
> - **Characters Involved**: Paul Seragon, Alvin Draper, 27, Happy, [[Faldara/People/Donneran]] (NPC).
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: The party successfully got high, which somehow sharpened their memories. They learned this place used to be a temple to Farinia the Spinner, a key detail for their main quest. They also left with a "souvenir" of Juwanna, because of course they did.

---

> [!combat]+ **History & Lore**
> 
> - **Known History**: Once upon a time, this was a temple dedicated to [[Faldara/People/Farinia]], the deity of time and fate also known as the Spinner. Then a firbolg moved in and decided it needed more plants and less of whatever was going on before. Progress.
> 
> - **Rumors & Legends**: None to speak of. It's too dilapidated to be worth a good rumor. - **Current Events**: A firbolg is squatting here, growing smokable herbs, and generally avoiding the city's daily demon apocalypse.
