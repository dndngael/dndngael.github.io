## Overview 

Welcome to Faldara, a land of ancient magic, shifting power, and deep-rooted traditions. From its sprawling kingdoms to its mysterious wilderness, Faldara is a world shaped by both the gods and the people who walk its lands. The sky often glows with soft twilight hues, and the boundaries between the physical world and the spiritual realm are thin, allowing magic to flow through the air like a constant current.

## Notes
The gods of Faldara, each tied to specific domains, regions, or organizations, are active forces in the world, though their influence can be felt in subtle and mysterious ways. Some believe the gods meddle in the affairs of mortals, while others seek to unravel the hidden truths of their divine actions. It is a world where identities are shaped by forces beyond mortal understanding, where masks, both physical and metaphorical, play a central role in defining who people are—or who they are forced to be.

Beyond the cities lie untamed forests, rugged mountains, and ancient ruins filled with forgotten magic, waiting to be discovered. Many in Faldara live in fear of the unknown, while others are driven by curiosity and the desire to reshape the world in their image. Some may seek to change Faldara from the inside out, starting with the [[Faldara/Groups/Vualio\|Kingdom of Vualio]].
