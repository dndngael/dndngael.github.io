## Overview 

To the north of [[Faldara/Places/Vualio City\|Vualio]], beyond the city gates, lies the Mistwind Forest. Dense and mystical, it is said that the mists here never fully lift, shrouding the forest in an eternal twilight. Locals speak of strange creatures and ancient shrines to forgotten gods hidden deep within the trees.
