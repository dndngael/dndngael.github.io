# **The Hero's Tapestry**
A man looking a lot like [[Faldara/People/Lurvius]] is the central figure on an old tapestry within [[Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil]]. An inscription reads: "Velmian. Hero apotheosized. Lost yet unforgotten." The figure has purple eyes and is depicted leading mortals and deities.
