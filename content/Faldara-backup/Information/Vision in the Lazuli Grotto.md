# **Vision in the Lazuli Grotto**

After [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Paul Seragon\|Paul]] touched a red and black object glowing with golden light on a pedestal in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]], [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/27]], [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Alvin Draper\|Alvin]], and [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Paul Seragon\|Paul]] saw a vision of a spectral figure made up of dark, swirling red and black vapor in the form of a humanoid man rising from the object. He was dressed in a black cloak, hiding any discernable features other than a red mask with magnificent, glittering golden markings. Behind the mask, they saw deep purple eyes seeming to call out with sadness. The figure pleaded, his voice distorted and distant, "I am imprisoned. Please help me."

Suddenly, the ground vanished beneath their feet, and they felt like they were falling, tumbling through a vortex of golden light. They fell deeper, deeper, deeper, then suddenly stopped. The golden light subsided, and they found themselves suspended in a pitch-black expanse. They felt weightless with pressure all around as if were underwater. A vermilion light appeared in the distance, illuminating the melancholy figure, who was shackled to the ground by disgusting strands of a fleshy, crimson substance.

He raised his gaze to the heavens and, sobbing, choked out, "Dream Weaver, I beg of you. Guide those with the power to save me to the site where first I prayed to you." He looked around, and his eyes settled on the group. "Oh, gods, you, please! I am the Apotheon. I am lost in darkness. Long ago, I prayed to The Spinner in the heart of a temple of evil. I beg you, take my mask and..."

His voice trailed off as their consciousness left, as if the pressure were crushing the life out of them. The Apotheon's voice was the last thing heard before passing out. "Save me. Please."
