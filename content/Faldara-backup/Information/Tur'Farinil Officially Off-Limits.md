# **Tur'Farinil Officially Off-Limits**
- The guards at the temple have new orders from [[Faldara/People/Seren Kaŭro\|the Grand Duke]] to forbid entry to anyone. They were willing to be bribed for a steep price (400 gold per guard).
- The Grand Duke has officially barred entry to [[Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil]], indicating a higher level of official interest or concern regarding the location. This escalates the party's actions from mere trespassing to actively defying a ducal decree.
