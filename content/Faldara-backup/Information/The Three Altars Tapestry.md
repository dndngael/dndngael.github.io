# **The Three Altars Tapestry**
A second tapestry within [[Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil]] showed a figure praying at three different altars, sectioned into three panels. [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Paul Seragon\|Paul]] and [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/27]] recognized the _top_ altar as the one from the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]]. This could imply the other two altars on the tapestry are locations for remaining mask pieces.
