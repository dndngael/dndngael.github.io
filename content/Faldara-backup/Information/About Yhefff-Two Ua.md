# **About Yhefff-Two Ua**
A human wizard from the college of magic in Vualio (V-U-M). He has a twin brother, [[Yhefff-Three Ua]], who is "really rich." He was deeply racist towards elves, calling them "knife-ears." His catchphrase was "spit on that thing." His soul song, had anyone bothered to resonate with him, was "Iris" by the Goo Goo Dolls.
