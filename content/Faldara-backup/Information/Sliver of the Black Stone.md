# **Sliver of the Black Stone**
The stone [[Temu]] and [[Shein]] were fighting over seemed to be a sliver of the same material that composed the heart of the monster [[Faldara/People/Jeff]]. It is said to cause psychosis. [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Yhefff-Two Ua]] purchased it, and after his death, it was consumed by his goo-monster remains and vanished.
