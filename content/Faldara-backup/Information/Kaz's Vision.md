# **Kaz's Vision**

After celebrating becoming Champions of the Lowgates, [[Faldara/People/Umbral Kaz]] relays a vision he had to you: "A vision came to me last night. A red and golden mask rose to the surface of a pool of blood, and countless hands lunged upward out of the blood to grasp it. In turn, all the hands fell back into the pool until only one remained. The one whose hand claimed the mask was a man with purple eyes and a somber expression. He turned to me and said, 'They must find me. The ones from the grotto.'"
