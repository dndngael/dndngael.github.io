# **Lheani's Very Recent History**
[[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Lheani]] is a tortle barbarian from "very far away." He was hired as a guard by a group of religious explorers. They found a key, but when he picked it up, a magic wall trapped him. His employers were apparently killed and carried off by kobolds, who then reset the chandelier trap. He was trapped for five days.
