# **Entering Tur'Farinil**
[[6 Transfer/NPCs/Faylen Nemyris\|Faylen]] believes only those who have "experienced the Twinlight" are worthy of entering the temple to seek the amplifier. She sees [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Paul Seragon\|Paul]] and [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/27]] as "lost causes" but permissible when with [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Alvin Draper\|Alvin]] and [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Happy]], yet was unwilling to let Dawn's Bane pass at all or Yhefff-Two Ua pass until worn down by social pressure. She claims [[Faldara/Places/Farin]] gave her the "moon's tear" and she is the one meant to amplify the signal from Farin.
