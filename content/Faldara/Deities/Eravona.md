# **Eravona** <span style="font-size: medium">"air-uh-VOH-nuh"</span>

## Overview

Eravona embodies the unpredictable nature of the ocean, both its beauty and its terror. Fishermen, sailors, and coastal dwellers respect and fear her, as her moods can shift from serene to vengeful in an instant.

> [!dm]
> ## Notes
> 
> Followers: Called "Wavewalkers", the followers of Eravona tend to be sailors, fisherman, and storm sorcerers.
> 
> ### Divine Domains
> Tempest, Nature, Ocean, Sea, Storm
> 
> ### Divine Symbols and Sigils
> - A trident crossing over a bolt of lightning
> - Animals: Shark, albatross
> - Colors: Deep blue, silver, stormy gray
> 
> ### Holidays
> The Taming of the Waves: an annual rite where offerings are cast into the sea to ensure Eravona’s favor for safe voyages.
> 
> The Call of Storms: a ritual performed during droughts, calling for rain, though risky as it may bring torrential storms.
