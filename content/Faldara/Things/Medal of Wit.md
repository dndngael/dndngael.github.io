#  **Medal of Wit**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> Sculpted and painted to look like the head of a fox

**In-Game Effects:**

You can press this medal to your temple as an action. Doing so gives you advantage on Intelligence checks and Intelligence saving throws for 1 hour. Once this property has been used, it can't be used again, and the medal becomes nonmagical.

## Notes

Earned by winning the "Riddles and Rhymes" pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] game in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]].
