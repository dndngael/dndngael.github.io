#  **Umbracite**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> Umbracite is a pitch-black, jagged, crystal-like stone that absorbs all light, showing no reflection. It feels unnaturally cold to the touch. When psionically sensed, it emits a chaotic and overwhelming cacophony of noise.

**In-Game Effects**:

- **Parasitic Nature**: [[Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius\|Nephil]] described this material as something he thought no longer existed, having read about it in ancient books from [[The Nivela Institute\|the Institute]]. He stated, "It's a material called Umbracite... It's said to be very powerful and feed off of the songs of one's soul." He believes a piece of it pierced [[Faldara/People/Fineks]], acting as a "parasite to her very soul."
- **Negative Effects**: Direct contact and proximity to Umbracite appears to have detrimental effects on living beings, as seen with Fineks.

## Notes:

- This material was discovered inside [[Faldara/People/Jeff\|a creature]] the party defeated, forming [[Faldara/Information/Jeff's Heart\|its heart]].
- The party believes this material is the cause of their friend Fineks's illness.

:::h

## DM Notes:

- Umbracite is a material from eons ago, thought to no longer exist.
    
- Nephel has theorized that a "Silent Stone," which absorbs psychic energy, could potentially be crafted into an item to negate or harmonize the effects of the Umbracite on a person's soul.
    
- The party has considered crafting weapons such as a sword or arrowheads from the Umbracite, though Nephel advised against it due to its negative effects.
    
- The ultimate cure for the negative effects of Umbracite is still unknown and may require finding lost knowledge within the temple of Turfarno, specifically Farinia's Codex.
