#  **Really Cool Cloak**

> [!recite]- Description
> This cloak appears to be made of a simple wool, but the inside is a swirling, iridescent material that shimmers with all the colors of the rainbow. The cloak is surprisingly light and makes a soft, rustling sound, like leaves in the wind, when it moves.

**In-Game Effects:**

- While wearing this cloak, you can use a bonus action to cause it to billow dramatically.
- When you attune to this cloak, it awakens to sentience and will try to protect you at all costs, which grants you a +1 bonus to your AC and saving throws. The cloak can float near you and move where you tell it to move within a 30ft radius.
