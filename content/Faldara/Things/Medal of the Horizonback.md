#  **Medal of the Horizonback**

## Details

> [!recite]- Description
> No two medals awarded from this contest look the same. Each one appears to be made from some different part of a tortoise: a piece of shell, a claw, or a tooth. Rest assured that these medals were made from tortoises that died naturally.

**In-Game Effects:**

When you would be hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to increase your AC by 5 until the start of your next turn, including against the triggering attack. You must be wearing the medal and able to see the creature that made the triggering attack to use this property. Once this property has been used, it can't be used again, and the medal becomes nonmagical.

## Notes

Earned by winning the "Herding the Horizonbacks" pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] game in the [[Faldara/Places/Lowgates]].
