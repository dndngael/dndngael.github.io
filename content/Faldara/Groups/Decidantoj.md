## Overview

The Decidantoj, or The Deciders, wield immense influence. These high ranking figures play a crucial role in determining the future of Vualio's citizens by assigning their adult masks during the [[Maskovo]] (Masking Ceremony), shaping the very fabric of society. To be chosen as a Decidanto is one of the highest honors, as they are seen as intermediaries between the people, the gods, and the complex societal structure of the [[Faldara/Groups/Vualio\|Kingdom]].

## Who Are the Decidantoj?
The Decidantoj are an elite group of individuals tasked with deciding the societal roles of Vualio’s citizens. Each Decidanto holds authority not only because of their deep knowledge of the kingdom's traditions but also because of their spiritual and philosophical understanding of the gods, especially [[Faldara/People/Vual]]. They are responsible for interpreting the will of the gods and the needs of society, ensuring that every citizen finds their rightful place within Vualio.

Decidantoj are diverse in background, coming from various social roles such as priests, scholars, artisans, and even former soldiers. However, they all share a common trait: an unshakable commitment to the kingdom’s ideals of order, identity, and purpose. These individuals are often regarded with a mix of respect, reverence, and fear, as their decisions can alter the course of a person's life.

:::h
## How Does One Become a Decidanto?
Becoming a Decidanto is not something one can pursue directly. The path to becoming one is long, filled with trials of wisdom, experience, and devotion to the kingdom. To be considered, individuals must exhibit a profound understanding of both the cultural and religious underpinnings of Vualio.

Key Steps in Becoming a Decidanto: 
- Early Training: Young individuals identified as potential candidates are often selected from among those who show an exceptional ability to understand the subtleties of social dynamics, history, and theology. These individuals undergo training in Vualian philosophy, the tenets of the gods, and the history of the kingdom. They are also taught to interpret omens and signs.
- Mentorship: Aspiring Decidantoj are paired with an established member of the group to learn their ways. This mentorship lasts for several years, during which the candidate is tested on their ability to discern truth, make just decisions, and predict the potential impact of societal changes.
- The Masking of a Decidanto: The final trial to become a Decidanto involves a symbolic death of one's former identity and a rebirth into their new role. In a private ceremony, the aspiring Decidanto receives their final mask—an intricate design that reflects their wisdom and insight. From that moment on, their old identity is shed, and they become part of a near-immortal tradition that transcends individual lives.
- Vows of Service and Secrecy: Upon their appointment, Decidantoj take lifelong vows of service to Vualio and the gods, particularly Vual. They also vow secrecy, never revealing the full extent of their deliberations or their identities to the common people. This secrecy gives them a mythical aura, as their masks become their sole identities in the public eye.
