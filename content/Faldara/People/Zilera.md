Zilera governs both tender love and fiery passion. Whether it’s the bond between friends, the intensity of romance, or the fire of artistic creation, she nurtures the heart’s deepest desires.

## Notes
Followers: Called "Heartkeepers", the followers of Zilera tend to be couples, friends, poets, artists, and performers.
### Divine Domains
Life, Light, Charm, Family, Love

### Divine Symbols and Sigils
- A rose entwined around a burning heart
- Animals: Doves, lions
- Colors: Red, pink, purple, gold, silver

### Holidays
The Festival of Hearts: a celebration of love, where couples and friends alike give offerings to Zilera for her blessing on their relationships.

The Flame of Desire: a sacred fire ceremony where followers cast their deepest wishes into the flames, hoping for Zilera’s favor in fulfilling them.
