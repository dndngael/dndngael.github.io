# **Fineks** <span style="font-size: medium">"FEE-niks"</span>

## Overview

Fineks is a tiefling druid and member of the party [[Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane]]. She has fiery, short orange hair, orange eyes, and she is very short for a tiefling. Her companion Torĉ (pronounced: Torch) looks like a mixture between a cat and an owl.

## Notes
- You met Fineks during the pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] games, and competed against her and Dawn's Bane for the final competition in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]].

:::h
## DM Overview

Fineks Ashensong is a Tiefling Wildfire Druid and a member of the adventuring party, Dawn's Bane. She is known for her brash personality, love of fire (particularly the destructive kind), and her constant clashes with 27, a Circle of Stars Druid. She struggles with hidden insecurities and a suppressed compassion, masked by a façade of recklessness and chaotic behavior. She is unknowingly being manipulated by the leader of Dawn's Bane, Lurvius.

**Personality & Traits:**

- **Core Traits:** Bold, brash, mischievous, reckless, sarcastic, secretly insecure.
- **Public Demeanor:** Flaunts her tiefling heritage, enjoys shocking others, delights in destruction.
- **Speaking Style:** Sarcastic, quick-witted, often uses fire-related metaphors.
- **Hidden Traits:** Suppressed compassion, fear of being consumed by her own power, longing for acceptance.
- **Flaws:** Reckless, impulsive, easily manipulated (due to her desire for acceptance), self-destructive tendencies.

**Motivations:**

- **Primary:** To prove herself as a powerful wildfire druid and to find a place where she truly belongs.
- **Secondary:** She seeks the spontaneity and freedom of chaos, since she has felt so controlled by her circle.

**Backstory (Brief):**

- Born a tiefling of Mephistopheles lineage, she faced prejudice and felt like an outsider.
- Raised in a Circle of Wildfire Druids who believed in _controlled_ burns for ecological renewal.
- She rebelled against the circle's strictures, embracing the _uncontrolled_ and destructive aspects of fire.
- She joined the adventuring party Dawn's Bane, seeking power, recognition, and a place to belong.
- She is unaware that Dawn's Bane's leader, Lurvius, is manipulating her.
- When she was younger, she saved a child from a burning building.

**Relationships:**

- **Lurvius (Leader of Dawn's Bane):** Sees him as a powerful and understanding leader (though he's manipulating her). She seeks his approval.
- **27 (Rival):** Constantly clashes with him due to their opposing philosophies (chaos vs. order). She finds him boring and predictable.
- **Ĵura (Dawn's Bane Member):** Finds him boring and overly strict, but respects his strength.
- **Astra (Dawn's Bane Member):** Fascinated by his bladesinging, but finds his artistic pretensions annoying.
- **Torĉ (Wildfire Spirit):** A fiercely loyal and protective companion who communicates through actions and a strong empathic bond. He often expresses concern for Fineks's recklessness, though she rarely heeds his silent warnings.

**Secrets:**

- **Hidden Compassion:** She secretly cares about the well-being of others, but hides it.
- **Fear of Being Consumed:** Terrified of losing control of her powers or being consumed by fire (literally or metaphorically).
- **Past Trauma:** Witnessed a devastating fire that deeply affected her.

**Dawn's Bane Involvement:**

- Member of the adventuring party, Dawn's Bane.
- Joined for the opportunity to use her fire magic, gain recognition, and find a sense of belonging.
- Unknowingly serves Lurvius, a fragmented fey being.

**Plot Hooks & Potential Arcs:**

- The PCs could try to exploit her insecurities and turn her against Lurvius.
- Her rivalry with 27 could lead to a major confrontation, forcing both characters to grow.
- She could be forced to confront her hidden compassion and choose a more balanced path.
- Torĉ could play a crucial role in saving her from danger or helping her realize the truth about Lurvius.
- She could be captured by an enemy.

**Key Quote:**

"Burn it all down! ...Just kidding. *Mostly*." (Said with a mischievous grin.)
