## Overview

Kreiĉiza embodies the forces of creation and transformation, not only in the physical sense but also in the realms of magic, artistry, and personal identity. They are the god of artisans who craft not just objects but themselves, encouraging mortals to see their identity as a canvas to be shaped, reshaped, and reimagined. Kreiĉiza is often depicted as a figure in the process of transforming, their appearance shifting mid-motion, hands crafting an unseen masterpiece from magic and raw material. They revel in the unknown, in pushing boundaries, and in the idea that everything—including the self—can be remade.
  
Kreiĉiza's teachings embrace the belief that transformation is an act of creation and that through the manipulation of magic and craft, one can forge a new reality. Whether through crafting items imbued with power, reshaping their own bodies, or wielding magic to change the world around them, their followers are always in flux, seeking to express their inner selves and ideas through their artistry.

## Notes
- Ages ago, there were countless followers of Kreiĉiza, but they are hard to find in modern times. They feel forgotten, as they haven't experienced Kreiĉiza's presence in a long time.


Followers: Called the "Weavers", Kreiĉiza's followers tend to be artisans, wizards, outcasts, and revolutionaries. 
### Divine Domains
Change, Arcana, Magic, Creation, Craft

### Divine Symbols and Sigils
- Impossible triangle
- Animals: Butterflies, octopuses, spiders, chameleons
- Colors: Iridescent blues, purples, and silvers

### Holidays
The Shifting Magic: A magical rite in which followers are encouraged to challenge their magical limitations, experimenting with new forms of spellcasting, transmutation, or enchanting. It's a time for pushing boundaries, testing limits, and even taking risks with their arcane abilities.

The Artisan's Trial: A ritual for those dedicated to craft and creation. Devotees gather materials and undergo a trial of creation where they must craft an object imbued with a piece of themselves. This trial can take days, and the outcome is often a reflection of the creator’s spirit, success, or struggle.
