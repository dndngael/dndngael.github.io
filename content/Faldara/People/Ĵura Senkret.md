## Overview

Lord Ĵura Senkret II is a noble of Vualio City, a devout follower of Vual and the Masked Ones, and a member of the party [[Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane]]. Ĵura is a taller, stout man with blue eyes, blonde shaggy but well-maintained hair.

## Notes
- You met Ĵura during the pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] games, and competed against him and Dawn's Bane for the final competition in the [[Faldara/Places/Lazuli Grotto]].

:::h

## DM Overview

Lord Ĵura Senkret II is a noble of Vualio City, a devout follower of Vual and the Masked Ones, and a member of the adventuring party, Dawn's Bane. He is characterized by his unwavering loyalty, jovial demeanor, tactical prowess, and a deep-seated, yet often unrecognized, internal conflict. He serves as a recurring rival to the player characters, particularly Paul Seragon, who opposes the Vualian system. Ĵura is unknowingly being manipulated by the leader of his adventuring party, the fragmented Lurvius.

## Personality & Traits

- **Core Traits:** Jovial, outwardly confident, deeply religious, tactically astute, righteous.
- **Public Demeanor:** Always smiling, prone to hearty laughter, uses formal language and religious pronouncements.
- **Speaking Style:** Booming voice, quotes Vual's scriptures (sometimes out of context), adjusts his mask frequently, refers to lower ranks as "Citizen".
- **Hidden Traits:** Suppressed doubts, prone to righteous anger when challenged, fiercely loyal to his family (despite past actions).
- **Flaws:** Blind loyalty to Vual and the Masked Ones, ambition, fear of change, prone to righteous anger, suppresses doubts.

## Motivations

- **Primary (Conscious):** Preservation of tradition and order in Vualio; believes the system is divinely ordained. Serving Vual through adventuring.
- **Secondary (Subconscious):** Personal glory and recognition; to live up to his father's legacy (his father was a famed adventurer and Veilguard member).
- **Root (Hidden):** Fear of change and the unknown, stemming from a past traumatic event (possibly related to social unrest).

## Backstory (Brief)

- Born into the lesser noble House Senkret in Vualio City.
- Raised with a strong belief in Vual and the Masked Ones.
- Trained as a warrior and joined the Veilguard, serving with distinction.
- His older brother, Kaelen, was accused of heresy, and Ĵura publicly denounced him to prove his loyalty.
- Sought adventure and greater glory, eventually joining the adventuring party, Dawn's Bane.
- He is completely unaware that Dawn's Bane's leader is the ancient fey, Lurvius, who was cursed by Vual.

## Relationships

- **Lurvius (Leader of Dawn's Bane):** Sees him as a charismatic and powerful leader, possibly divinely chosen (completely deceived).
- **Earl Paul Seragon (Rival):** Former friend, now ideological opponent. Ĵura pities him and believes he is misguided.
- **Lady Elara Senkret (Mother):** A devout and influential noblewoman who pressures him to succeed.
- **Kaelen (Brother):** A strained relationship due to Kaelen's accusation of heresy and Ĵura's actions. Kaelen is _not_ a member of Dawn's Bane, but their paths may still cross.
- **Astra (Dawn's Bane Member):** Views Astra as an artist and a loyal member.
- **Fineks (Dawn's Bane Member):** Enjoys Fineks company.
- **High Priest Vorlag (Veilguard Leader):** Respects Vorlag as a religious authority.

## Secrets

- **Family Shame:** His brother's accusation of heresy and Ĵura's subsequent actions. He secretly regrets denouncing his brother.
- **Forbidden Knowledge:** Possesses a fragment of an ancient prophecy about an "Apotheon" cursed by a masked god, which he dismisses as heresy. He also experiences recurring visions/dreams of Lurvius as the Apotheon, which he suppresses.

## Dawn's Bane Involvement

- Member of the adventuring party, Dawn's Bane.
- Joined for adventure, glory, and to serve Vual in a more direct way.
- Unknowingly serves Lurvius, a fragmented fey being who is the antithesis of Vual.
- **Symbol:** A silver mask with gold embellishments and three blue streaks, in a triangle, under the mask.

## Knowledge and Skills

- Expert battle tactician.
- Knows the history of the city of Vualio.
- Expert in the scripture of Vual.

## Plot Hooks & Potential Arcs

- The PCs could attempt to expose Ĵura's secret doubts and turn him against Lurvius.
- Ĵura could be forced to confront the truth about Lurvius, leading to a crisis of faith.
- His relationship with his brother could be explored, potentially leading to reconciliation or further conflict.
- He could become a temporary ally if the PCs can convince him of a greater threat to Vualio.
- His forbidden knowledge could become a key piece of information in the larger campaign plot.
- He could be used to show the dark side of the city.
- He could be captured and interrogated by the PCs.
