# **Agathe Silverspoon** <span style="font-size: medium">"Æ-guh-thuh"</span>

## Overview

Agathe moves from counter to counter of the [[Faldara/Places/Silent Falls]] tavern-inn, deftly decorating a variety of baked goods and preparing batters to put into the oven behind her.

## Interactions

- You met Agathe while competing to earn medals for the pre-[[Faldara/Information/La Festo de Maskoj\|festival]] games. She ran a dragoncake eating contest, claiming to bake the best cakes in the [[Faldara/Groups/Vualio\|kingdom]]. [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Paul Seragon\|Paul]] however ate one too many cakes and projectile vomited on one of his fellow contestants and the table. Agathe has been avoiding the group since.
