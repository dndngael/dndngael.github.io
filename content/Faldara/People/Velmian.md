> [!profile]+ **Profile**
> 
> #### Physical Description
> 
> - **Appearance**: Picture a small, faintly glowing spirit, disappointingly child-shaped with elf ears and startlingly purple eyes. Notably, and perhaps scandalously in this masked society, he wears no mask.
>     
> - **Notable Features**: Glows slightly, phases through walls like they're suggestions, and possesses those aforementioned purple eyes.
>     
> 
> #### Personality
> 
> - **Personality Overview**: A shy, ancient being trapped in a child's form, with a memory as reliable as a chocolate teapot when it comes to ancient history. Generally harmless, unless you count petty theft of glowing objects or accidental plate-slaughter.
>     
> - **Primary Traits**:
>     - **Eternally Evasive**: When questioned about anything remotely complex, ancient, or requiring a concrete answer, his go-to is a resounding "I don't know." This was demonstrated when asked about his age, why he took the glass, and the specifics of the temple's decline.
>         
>     - **Painfully Shy**: Confronted by new faces (Paul and Alvin), he promptly used Happy as a human (or half-elf) shield. Also admitted he doesn't usually like being seen.
>         
>     - **Antique Child**: Despite his childlike form, he casually mentions Farinia being around "a million billion years ago" and expresses a childlike fascination with "fun" things like glowing knick-knacks.
>         
> - **Motivations**: Mostly just... exists. He was "having fun" when he relocated the glowing glass to Lurvius because he "felt like it should go there." Sometimes he likes to "try to say hi to the people that are new." Deep stuff.
>

> [!locations]+ Events & Interactions
> 
> Oh, the places he's haunted, the things he's vaguely remembered...
> 
> #### The Great Glass Relocation Service (and Subsequent Plate-icide)
> 
> - **Summary of Interaction**: After Happy and Alvin were serenaded by mysterious crying, Happy left a glowing glass as a ghost-bait. Surprise! Lurvius found it next to his bed. Velmian, the spectral crybaby, fessed up to moving it because it "was fun" and he "felt like it should go there." He also accidentally (we assume) broke a glowing plate later.
>     
> - **Location**: The Old Inn.
> - **Characters Involved**: Happy, Alvin, Paul, 27, Lurvius, Velmian.
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: The party learned the crying ghost was an ancient child spirit named Velmian. He can interact with objects. Happy lost a glass and a plate.
> - **Relationship Changes:** Initial contact established. Velmian shows a preference for Happy and an interest in Lurvius.
> 
> #### Temple Timeshare: Riddle Me This
> 
> - **Summary of Interaction**: Velmian, bless his non-corporeal heart, tried to lead the party to a "quiet room" in the temple by phasing through a wall, which was... unhelpful for the distinctly solid party. After they blundered in the front door, Happy's door-touching antics triggered a riddle from a disembodied voice. Velmian helpfully confirmed the voice was Farinia's, then offered no further useful commentary on the riddle itself.
>     
> - **Location**: Main Temple Entrance & Entryway.
> - **Characters Involved**: Happy, Alvin, Paul, 27, Lurvius, Velmian.
> - **Outcome, Decisions & Consequences**: Party is now stumped by a divine riddle. They also learned there are bat creatures behind all three doors. Good times.
> - **Relationship Changes:** Velmian continues to be a source of ancient, if fragmented, information.

> [!info]+ **Meta**
> 
> #### Roleplaying Notes
> 
> - **Voice & Speech Patterns**: Childlike, simple, often punctuated by "I don't know." Heard by Happy as a distinct "Thank you" and "Put him down!" So, capable of clear, short statements.
>     
> - **Body Language**: A small, glowing child-spirit. Prone to hiding behind trusted figures when startled. Pokes his head through walls curiously.
>     
> - **Common Phrases/Catchphrases**:
>     - **Common Phrase 1 + Context**: "I don't know." - His default answer to hard questions, questions about the distant past, or really, anything requiring too much thought.
>     - **Common Phrase 2 + Context**: Describing ancient times with "a million billion years ago" or similar hyperbole.
>         
> - **Emotional Tendencies**: Initially presents as sad (the crying), but also shows curiosity and a capacity for finding things "fun." Can be very shy with new people.
>     
> - **Reference Character:** Casper the Friendly Ghost, if Casper was incomprehensibly ancient and had a slightly dodgy memory for historical details.
> - **Roleplaying Tips**:
>     - **Consistency**: Keep him childlike in demeanor and speech, but with an undercurrent of immense age and a touch of melancholy. His understanding of time and complex events should be fuzzy.
>     - **Accent/Dialect**: Simple, clear, perhaps slightly hesitant.
>     - **Behavioral Cues**: When confused or pressured, he defaults to "I don't know" or hides. Drawn to shiny or "fun" things.
> 
> #### DM Notes
> 
> - **Unrevealed Secrets**: The _exact_ reason the glass went to Lurvius. The full story of the "soldiers." The true nature of the "shiny room" in the temple. The circumstances of his own demise/spiritual binding. The complete history of Farinia and Vual's involvement with the temple.
> - **Role in Campaign**: Primary source for ancient (if unreliable) temple lore. Potential (if frustrating) guide. Embodiment of the inn's haunting.
> - **Reception**: The players are definitely intrigued. His childlike nature combined with his ancient knowledge (and lack thereof) makes for an interesting NPC. The purple eyes were a player-driven detail that stuck.
> - **Misc.**: His skewed perception of time is a key trait. He's more of a passive observer unless directly engaged or something catches his spectral eye. The "no mask" detail is a big clue about his origins relative to the current culture.
