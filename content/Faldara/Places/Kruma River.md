## Overview 

The Kruma River is the lifeblood of [[Faldara/Places/Vualio City\|Vualio]], flowing from the northern [[Faldara/Places/Mistwind Forest]] and winding through the heart of the city before continuing west. It divides the capital, creating a natural boundary between the noble districts on the west and the bustling trade districts on the east side.
