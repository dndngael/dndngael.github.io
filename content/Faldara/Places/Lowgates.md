## Overview 

This district is at the lowest elevation of the city and extends beyond the city walls. The district is primarily a market and entertainment district, housing many artisans, entertainers, and performers. It serves as a transitional area between the busy city life and the quiet countryside. Beyond the gates, small, close-knit communities have sprung up. Farmers and artisans often live here, enjoying proximity to the city while working the lands outside the walls.
