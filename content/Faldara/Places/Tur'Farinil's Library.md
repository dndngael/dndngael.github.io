# **Tur'Farinil's Library** 

> [!recite]- The party descends the staircase revealed by the pillar puzzle...
> You walk down the newly revealed stairs into the darkness below, the air changing from the dusty scent of the pillar room to the ancient, dry smell of paper and time. As you step into the chamber, your light falls upon impossible rows of shelves lining the walls, stretching up into the gloom. Books, thousands of them, sit untouched, covered in a fine layer of dust that suggests they haven't been disturbed for millennia. This is it... the Great Library.

> [!tip]+ **Description**
> 
> #### Physical Description
> 
> - **General Overview**: This is a massive, multi-room library hidden deep within the temple. The main chamber features countless bookshelves lining the walls, filled with ancient tomes. The room is quiet, still, and untouched by time, though it also contains hidden sections and secrets.
> 
> - **Sensory Details**: The library smells of ancient paper, dust, and still air. It is incredibly quiet, save for the sounds of the party. Visually, it is overwhelming, with endless rows of books on shelves that disappear into the darkness above. Some book spines are in archaic but recognizable languages, while others are in a completely unknown, ancient tongue. A faint hum or tone can be heard beyond the walls of books.
>    
> - **Points of Interest**:
> 	- **Main Shelves**: Endless rows of books line the walls, containing knowledge from millennia ago.
> 	- **The Wall**: A hum seems to emanate from a specific wall, which the party investigates for hidden passages.
> 	- **Hidden Room 1 (The Dyad War)**: A secret room hidden behind a moving bookshelf, containing even more shelves. This room contains a book Happy can partially read, titled "[[In a Time Before the Dyad War]]".
> 	- **Hidden Room 2 (The Erotica)**: Another hidden room containing what appears to be a large collection of interdimensional pornography.
> 	- **Hidden Room 3 (The Codex)**: A third hidden room, where a glint of light at the bottom of a shelf reveals Farinia's Codex.
