# **Farin** <span style="font-size: medium">"FAH-rin"</span>

## Overview 

The Silver Moon above the skies of [[Faldara/Places/Faldara]]. Much of the calendar is based off of the cycles of Farin.

## Notes

- Named after the [[Faldara/People/Farinia\|Deity of Time, Farinia]].
