# **Velmian-Lurvius Connection**
The party had a major revelation: The figure on the "Hero" tapestry, named Velmian, looks like an older version of the ghost child [[Faldara/People/Velmian]], and _also_ looks identical to [[Faldara/People/Lurvius]]. This heavily implies they are the same person at different ages, or are related.
