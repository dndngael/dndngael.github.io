# **Learning of the Dyad War**
[[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/27]] discovered a tapestry depicting this ancient war. His history check revealed it was a war where gods fought on both sides. More importantly, the name triggered a memory in [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/Player Characters/Happy]], though he cannot recall where he learned about it, hinting at a suppressed or forgotten past.
