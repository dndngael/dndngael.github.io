# **Soul Songs**

[[Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius\|Nephil]] described these as "the natural frequency of someone's soul," with each person's being unique. He notes that [[Faldara/People/Fineks\|Fineks's]] has been "nullified" by the [[Faldara/Things/Silentstone]], making her like a "void, or like zero."
