# **The Apotheon's Voice Sounds Familiar to Dawn's Bane**

While, they didn't receive the [[Faldara/Information/Vision in the Lazuli Grotto\|vision in the Lazuli Grotto]] that [[1 Adventures/Adventures in Vualio/SPP3 4eva/SPP3 4eva]] received, three members of [[Faldara/Groups/Dawn's Bane]], [[Faldara/People/Astra]], [[Faldara/People/Fineks]], and [[Faldara/People/Ĵura Senkret\|Ĵura]], said they heard a familiar voice, but it sounded like it was underwater. They could not tell who the voice belongs to.
