# **Farinia's Codex**

This artifact is not a book but a crystal that "stores information out of pure psychic frequencies," making it readable by [[Faldara/People/Nephil Salŝius\|Nephil]]. It is supposedly located in [[Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil's Library\|the Great Library]] of [[Faldara/Places/Tur'Farinil]]. Nephil has tasked the party with finding it with him in exchange for his [[Faldara/Things/Silentstone]]
