# House Rules

This page collects mechanical house rules that are explicitly recorded in campaign notes. Unless noted otherwise, rules are listed under the campaign where they are documented rather than assumed to apply to every campaign.

## Faldara

### Potions

A potion can be used as either an **action** or a **bonus action**.

- **Action:** Use the potion normally.
- **Bonus action:** Make a Dexterity saving throw.
  - **Success:** The potion is used normally.
  - **Failure:** The potion is consumed with no effect.

> [!note]
> The surviving session note does not record the DC for the Dexterity saving throw.

### Lore Points

- **1 Lore Point = 1 reroll.**
- A Lore Point can be used at any time on any of your own rolls.
- Lore Points reset every session.

> [!note]
> The campaign notes connect Lore Points to Blooket, but the surviving rule does not clearly record how many Lore Points are awarded or the full earning procedure.
